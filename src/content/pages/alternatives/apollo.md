## The shortlist

Prices are the lowest plan each vendor published, checked on its own site in November 2026. Where a vendor doesn't publish a price, we say so.

| Tool | Best for | Open source or self-hosted | Price (checked November 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ZoomInfo](#zoominfo) | Enterprise data and go-to-market platform | No | Not published |
| [Cognism](#cognism) | European data and mobile numbers | No | Not published |
| [Lusha](#lusha) | Simple contact lookups | No | Free plan, 40 credits a month |
| [Hunter](#hunter) | Finding and verifying work emails | No | Free plan; Starter $49/month billed monthly |
| [People Data Labs](#people-data-labs) | A people data API for developers | No | Free up to 100 credits a month; Pro from $98/month |

## ZoomInfo

A go-to-market platform with packages for prospecting, enrichment, account-based marketing and more, plus an API, MCP and CLI priced on usage, and data delivery to warehouses such as Snowflake and BigQuery. Prices are custom; you request them ([pricing](https://www.zoominfo.com/pricing)). A ZoomInfo connector for Outflare is planned, not available.

## Cognism

B2B sales intelligence that presents itself as a European data provider, with an emphasis on mobile numbers and on-demand mobile verification, plus API and MCP access. Pricing is tailored and not published; Standard and Pro tiers include 5 seats ([pricing](https://www.cognism.com/pricing)).

## Lusha

Contact and company lookups with one credit balance across its app, extension, API and MCP. The free plan gives up to 40 credits a month ([pricing](https://www.lusha.com/pricing/)). Good for a few lookups a day rather than large lists.

## Hunter

Finds and verifies work emails. The free plan has 50 credits a month, and Starter is $49/month billed monthly or $34/month billed yearly ([pricing](https://hunter.io/pricing)). Outflare connects to Hunter on your own key for email finding and verification.

## People Data Labs

Person and company data through developer APIs. The free plan has up to 100 credits a month without contact data; Pro starts at $98/month ([pricing](https://www.peopledatalabs.com/pricing)). Outflare can build [lead lists](/docs/guides/lead-lists/) from People Data Labs searches on your own credits.

## Clay

A workspace for enrichment and go-to-market workflows that can find and enrich data from 150+ providers in waterfalls. It lets you bring your own API keys on every plan, which skips Clay's data credits. The free plan includes 100 data credits a month ([pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)). A Clay connector for Outflare is planned, not available.

## Outflare

Outflare has no contact database. It is open-source software (AGPL-3.0) that builds [lead lists](/docs/guides/lead-lists/) from your own Apollo, People Data Labs or MoltSets account, finds emails through a waterfall of the providers you connect (Prospeo, LeadMagic, Findymail, Hunter), with a 180-day cache and a credit cap per run, and scores each lead against your [ideal customer profile](/docs/guides/ideal-customers/). Its free [email finder](/docs/guides/find-emails/) proposes addresses from the patterns in your own contacts.

Then it does what Apollo does after the data: [cold email](/product/cold-email/) from your own mailboxes, a [reply inbox](/product/inbox/), a [CRM](/product/customer-data/) and [cost per meeting](/product/outcomes/). Free to self-host; Outflare Cloud is free during early access. See [Outflare vs Apollo](/compare/outflare-vs-apollo/).

## How to choose

1. **Data or workflow?** If you need contacts, pick a data provider (ZoomInfo, Cognism, Lusha, Hunter, People Data Labs). If you need to turn data into lists and campaigns, pair one with Clay or Outflare.
2. **Region and channel.** For European contacts and mobile numbers, look at Cognism first.
3. **Volume and budget.** Free plans from Hunter, Lusha, People Data Labs and Clay cover testing. Enterprise data from ZoomInfo and Cognism is priced by quote.
4. **Developers and agents.** People Data Labs is API-first; ZoomInfo, Cognism and Lusha offer MCP. Outflare's [MCP server](/product/mcp/) marks tools that spend credits, so an AI client can ask before running them.
5. **Keep Apollo?** Often the answer is to keep Apollo for data and send from Outflare, which flags mailboxes that Apollo can also send from.
