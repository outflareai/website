## The shortlist

Prices are the lowest paid plan each vendor published, checked on its own site in November 2026. Where we couldn't read a price, we leave it out.

| Tool | Best for | Open source or self-hosted | Price (checked November 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Attio](#attio) | A flexible, modern hosted CRM | No | Free up to 3 seats; Plus $44/seat/month billed monthly |
| [Pipedrive](#pipedrive) | Pipeline-first sales CRM | No | Per seat; see its pricing page |
| [Zoho CRM](#zoho-crm) | A free tier in a wider suite | No | Free for 3 users; paid per user |

## Attio

A hosted CRM with real-time contact syncing and automatic enrichment, an API and webhooks. The free plan covers up to 3 seats; Plus is $44 per seat a month billed monthly, $35 billed yearly ([pricing](https://attio.com/pricing)). Moving between Attio and Outflare is easy one way: Outflare can [import an Attio workspace](/docs/guides/move-from-attio/) once.

## Pipedrive

A hosted sales CRM organised around deal pipelines, priced per seat. We couldn't read current prices on its pricing page, so check [pipedrive.com/pricing](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing). Pick it if your team lives in a sales pipeline and doesn't need marketing tools.

## Zoho CRM

A hosted CRM in Zoho's wider suite, free for up to 3 users, with paid plans per user ([pricing](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html)). Good value if you already use other Zoho apps.

## Twenty

An open-source CRM you can self-host for free, or use hosted: Pro is $9 per user a month and Organization $39, both billed yearly ([pricing](https://twenty.com/pricing)). Pick it for a dedicated open-source CRM you can run yourself.

## SuiteCRM

An open-source CRM you can download and host on your own server ([suitecrm.com](https://suitecrm.com/)). Suits teams that want a traditional CRM with full control of the code and data.

## Mautic

Open-source marketing automation you can self-host on your own infrastructure ([mautic.org](https://mautic.org/)). Pick it to replace HubSpot's marketing automation specifically, with another tool as the CRM.

## Outflare

An open-source (AGPL-3.0) workspace with a [CRM on customer records](/product/customer-data/): one record per person and company, deals and custom objects, full history, duplicates and merges, and [smart lists](/docs/guides/smart-lists/). Around it: [lifecycle email](/product/lifecycle-email/) and [journeys](/product/journeys/) through your own Amazon SES, Postmark or Resend; [cold email](/product/cold-email/) from your own mailboxes; [website analytics](/product/analytics/), [website visitors](/product/website-visitors/), [SEO](/product/seo/) and [cost per meeting](/product/outcomes/). AI agents work through the same API and an [MCP server](/product/mcp/).

What it isn't: a hosted email sender or a content management system. You connect your own email provider. There's no per-seat or per-contact pricing: self-hosting is free, and Outflare Cloud is free during early access. See [Outflare vs HubSpot](/compare/outflare-vs-hubspot/).

## How to choose

1. **What do you use HubSpot for?** If it's mostly the sales pipeline, Pipedrive, Attio or Twenty. If it's marketing automation, Mautic or Outflare. If it's CRM plus outreach and reporting, Outflare.
2. **Hosted or self-hosted?** Twenty, SuiteCRM, Mautic and Outflare run on your own servers; Attio, Pipedrive and Zoho CRM are hosted.
3. **Cold email?** HubSpot's sales sequences are per user. If you send cold email from many mailboxes, Outflare does that with one [policy check](/docs/concepts/policy-check/) on every email.
4. **Cost as you grow.** Check how each tool prices seats and contacts. HubSpot's paid hubs count marketing contacts; open-source tools cost what your hosting costs.
5. **Keep HubSpot?** If your sales team is settled in it, connect HubSpot to Outflare instead of replacing it, and get [cost per meeting](/product/outcomes/) from your outreach.
