## The shortlist

Prices are the lowest plan each vendor published, checked on its own site in November 2026. Where a vendor didn't publish a price we could read, we leave it out.

| Tool | Best for | Open source or self-hosted | Price (checked November 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Smartlead](#smartlead) | Budget cold email, agencies | No | Base from $39/month billed monthly |
| [Saleshandy](#saleshandy) | Low entry price, unlimited mailboxes | No | Starter from $41/month billed monthly |
| [EmailBison](#emailbison) | Very high volume on dedicated IPs | No | From $599/month for 500,000 emails |
| [lemlist](#lemlist) | Email plus LinkedIn, SMS and calls | No | Email plan from $55/month billed yearly |
| [Woodpecker](#woodpecker) | A base plan with add-ons you choose | No | Not published in readable form |

| [Outflare](#outflare) | Open source, AI agents, more than cold email | Yes, AGPL-3.0 | Free to self-host; Cloud free during early access |

## Smartlead

A hosted cold email platform with unlimited email accounts on every plan and a warm-up pool. Client workspaces and white label (a $29/month add-on per client workspace) suit agencies. It has an API and an MCP server. Base starts at $39/month billed monthly ([pricing](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing)). Outflare can control Smartlead's warm-up and import its campaigns. See [Outflare vs Smartlead](/compare/outflare-vs-smartlead/).

## Saleshandy

Cold email with unlimited sending mailboxes and no per-inbox fees, and unlimited warm-up for connected mailboxes. Its Starter plan was $41/month billed monthly, or $34/month billed yearly ([pricing](https://www.saleshandy.com/pricing/)). A good fit when price is the main reason you're leaving.

## EmailBison

Cold email at high volume on isolated infrastructure with dedicated IPs, with warm-up, an API, and unlimited leads, workspaces and teammates. The base plan is $599/month for 500,000 emails a month ([pricing](https://emailbison.com/pricing)). Outflare connects to EmailBison: it decides which sender emails each campaign uses, runs warm-up there, and brings replies into the [Outflare inbox](/product/inbox/).

## lemlist

Outreach across email, LinkedIn, SMS, calls and WhatsApp (as an add-on), with lemwarm warm-up on all plans and a lead database. The Email plan was $55/month billed yearly; Multichannel $87/month billed yearly ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Pick it if LinkedIn steps matter. See [Outflare vs lemlist](/compare/outflare-vs-lemlist/).

## Woodpecker

Cold email with unlimited email accounts, unlimited email verification and three warm-ups in the base plan, plus a LinkedIn plan. The API, webhooks and MCP come as one add-on, and an agency panel and white label are add-ons too ([pricing](https://woodpecker.co/pricing/)). Suits teams that want to pay only for the parts they use.

## Apollo

A B2B contact database with sequences, a parallel dialer and an MCP server. It has a free plan, which connects Gmail accounts only, and paid plans with credits pooled across the team ([pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing)). Pick it when finding contacts is the bigger problem than sending. Outflare can build lists from your Apollo account. See [Outflare vs Apollo](/compare/outflare-vs-apollo/).

## Outflare

Open-source software (AGPL-3.0) for [cold email](/product/cold-email/) from your own Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or SMTP mailboxes, with one [policy check](/docs/concepts/policy-check/) on every email, mailbox rotation, a [reply inbox](/product/inbox/) and a full API and [MCP server](/product/mcp/) for AI agents. The same workspace holds [analytics](/product/analytics/), [SEO](/product/seo/) and a [CRM](/product/customer-data/).

What it doesn't do: it sends no warm-up mail and has no lead database. Warm-up runs in a connected EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead, and leads come from your own data provider. Free to self-host; Outflare Cloud is free during early access.

## How to choose

1. **Why are you leaving Instantly?** If it's price, compare Smartlead and Saleshandy. If it's volume or deliverability control, look at EmailBison. If it's channels, lemlist.
2. **Do you need warm-up built in?** Every tool here except Outflare includes warm-up. With Outflare you keep a sequencer for warm-up, which can be Instantly itself.
3. **Do you need contact data?** Apollo and lemlist include a lead database. Outflare and the pure senders expect you to bring your own.
4. **Will AI agents send for you?** Check what an agent can and can't do. Outflare's MCP tokens carry scopes and a daily send budget, and every email passes the same policy check.
5. **Where must your data live?** Only Outflare can run on your own servers, with prospects and replies in your own Postgres.
6. **Can you keep Instantly?** Often the cheapest move is not to switch: connect Instantly to Outflare for warm-up and mailbox control, and use Outflare for the inbox, reporting and [cost per meeting](/product/outcomes/).
