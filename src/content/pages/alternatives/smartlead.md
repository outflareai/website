## The shortlist

## Instantly

Hosted cold email with warm-up, unlimited email accounts on its Growth and Hypergrowth plans, a 450M+ lead database paid with credits, a CRM on higher plans and AI agents in its bundles. It has an API and an official MCP server. Growth starts at $47/month billed monthly ([pricing](https://instantly.ai/pricing)). Outflare connects to Instantly too. See [Outflare vs Instantly](/compare/outflare-vs-instantly/).

## EmailBison

High-volume cold email on isolated infrastructure with dedicated IPs, warm-up, an API and unlimited leads, workspaces and teammates. $599/month for 500,000 emails ([pricing](https://emailbison.com/pricing)). Outflare connects to it for warm-up, sender allocation and replies.

## Saleshandy

Unlimited sending mailboxes with no per-inbox fee and unlimited warm-up. Starter was $41/month billed monthly, $34/month billed yearly ([pricing](https://www.saleshandy.com/pricing/)). A close match to Smartlead on price.

## Woodpecker

Unlimited email accounts and verification, three warm-ups in the base plan, and a LinkedIn plan. API, webhooks and MCP come as one add-on; the agency panel and white label are add-ons too ([pricing](https://woodpecker.co/pricing/)).

## lemlist

Email, LinkedIn, SMS, calls and WhatsApp (add-on) in one sequence, with lemwarm warm-up and a lead database. Email plan $55/month billed yearly, Multichannel $87/month billed yearly ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). See [Outflare vs lemlist](/compare/outflare-vs-lemlist/).

## Apollo

A B2B contact database with sequences, a dialer and an MCP server, a free plan and per-user paid plans with credits ([pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing)). Better when you need data as much as sending. See [Outflare vs Apollo](/compare/outflare-vs-apollo/).

## Outflare

Open-source (AGPL-3.0) [cold email](/product/cold-email/) from your own mailboxes, with one [policy check](/docs/concepts/policy-check/) on every email, [mailbox allocation](/docs/guides/mailbox-allocation/) that keeps follow-ups on the mailbox that started the thread, [infrastructure plans](/docs/guides/infrastructure/) for new domains and mailboxes, a [reply inbox](/product/inbox/), and an API and [MCP server](/product/mcp/) for AI agents. The same workspace holds [analytics](/product/analytics/), [SEO](/product/seo/) and a [CRM](/product/customer-data/).

It sends no warm-up mail and has no lead database, no agency accounts and no white label. Free to self-host; Outflare Cloud is free during early access.

## How to choose

1. **Agency or in-house?** Agencies reselling outreach under their own brand should look at Smartlead's own client workspaces, Woodpecker's agency panel or Instantly's Agency bundle. Outflare is for one company, with several [brands](/product/brands/).
2. **Volume.** At hundreds of thousands of emails a month, EmailBison's dedicated infrastructure is built for it. Outflare can run on top of it.
3. **Warm-up.** Every hosted tool here includes warm-up. Outflare switches warm-up on in a connected EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead and uses the score to decide when a mailbox is ready.
4. **Data.** Instantly, lemlist and Apollo sell lead data. Outflare uses your own Apollo, People Data Labs or MoltSets account.
5. **Agents and code.** If AI agents will run campaigns, prefer scoped tokens, send budgets and an audit log. If your data must stay on your servers, only Outflare self-hosts.
6. **Keep Smartlead?** You can: connect it to Outflare for warm-up control and sender allocation, and use Outflare for replies and [cost per meeting](/product/outcomes/).
