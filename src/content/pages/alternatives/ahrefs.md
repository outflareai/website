## The shortlist

Prices are the lowest plan each vendor published, checked on its own site in November 2026.

| [Majestic](#majestic) | Backlink data only | No | Lite $49.99/month billed monthly |

## Semrush

An SEO and AI search platform on its own database, which it puts at 29B keywords, with toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, content, local, advertising, PR and social. The SEO plan was $139/month billed monthly with 500 keywords tracked daily and AI visibility reports covering Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini; there is a free plan ([prices](https://www.semrush.com/prices/)). Its MCP server uses the same API units as its API. See [Outflare vs Semrush](/compare/outflare-vs-semrush/).

## SE Ranking

An SEO suite whose Core plan was $129/month billed monthly with 2,000 keywords tracked daily, API credits and MCP access, and AI visibility tracking with 25 prompts a day across 5 AI engines ([pricing](https://seranking.com/subscription.html)). Ahrefs Lite tracks 750 keywords for the same headline price.

## Majestic

A backlink specialist with a Fresh Index of links seen in the last 120 days and a Historic Index back to 2006. Lite is $49.99/month and Pro $99.99/month billed monthly; the API plan is $399.99/month ([pricing](https://majestic.com/plans-pricing)). Pick it if backlinks are all you use Ahrefs for.

## DataForSEO

SEO data by API with no subscription: add funds, pay per call. Minimum top-up $50, funds don't expire, SERP results from $0.0006 ([pricing](https://dataforseo.com/pricing)). Outflare connects to DataForSEO on your own account for domain rating, keywords, backlinks, competitors, rank tracking and AI visibility, and records each call's exact cost.

## Google Search Console

Google's free service for your own site: clicks, impressions, position, queries and index coverage ([about](https://search.google.com/search-console/about)). No competitor or backlink research. Outflare reads it page by page on the [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/).

## Outflare

Open-source (AGPL-3.0) SEO tracking for your own sites, in one workspace with [analytics](/product/analytics/), CRM and outreach. [OutflareBot](/docs/guides/site-audits/) crawls your sites, [Search Console](/docs/guides/keywords/) shows each page's queries for up to 16 months, [rank tracking](/docs/guides/keywords/#track-your-rankings) checks Google's top 100 on your DataForSEO or Geonode account, and [backlinks](/docs/guides/backlinks/) from Ahrefs and DataForSEO are merged into one list with every link gained, lost and regained. [Competitors](/product/competitors/) and [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/) run on the same accounts.

It has no backlink index or keyword database of its own. Free to self-host; Outflare Cloud is free during early access. See [Outflare vs Ahrefs](/compare/outflare-vs-ahrefs/).

## How to choose

1. **Do you need your own index?** Link and keyword research across the web needs a tool with its own data: Semrush, SE Ranking, Majestic for links, or DataForSEO by API.
2. **Is it only your own sites?** Search Console and Ahrefs Free cost nothing. Add Outflare for history, rank tracking, budgets and one view across several [brands](/product/brands/).
3. **Do you want AI visibility too?** Semrush and SE Ranking include it in plans; Ahrefs sells Brand Radar as an add-on; Outflare runs it on your DataForSEO account or your own AI key.
4. **Keep Ahrefs?** If its data is what you trust, connect it to Outflare: snapshots are stored weekly, so reading history in Outflare spends no units.
