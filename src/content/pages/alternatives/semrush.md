## The shortlist

Prices are the lowest plan each vendor published, checked on its own site in November 2026.

| Tool | Best for | Open source or self-hosted | Price (checked November 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| [SE Ranking](#se-ranking) | A lower-priced suite with AI visibility | No | Core $129/month billed monthly |

## Ahrefs

An SEO platform on its own crawler and index, with Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker, Content Explorer and Brand Radar for AI search. Lite is $129/month with 750 tracked keywords and API and MCP access; Starter is $29/month for keyword and competitor research; Ahrefs Free covers verified sites ([pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)). Outflare connects to Ahrefs. See [Outflare vs Ahrefs](/compare/outflare-vs-ahrefs/).

## SE Ranking

An SEO suite whose Core plan was $129/month billed monthly with 2,000 keywords tracked daily, API credits and MCP access, and AI visibility tracking with 25 prompts a day across 5 AI engines included ([pricing](https://seranking.com/subscription.html)). Its entry price was below Semrush's $139/month SEO plan when we checked.

## Majestic

A backlink specialist, with a Fresh Index of links seen in the last 120 days and a Historic Index going back to 2006. Lite is $49.99/month billed monthly; an API plan is $399.99/month ([pricing](https://majestic.com/plans-pricing)). Pick it if links are what you use Semrush for.

## DataForSEO

SEO data by API with no subscription: you add funds and pay per call. The minimum top-up is $50, funds don't expire, and SERP results start at $0.0006 each ([pricing](https://dataforseo.com/pricing)). It suits developers and tools that build on raw data. Outflare connects to DataForSEO for rankings, backlinks, competitors, rank tracking and AI visibility, on your own account.

## Google Search Console

Google's free service for your own site: clicks, impressions and position on Google Search, the queries that bring people, and index coverage ([about](https://search.google.com/search-console/about)). It doesn't show competitors. Outflare reads it page by page and query by query on the [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/).

## Outflare

Open-source (AGPL-3.0) SEO tracking for your own sites. [OutflareBot audits](/docs/guides/site-audits/) your sites (up to 10,000 pages a crawl once you prove ownership), the [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/) shows up to 16 months of Search Console by page and query, and [rank tracking](/docs/guides/keywords/#track-your-rankings) checks Google's top 100 through your DataForSEO or Geonode account. Ahrefs or DataForSEO adds domain rating, [backlinks](/docs/guides/backlinks/) and [competitors](/product/competitors/), and [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/) shows how AI answers mention you.

Every paid call has a monthly budget and is recorded with its cost. It has no keyword research database of its own. Free to self-host; Outflare Cloud is free during early access. See [Outflare vs Semrush](/compare/outflare-vs-semrush/).

## How to choose

1. **Research or tracking?** For finding new keywords and studying markets, you need a tool with its own data: Ahrefs or SE Ranking. For tracking your own sites, Search Console plus Outflare or Ahrefs Free may be enough.
2. **Links only?** Majestic is focused on backlinks.
3. **Building your own reports or agents?** DataForSEO's pay-as-you-go API, or Outflare's API and [MCP server](/product/mcp/) over the data it stores.
4. **AI search.** Semrush, Ahrefs and SE Ranking track AI answers in their plans or add-ons; Outflare does it on your own DataForSEO account or AI key.
5. **Cost control.** Subscriptions are predictable; pay-per-call is cheaper for a few domains. Outflare caps each connection with a monthly budget.
