## How we chose

"AI marketing agent" covers very different products. We looked for tools that act on marketing work, not only write copy, and compared them on:

- **What the agent can act on.** Your CRM, mailboxes, website, SEO data, ads or only documents.
- **Control.** Approvals, limits on sending and spend, and a record of what the agent did.
- **Whose AI it runs on.** The vendor's bundled model, or your own key.
- **Openness.** Open source, self-hosting, an API and MCP for your own agents.

Every fact about another company below was checked on its own website in November 2026. Prices change; follow the links before you buy.

## The shortlist

### Outflare

An open-source workspace where an agent runs [missions](/docs/agents/missions/): you give it a goal such as "book 10 meetings with fintech CFOs", it drafts a plan from your workspace, and works within the mode, send limits and spend limits you approve. It acts on cold email, lead lists, the reply inbox, SEO, AI visibility, analytics and customer records, and every call it makes is shown. You can also drive it from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor through the [MCP server](/product/mcp/).

**Best for:** teams that want one agent working across outreach, SEO and CRM, with approvals and their own AI key. **Not the best pick** if you mainly want a content-writing agent with brand templates: Jasper or Copy.ai do that better.

### HubSpot Breeze agents

HubSpot lists a [Customer agent, Prospecting agent, Data agent and Breeze Assistant](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence/breeze-ai-agents), plus an agent builder. Its page says Agent Hub is included for Starter, Professional and Enterprise customers, and custom agents use HubSpot Credits each time they run.

**Best for:** teams whose contacts, deals and support already live in HubSpot.

### Jasper

Jasper calls itself a generative platform for marketing, with "100+ purpose-built marketing agents" and a no-code agent builder on Business plans. [Pro is $69 per seat a month billed monthly, or $59 billed yearly](https://www.jasper.ai/pricing) (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** marketing teams producing a lot of on-brand written and visual content.

### Copy.ai

A go-to-market AI platform built around Workflows: chains of AI actions that codify a process, such as an account plan or an SEO article. The [Chat plan is $29 a month](https://www.copy.ai/prices) billed monthly; Workflow plans start at $1,000 a month (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** sales and marketing operations teams that want repeatable AI workflows.

### AirOps

AirOps presents itself as the partner behind a brand's AI search strategy, with answer engine tracking and content tasks. Its [pricing page](https://www.airops.com/pricing) lists Solo and Pro plans you can start for free, priced by tasks, and Enterprise.

**Best for:** content teams producing and refreshing pages for AI search.

### Relevance AI

"The home of the AI Workforce": a platform for building your own AI agents and tools for sales, marketing and operations, with over 2,000 integrations. Its [pricing page](https://relevanceai.com/pricing) lists an Enterprise plan through sales.

**Best for:** teams that want to design their own agents rather than use ready-made ones.

### Profound

An AI marketing platform whose AI Marketer runs research, writing and reporting through Agents, alongside tracking how brands appear in AI answer engines. [Pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) shows a free trial and custom Enterprise plans.

**Best for:** larger brands focused on AI search presence.

## How Outflare fits

Outflare is the pick when the agent must touch real systems: send cold email from your mailboxes, build lists from your data provider, read Search Console, update customer records. Each action goes through the same API a person uses, with the token's scopes, the [policy check](/docs/concepts/policy-check/) on every email and an audit log entry under the agent's name. [Playbooks](/docs/agents/playbooks/) give it ready-made recipes.

Agents that keep working toward a target on their own, such as the [PR autopilot](/docs/guides/pr-autopilot/), are Outflare Cloud features; everything else here is in the open-source engine. See [Open source and Cloud](/docs/project/open-source-and-cloud/).

Outflare never supplies an AI key, data or mailboxes. Each workspace connects its own accounts and pays those providers directly.
