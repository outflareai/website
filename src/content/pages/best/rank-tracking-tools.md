## How we chose

A rank tracker checks where your pages rank on Google for keywords you choose, on a schedule, and keeps the history. We compared:

- **Keywords and price.** How many keywords the entry plan tracks, and what it costs.
- **Update frequency.** Daily, weekly or on demand.
- **Extras.** Local and mobile results, competitors, AI search tracking.
- **Access.** API, exports and reports.

Every fact about another company was checked on its own website in November 2026.

## The shortlist

### AccuRanker

A dedicated rank tracker. [Professional starts at €229 a month](https://www.accuranker.com/pricing/) for 2,000 keywords with daily updates; Expert covers 10,000 to 20,000 (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** agencies and in-house teams tracking thousands of keywords daily.

### SE Ranking

An SEO platform with rank tracking, a report builder and AI visibility tracking. [Core is $129 a month](https://seranking.com/subscription.html), or $103.20 billed yearly, for 2,000 keywords tracked daily (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams that want daily tracking and client reports in one suite.

### Semrush Position Tracking

Part of Semrush's SEO plan. [The SEO plan is $139 a month](https://www.semrush.com/prices/), or $117.33 billed yearly, with 500 keywords tracked daily across five websites (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams already using Semrush for research.

### Ahrefs Rank Tracker

Part of Ahrefs. [Lite is $129 a month](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) with 750 tracked keywords; Standard is $249 with 2,000 (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** Ahrefs users who want rankings next to backlinks.

### Nightwatch

Rank tracking with local results (Google Maps) and AI tracking on every plan, covering ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Mode and AI Overviews. [Starter is €79 a month](https://nightwatch.io/pricing/) for 500 keywords tracked daily (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** local SEO and teams that want classic and AI rankings together.

### Wincher

A rank tracker whose [plans](https://www.wincher.com/pricing) all include daily ranking updates, from 500 keywords for small teams to 5,000 and more for agencies, with a free trial.

**Best for:** small teams that want a simple daily tracker.

### Mangools SERPWatcher

Rank tracking inside the Mangools suite, with [weekly updates, or daily on the Agency plan](https://mangools.com/plans-and-pricing).

**Best for:** beginners who also want Mangools' keyword research tools.

### Outflare

Rank tracking in an [open-source SEO module](/product/seo/). Type keywords, or track every page's target keyword, choose desktop or mobile, and each is checked weekly or daily. Checks read Google's top 100 through your own [DataForSEO or Geonode](/docs/integrations/seo/#rank-tracking) connection: about $0.02 a check on DataForSEO, or about $0.005 on Geonode, billed by them, within a monthly budget you set. Each keyword shows its position, movement, best position and the ranking page, beside the page's target and its Search Console clicks, and the history keeps the top 10 so you see who outranks you.

**Best for:** teams that track a modest number of keywords and want to pay per check, or want rankings in an API and for AI agents. **Not the best pick** for tracking thousands of keywords across many locations with polished client reports: AccuRanker or SE Ranking are built for that.

## Rank tracking without a tracker

[Google Search Console](/docs/integrations/analytics/) gives every page's average position for free. Outflare reads it on its [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/) for up to 16 months, with drops, cannibalisation and indexing, and sends any query to rank tracking with one click. Use Search Console to see trends, and a rank tracker for the keywords you are working on.

Outflare never supplies a data account. Rank tracking stays off until you connect DataForSEO or Geonode. See [SEO](/product/seo/) and [competitor tracking](/product/competitors/).
