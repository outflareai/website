## How we chose

An SEO report pulls together search traffic, rankings, technical health and, more and more, AI search, for someone who has to act on it. We compared tools on:

- **Sources.** Google Analytics, Search Console, rank data and other SEO tools.
- **Who reads it.** Your own team, or clients who expect a branded report.
- **Sharing.** Links, PDFs, schedules and white label.
- **Price.** Per client, per dashboard or per plan.

Every fact about another company was checked on its own website in November 2026.

## The shortlist

### Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio)

Google describes [Data Studio](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/studio) as "a no-cost tool" for dashboards and reports, and its [connector list](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/available-connectors) includes Google Analytics and Search Console.

**Best for:** anyone willing to build and maintain their own dashboards.

### AgencyAnalytics

A reporting platform for agencies with 90+ integrations and a white-labelled client portal. [The core plan is $20 per client a month](https://agencyanalytics.com/pricing), billed yearly; rank tracking is an add-on (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** agencies reporting to many clients.

### DashThis

Marketing dashboards with every integration on every plan. [Individual is $54 a month](https://dashthis.com/pricing/), or $44 billed yearly, for three dashboards (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** small agencies and freelancers with a few clients.

### Whatagraph

A marketing reporting tool that connects, blends and reports on data, with AI agents for setup and insights. [Max starts at €699 a month](https://whatagraph.com/pricing), billed yearly, with unlimited users and reports (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** larger teams reporting across many channels.

### SE Ranking report builder

Included in SE Ranking's plans: [Core, $129 a month](https://seranking.com/subscription.html), includes up to 10 reports; white-label reports need the Agency Pack add-on (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** SE Ranking users reporting rankings and audits.

### Ahrefs Report Builder

[Listed on Ahrefs' Lite plan, $129 a month](https://ahrefs.com/pricing), with a $99 a month add-on for 50 scheduled reports; Looker Studio integration is on Advanced (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** Ahrefs users who want reports from Ahrefs data.

### Outflare

[SEO analytics](/product/analytics/) for your own websites in an open-source workspace. It reads [Search Console, GA4, Bing Webmaster Tools, PostHog, Plausible and more](/docs/integrations/analytics/) into one view per website, with each tool side by side. The [SEO module](/product/seo/) adds what each page ranks for, target keywords, drops, cannibalisation, indexing, conversions by page, tracked rankings and [site audits](/docs/guides/site-audits/), and [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/) adds how AI answers mention you, with a read-only share link. A [digest](/docs/guides/digests/) sums it up each week, against the week before, as a webhook or email. Agents can ask "how is the site doing?" over [MCP](/product/mcp/).

**Best for:** in-house teams and founders who want one place for SEO numbers and next steps. **Not the best pick** for branded client reports: Outflare has no white label or agency accounts, and no drag-and-drop report designer for Search Console widgets.

## Picking one

If clients receive the report, choose a tool with white label and scheduling. If your own team acts on it, choose the tool that shows what to do next: the pages that dropped, the fixes from the last crawl, the questions AI answers don't name you for. Outflare lists drops on its Keywords page, and audit and AI visibility problems on its fixes list.

Outflare never supplies keys or data. Analytics connections sign in to your own accounts read-only, and rank and SEO data run on your own DataForSEO, Geonode or Ahrefs account. See [rank tracking tools](/best/rank-tracking-tools/).
