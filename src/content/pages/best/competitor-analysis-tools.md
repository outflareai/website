## How we chose

Competitor analysis covers several jobs: finding who you compete with, studying their keywords, links and ads, estimating their traffic, and following what they change. We compared:

- **Where the data comes from.** The vendor's own index or panel, or an account you connect.
- **What it is best at.** Traffic and market research, keyword and backlink research, search ads, or ongoing tracking of a fixed list.
- **Entry price.** Taken from the vendor's own pricing page. Where the page gave no clear price, we left it out.
- **Access.** API and MCP, for scripts and AI agents.

Every fact about another company was checked on its own website in November 2026.

## The shortlist

### Similarweb

A digital intelligence platform for websites, apps and markets. It says it blends first-party analytics, its own contributory network, public data and partner data ([our data](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/ourdata/)), and lists suites for competitive intelligence, SEO, ads and AI search. Self-service packages are for individuals and small teams, business packages are quoted by sales, and there is a free trial ([pricing](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/pricing/)).

**Best for:** market research and benchmarking traffic across many sites, channels and countries. See [Outflare vs Similarweb](/compare/outflare-vs-similarweb/).

### Semrush

An SEO and AI search platform on its own keyword and domain data, with toolkits beyond SEO, including traffic and market research. [The SEO plan is $139 a month](https://www.semrush.com/prices/), or $117.33 billed yearly, and there is a free plan (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** keyword research and competitor research on a large data set in one subscription. See [Outflare vs Semrush](/compare/outflare-vs-semrush/).

### Ahrefs

An SEO platform built on its own crawler and backlink index. Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer and Competitive Analysis are on [Lite, from $129 a month](https://ahrefs.com/pricing); Starter is $29 a month (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** backlink research and studying how competitors earn links. See [Outflare vs Ahrefs](/compare/outflare-vs-ahrefs/).

### SpyFu

A competitor research tool for SEO and Google Ads: the keywords rivals rank for and buy, the ads they have tested, ranking history and backlinks ([spyfu.com](https://www.spyfu.com/)). SpyFu's prices differed between its own pages when we checked, so we left them out.

**Best for:** search ads research and finding the paid keywords competitors bid on.

### SE Ranking

An SEO platform with rank tracking, competitor research and reports. [Core is $129 a month](https://seranking.com/subscription.html), or $103.20 billed yearly, and lists unlimited keyword, competitor and backlink research with 2,000 keywords tracked daily (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams that want competitor research and daily rank tracking at a mid-range price.

### Search Atlas

An SEO platform that calls itself an agentic marketing platform: OTTO SEO applies technical and on-page fixes, with site audits, rank tracking, backlink analysis, content, local SEO, ads and LLM visibility. [Starter is $99 a month](https://searchatlas.com/pricing/), billed monthly, with a 7-day trial (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** small teams and agencies that want one platform that also makes changes on the site. See [Outflare vs Search Atlas](/compare/outflare-vs-search-atlas/).

### seoClarity

An enterprise SEO and AEO platform: rank tracking with unlimited competitors, site audits, log file analysis, content, Clarity ArcAI for AI search, an API and an MCP server. [Packages are priced](https://www.seoclarity.net/pricing) by the number of domains and keywords tracked, with a quote.

**Best for:** large sites and global teams with thousands of keywords. See [Outflare vs seoClarity](/compare/outflare-vs-seoclarity/).

### Outflare

[Competitor tracking](/product/competitors/) in an open-source marketing workspace. Every day your own [Ahrefs or DataForSEO](/docs/integrations/seo/) connection suggests the domains sharing the most keywords with your site. For the ones you track, Outflare reads domain rating, organic traffic, keywords and referring domains each week, and OutflareBot looks at their sitemaps and home page. A Changes feed shows new pages, removed pages, title changes and moves in their numbers. Keyword gaps and link gaps compare them with you, and an Overview ranks you against them across search, [AI answers](/product/ai-visibility/), placements, social followers and reviews. Everything is in the [API and MCP server](/product/mcp/).

**Best for:** following a set list of competitors over time, next to your own [SEO](/product/seo/), [analytics](/product/analytics/) and outreach, and paying your data provider per call. **Not the best pick** for research across a whole market or many sites you don't track: Outflare has no index or traffic panel of its own, so Similarweb, Semrush or Ahrefs do more there.

## Where Outflare fits

Use a research tool to find and size competitors once. Use Outflare to keep watching the ones that matter: what they publish, how their search numbers move, which keywords and links they have that you don't, and who mentions them in [AI answers](/product/ai-visibility/). Each competitor is read at most once a month by default, within a monthly budget you set, and everything read is stored, so looking back costs nothing.

Outflare never supplies a data account. Suggestions and search numbers stay off until you connect Ahrefs or DataForSEO; without one you can still add competitors by hand, and OutflareBot's weekly look still works. See [Follow your competitors](/docs/guides/competitors/).
