  - q: What is the best email warm-up tool?
    a: If you already send with Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist or EmailBison, use the warm-up it includes. If you send from a tool without warm-up, a standalone tool such as MailReach, Warmy or Warmup Inbox does the job.
  - q: Does Outflare warm up mailboxes?
    a: No. Outflare sends no warm-up mail. It turns warm-up on or off for each mailbox in a connected EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead account, shows that sequencer's warm-up score, and uses it to decide when a new mailbox is ready to send.
  - q: How long should I warm up a new mailbox?
    a: Outflare's docs plan on two weeks before a new mailbox sends sequences, and recommend leaving warm-up on afterwards. Smartlead's own FAQ suggests at least two weeks for existing accounts and one to two months for new domains.
  - q: Should a mailbox warm up in two tools at once?
    a: No. That doubles its warm-up mail. Outflare flags a mailbox warming in two connected sequencers and lets you pick one with Warm up in.
  - q: Are free email warm-up tools any good?
    a: Warmup Inbox has a free tier of 15 warm-up emails a week. It is enough to keep a quiet mailbox active, but not to prepare a new mailbox for cold email volume.

## How we chose

Warm-up sends and receives mail between your mailbox and a network of other mailboxes, opening it, replying and rescuing it from spam, so Google and Microsoft see a history of wanted mail before your cold email starts. We compared tools on:

- **Where it runs.** Inside the sequencer you send with, or standalone.
- **The network.** What the vendor says about the mailboxes your warm-up mail goes to.
- **Extras.** Placement tests, DNS and DMARC checks, blocklist monitoring.
- **Price.** Per mailbox, per plan, or included.

Every fact about another company was checked on its own website in November 2026. New to the idea? Read [what email warm-up is](/glossary/email-warmup/).

## The shortlist

### Instantly

Warm-up in the same tool that sends. Instantly's [warm-up page](https://instantly.ai/email-warmup) says its pool has over 1,000,000 real email accounts and that volume ramps up gradually. The [Growth plan, $47 a month](https://instantly.ai/pricing), lists unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams sending cold email with Instantly.

### Smartlead

[Warm-up starts](https://www.smartlead.ai/email-warmup) as soon as you connect a mailbox, in a private pool of vetted senders, and pauses on risks such as bounce spikes.

**Best for:** agencies sending with Smartlead from large mailbox pools.

### lemwarm (lemlist)

Warms up inboxes "across 20,000+ real domains" and tracks a deliverability score. [lemwarm](https://www.lemwarm.com/) is included in every lemlist plan and is also sold alone, with the Essential plan listed at $29 per mailbox a month, less on longer billing (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** lemlist users, or senders who want a standalone warm-up plan with a deliverability score.

### MailReach

Warm-up plus spam tests (inbox placement tests with a report), SPF, DKIM and DMARC checkers and blacklist checks. [Warm-up is $19.5 per mailbox a month](https://www.mailreach.co/pricing) on monthly billing (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams that want warm-up and placement testing without changing sender.

### Warmy

A deliverability platform: warm-up for domains and mailboxes, placement tests, DMARC monitoring and list verification. [Plans start from $49 a month](https://www.warmy.io/pricing), priced by volume, with a 7-day free trial (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** senders who want warm-up and authentication monitoring in one place.

### Warmup Inbox

A standalone warm-up service priced per inbox. Its [pricing page](https://www.warmupinbox.com/pricing) lists a free tier (15 warm-up emails a week) and paid plans from Basic, at 75 warm-up messages a day, to Max, at 1,000.

**Best for:** a few mailboxes on a sender without built-in warm-up.

### EmailBison

A sending platform whose [Base plan, $599 a month](https://emailbison.com/pricing) for 500,000 emails, includes warm-up with bounce protection, on isolated infrastructure with dedicated IPs (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** very high volume senders who want sending and warm-up together.

## Where Outflare fits

Outflare is cold email and deliverability software, not a warm-up tool. It [used to send warm-up mail](/docs/guides/warmup/) between your own mailboxes; it no longer does. Instead:

- **It switches warm-up in your sequencer.** Connect EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead, then turn warm-up on for one mailbox, a pool or every mailbox. Outflare doesn't switch warm-up in lemwarm, MailReach, Warmy or Warmup Inbox.
- **It shows the score next to everything else.** Each mailbox's warm-up score sits beside its bounces, DNS and blocklist standing in [mailbox health](/docs/guides/mailbox-health/).
- **It acts on the score.** In an [infrastructure plan](/docs/guides/infrastructure/), a mailbox moves from warming to ready once its warm-up days are done and its score is at least 85. A score under 75 keeps a mailbox from taking new leads.
- **It keeps warm-up mail out of your inbox.** Paste the sequencer's warm-up tag and Outflare's reply inbox leaves those emails out.

Your sequencer account is your own and you pay it directly. See [deliverability](/product/deliverability/).
