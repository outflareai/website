## How we chose

We compared tools that send multi-step cold email sequences from your own mailboxes, on:

- **Sending at volume.** Mailbox rotation, limits, and how many mailboxes a plan allows.
- **Deliverability.** Warm-up, DNS checks, and what stops a bad send.
- **Data.** Whether leads come built in or from your own provider.
- **Control and openness.** API, MCP for AI agents, open source, self-hosting.

Every fact about another company was checked on its own website in November 2026. We list a price only where the vendor's page showed it clearly.

## The shortlist

### Instantly

A cold email platform with mailbox rotation and its own warm-up network, which its warm-up page says has over 1,000,000 real email accounts. The [Growth plan is $47 a month](https://instantly.ai/pricing) billed monthly and lists unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams and agencies sending from many mailboxes who want warm-up in the same tool.

### Smartlead

High-volume sending with unlimited mailboxes; its [pricing FAQ](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing) says "All Smartlead plans include unlimited email accounts at no extra cost." Warm-up runs from the moment you connect a mailbox, in a private pool.

**Best for:** agencies running many client campaigns from large mailbox pools.

### lemlist

Outreach across email, LinkedIn, calls and SMS, with the lemwarm warm-up included in every plan. The [Email plan is $55 per user a month billed yearly](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing); Multichannel adds LinkedIn and a dialer (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** personalised, multichannel outreach by small sales teams.

### Apollo

A sales platform with a B2B database and email sequences. [Its pricing page](https://www.apollo.io/pricing) lists a free plan with no time limit (campaigns from Gmail accounts only) and paid Basic, Professional and Custom plans.

**Best for:** teams that want contacts and sequences in one tool.

### EmailBison

A deliverability-focused sending platform with a master inbox, sequences, private warm-up and dedicated IPs. [The Base plan is $599 a month](https://emailbison.com/pricing) for 500,000 emails, with no per-seat fees (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** very high volume senders who want isolated infrastructure.

### Woodpecker

Cold email and LinkedIn outreach. Its [pricing page](https://woodpecker.co/pricing/) lists unlimited email accounts and three warm-ups on its Email and Multichannel plans, with unlimited email verification.

**Best for:** smaller teams that want a straightforward sequencer.

### Outflare

Open-source cold email: sequences of up to 20 steps sent from your own Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or SMTP mailboxes, in each lead's thread, until they reply. One [policy check](/docs/concepts/policy-check/) runs on every email from the app, the API or an AI agent, so suppression and send limits can't be skipped. Replies land in a [unified inbox](/product/inbox/), and the same workspace holds your CRM, SEO and analytics.

**Best for:** teams that want AI agents to run outreach safely, or want to self-host. **Not the best pick** if you need a built-in contact database (use Apollo) or warm-up inside the same tool: Outflare switches warm-up in a connected EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead account and sends no warm-up mail itself.

## Can you use more than one?

Yes, and many teams do. Outflare reads a connected EmailBison, Instantly or Smartlead account, turns warm-up on there, and can [import its campaigns](/docs/guides/switch-from-instantly-smartlead/) as draft sequences, with bounces and unsubscribes added to your suppression list. Nothing in the other tool is changed.

Outflare never sells mailboxes, sending or data. You connect your own mailboxes and providers and pay them directly. See [cold email](/product/cold-email/) and [deliverability](/product/deliverability/).
