## How we chose

We compared tools built to track how AI answers mention a brand, on:

- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode.
- **What you learn.** Mentions, recommendations, cited sources, sentiment, competitors.
- **What to do next.** Whether the tool points at pages to get onto or facts to fix.
- **Price and whose account it runs on.**

Engines and prices were checked on each vendor's own website in November 2026. New to the term? Read [what LLM visibility is](/glossary/llm-visibility/).

## The shortlist

### Profound

An AI marketing platform that tracks citations, sentiment, ranking and competitive presence in AI answer engines. Its [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) lists ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews on the free trial, and up to nine engines on Enterprise, including Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude and DeepSeek.

**Best for:** large brands that want many engines and a managed platform.

### Peec AI

AI search analytics for marketing teams and agencies. [Starter is $95 a month](https://peec.ai/pricing) for 50 prompts, choosing three of ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI; Enterprise tracks up to 13 models (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams that want a focused, self-serve tracker.

### Otterly.AI

An AI search monitoring tool whose [site](https://otterly.ai/) leads with tracking ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. See its pricing page for current plans.

**Best for:** small teams and agencies starting out with AI search monitoring.

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

Shows how brands appear in AI-generated answers, with prompt tracking on platforms such as Google AI Mode and ChatGPT. Semrush's [knowledge base](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) lists it at $99 a month (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** teams already using Semrush for SEO.

### Ahrefs Brand Radar

Tracks your brand across AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. [Custom prompts start at $50 a month and the AI Visibility Index at $199 a month](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar) (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** Ahrefs users who want AI visibility next to backlinks.

### Scrunch

Monitors ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and Meta, and adds a layer that serves AI agents. [Starter is $300 a month](https://scrunch.com/pricing), or $250 billed yearly (checked November 2026).

**Best for:** brands that want monitoring and changes to how AI agents read their site.

### Outflare

[AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/) in an open-source workspace. It asks your buyers' questions two ways, both on your own accounts: ChatGPT and Gemini on their own screens through your [DataForSEO](/docs/integrations/connectors/) connection, and models such as Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, xAI and DeepSeek through your own AI key. You get a 0 to 100 score per brand, question by question; the pages the answers cite, ranked by what being named there is worth; what AI says you are known for and holds against you; facts the answers get wrong; and the visits AI assistants sent your site. Site audits check which AI crawlers your robots.txt lets in.

**Best for:** teams that want open-source tracking with clear next steps, and to pay providers directly. **Not the best pick** if you need Perplexity, Claude's app or Google AI Mode as people see them on screen: the tools above list those surfaces.

## What to look for

A score on its own is a vanity number. Look for a tool that tells you which questions don't name you, which pages the answers cite, and whether those pages mention you. That list is your to-do list, and it is the same work as [generative engine optimisation](/glossary/generative-engine-optimization/).

Outflare never supplies a key or data. With neither a DataForSEO nor an AI model connection, AI visibility stays off and says which connection to add. See [the AI visibility guide](/docs/guides/ai-visibility/).
