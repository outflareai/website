## The short verdict

- **Pick seoClarity** if you are an enterprise SEO team: many domains, thousands of keywords, large sites, log files, AI search tracking and a support team with SLAs, on a platform built for that scale.
- **Pick Outflare** if you want to track your own sites on accounts you own: audits by [OutflareBot](/docs/guides/site-audits/), [Search Console by page](/docs/guides/keywords/), rank tracking, backlinks, [competitors](/product/competitors/) and [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/), next to your [analytics](/product/analytics/), [CRM](/product/customer-data/) and outreach, with the code open.
- **Use both** if you already run seoClarity. There is no seoClarity connector, so they run side by side.

## Where seoClarity is stronger

- **Scale.** Packages are built around many domains and thousands of keywords, with unlimited crawl pages on Technical SEO. OutflareBot stops at 10,000 pages per crawl.
- **Log file analysis.** seoClarity reads server logs to show how search engines crawl a site. Outflare doesn't.
- **Support.** Its Research & Content package lists a dedicated support team (client success, technical support and SEO advice), and Enterprise adds SLAs and roadmap input.
- **One subscription.** Ranking and research data come with the package. With Outflare you connect and pay DataForSEO, Ahrefs or Geonode yourself.

## Where Outflare is different

- **Open source on your accounts.** Every paid call goes to your own provider, is checked against a monthly budget before it runs, and is recorded in [Outcomes](/product/outcomes/) with its cost. Self-hosted, the data stays in your own Postgres.
- **A fixes list for people and agents.** Audit issues become a [fixes list](/docs/guides/site-audits/#the-fixes-list), and [fix autonomy](/docs/guides/fix-autonomy/) sets how far agents may go, with changes to redirects, canonicals, indexing and robots.txt always held for a person.
- **Experiments.** [SEO experiments](/product/experiments/) compare matched treatment and control pages from your own Search Console data.
- **Competitors and a Changes feed.** Outflare follows a list of competitors each week and shows what they publish and change, with keyword and link gaps. See [competitor tracking](/product/competitors/).
- **Search next to everything else.** [Website analytics](/product/analytics/), [paid media](/product/paid-media/), [customer records](/product/customer-data/) and [cold email](/product/cold-email/) live in the same workspace, and agents read all of it through the [MCP server](/product/mcp/).

## How to use Outflare with or instead of seoClarity

1. **Add your sites** under Site audits and prove ownership to crawl up to 10,000 pages.
2. **Connect Google Search Console** (read-only) for the [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/).
3. **Connect DataForSEO or Ahrefs** for domain rating, backlinks and competitors, and DataForSEO or Geonode for rank tracking, each with a monthly budget.
4. **Turn on AI visibility** with your DataForSEO connection or your own AI key.

If your sites outgrow 10,000 pages per crawl, or you need log files, keep an enterprise platform for those.

## What it costs

Self-hosting Outflare is free under AGPL-3.0, and Outflare Cloud is free during early access. DataForSEO, Ahrefs, Geonode and your AI provider bill you directly. seoClarity quotes its own packages.
