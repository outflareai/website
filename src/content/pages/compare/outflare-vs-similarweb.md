## The short verdict

- **Pick Similarweb** if your question is about the market: how much traffic other sites get, from which channels and countries, how apps and companies compare, and how AI search sends visits. It is a larger, specialised product built on its own data.
- **Pick Outflare** if your question is about your own sites and a known list of competitors: what they published this week, how their search numbers moved, which keywords and links they have that you don't, all next to your own [analytics](/product/analytics/), [SEO](/product/seo/) and outreach.
- **Use both** if you rely on Similarweb for market research. There is no Similarweb connector, so they run side by side.

## Where Similarweb is stronger

- **Market data you can't get from your own accounts.** Similarweb estimates visits, channels and audiences for sites you don't own. Outflare only reads what your own SEO connection returns.
- **Breadth.** It lists products for web, apps, sales, retail and investors, and coverage it puts at 100M+ websites and 4M+ apps.
- **History and countries.** Its pricing page offers up to 37 months of data and country-level coverage in more than 100 countries.
- **AI search intelligence.** AI traffic trends, AI brand visibility and prompt analysis are part of its suites.

## Where Outflare is different

- **A feed of what competitors do.** OutflareBot reads a tracked competitor's robots.txt, sitemaps and home page each week, and the [Changes feed](/docs/guides/competitors/#the-changes-feed) shows new pages, removed pages, a new title or description, and moves in domain rating, traffic, keywords and referring domains.
- **Gaps you can act on.** Keyword gaps and link gaps compare tracked competitors with your own site, down to the exact pages that link to them.
- **Who is winning.** The [Competitors Overview](/product/competitors/) scores and ranks you against them across search, [AI answers](/product/ai-visibility/), placements, social followers and reviews, with time to catch up.
- **Your own numbers, measured.** Outflare's [website analytics](/product/analytics/) reads Google Analytics, Search Console, PostHog and other tools you use, so your side of the comparison is measured, not estimated.
- **Your accounts, your costs.** Each competitor is read at most once a month by default, within a monthly budget, and stored, so looking back costs nothing. Everything is in the [API and MCP server](/product/mcp/).

## How to follow competitors in Outflare

1. **Add your site** under Site audits. It doesn't need to be proved for competitor tracking.
2. **Connect Ahrefs or DataForSEO** on your own account, with a monthly budget. See [SEO connectors](/docs/integrations/seo/).
3. **Answer the suggestions.** Say yes to the competitors that matter, or type their domains.
4. **Read the Changes feed and gaps** each week, or ask an agent "what did our competitors publish this month?"

Keep Similarweb for market sizing and traffic research if you use it; Outflare follows the competitors you picked.

## What it costs

Self-hosting Outflare is free under AGPL-3.0, and Outflare Cloud is free during early access. Ahrefs or DataForSEO bill you directly for competitor numbers. Similarweb bills its own packages; see [its pricing page](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/pricing/).
