## The short verdict

- **Pick Search Atlas** if you want one hosted platform that does SEO work as well as reporting it: OTTO SEO applies fixes, Content Genius writes and publishes, and plans add local SEO, ads, LLM visibility and, on Pro and Agency, white label. It is a broader, specialised SEO product.
- **Pick Outflare** if you want open-source SEO on accounts you own: audits by [OutflareBot](/docs/guides/site-audits/), a fixes list with [fix autonomy](/docs/guides/fix-autonomy/), [Search Console by page](/docs/guides/keywords/), rank tracking, [competitors](/product/competitors/) and [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility/), next to your [analytics](/product/analytics/), [CRM](/product/customer-data/) and outreach.
- **Use both** if you rely on Search Atlas. There is no Search Atlas connector, so they run side by side.

## Where Search Atlas is stronger

- **One subscription that does the work.** OTTO applies fixes and Content Genius writes and publishes to your CMS, with data included in the plan's credits. With Outflare you connect and pay DataForSEO, Ahrefs or Geonode yourself.
- **Local SEO.** Local rank tracking, heatmaps, citation management and Google Business Profile tools. Outflare doesn't manage local listings.
- **Agencies.** White label, client portals and branded dashboards on its higher plans. Outflare has no agency accounts or white label: a workspace is one company with several [brands](/product/brands/).
- **Bigger crawls.** Site audits from 50,000 pages on Starter to unlimited on Agency. OutflareBot stops at 10,000 pages per crawl.

## Where Outflare is different

- **Fixes behind an approval gate.** The [fixes list](/docs/guides/site-audits/#the-fixes-list) is shared by people and agents. [Fix autonomy](/docs/guides/fix-autonomy/) starts on copilot, and changes to redirects, canonicals, indexing, robots.txt or URLs always wait for a person.
- **Your accounts, your costs.** Every paid call is checked against a monthly budget before it runs and recorded in [Outcomes](/product/outcomes/) with its cost. Self-hosted, the data stays in your own Postgres.
- **Search Console by page, and experiments.** Up to 16 months of queries per page, drops, cannibalisation and indexing, and [SEO experiments](/product/experiments/) with matched control pages.
- **Competitors and a Changes feed.** A tracked list of competitors, their weekly numbers, what they publish and change, and keyword and link gaps. See [competitor tracking](/product/competitors/).
- **Search next to everything else.** [Website analytics](/product/analytics/), [paid media](/product/paid-media/), [customer records](/product/customer-data/) and [cold email](/product/cold-email/), all readable by agents through the [MCP server](/product/mcp/).

## How to use Outflare with or instead of Search Atlas

1. **Add your sites** under Site audits and prove ownership to crawl up to 10,000 pages.
2. **Connect Google Search Console** (read-only) for the [Keywords page](/docs/guides/keywords/).
3. **Connect DataForSEO or Ahrefs** for domain rating, backlinks and competitors, and DataForSEO or Geonode for rank tracking, each with a monthly budget.
4. **Choose fix autonomy** for your agents, or keep copilot and approve each fix yourself.

## What it costs

Self-hosting Outflare is free under AGPL-3.0, and Outflare Cloud is free during early access. DataForSEO, Ahrefs, Geonode and your AI provider bill you directly. Search Atlas bills its own plans and credits; see [its pricing page](https://searchatlas.com/pricing/).
