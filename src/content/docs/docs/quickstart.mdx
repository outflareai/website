This gets a local instance running with Docker Compose. If you'd rather not run it yourself, [sign up](https://app.outflare.ai/signup) for the hosted app instead. It's free during early access.

You need [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/get-docker/) with the Compose plugin, `git` and `openssl`.

<Steps>

1. **Clone the repository**

    ```sh
    git clone https://github.com/outflareai/outflare
    cd outflare
    ```

2. **Create your settings file**

    ```sh
    cp deploy/.env.example deploy/.env
    echo "OUTFLARE_SECRET_KEY=$(openssl rand -base64 32)" >> deploy/.env
    ```

    Open `deploy/.env` and change `POSTGRES_PASSWORD`. `OUTFLARE_SECRET_KEY` encrypts mailbox passwords at rest. Compose refuses to start without it.

    <Aside type="caution">
    Back up `OUTFLARE_SECRET_KEY` together with your database. If the key is lost, stored mailbox credentials cannot be read and every mailbox has to be imported again.
    </Aside>

3. **Start it**

    ```sh
    docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml up -d
    curl localhost:8080/readyz
    ```

    `readyz` returns `{"status":"ok"}` once the engine can reach Postgres. The engine runs its database migrations on start.

4. **Create the first user and workspace**

    Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080). The first visit runs setup: you enter your name, email, a password of at least 10 characters and a workspace name, and you become the workspace owner. Setup only works while the instance has no users. The app then opens on [Get started](/docs/guides/getting-started/): add your brand, invite your team and connect your tools, or skip straight past it.

5. **Import your mailboxes**

    Go to **Mailboxes → Import CSV** and upload the file your inbox reseller gave you. The only required columns are `email` and `password` (an app password). Each mailbox is signed in to over SMTP and IMAP, and each domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC are checked. See [Import mailboxes](/docs/guides/import-mailboxes/) for every column that is recognised.

6. **Create an API token and call the API**

    Go to **API tokens**, create a token with the `mailboxes.read` scope, and copy it. It starts with `oft_` and is shown once.

    ```sh
    export OUTFLARE_TOKEN=oft_...
    curl localhost:8080/v1/whoami -H "Authorization: Bearer $OUTFLARE_TOKEN"
    # → { "token": { "name": "…", "workspace": { "id": "…", "slug": "acme", "name": "Acme" }, "scopes": ["mailboxes.read"], … } }

    curl localhost:8080/v1/workspaces/acme/mailboxes \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $OUTFLARE_TOKEN"
    ```

    The workspace's slug is also in the app's address bar (`localhost:8080/acme/...`). Scripts should keep the ID, which never changes.

    Or use the command line client. It is the engine's own binary, and with only a server and a token it finds the workspace by itself:

    ```sh
    docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml exec \
      -e OUTFLARE_URL=http://localhost:8080 -e OUTFLARE_TOKEN engine /outflare list-mailboxes
    ```

    On any page of the app, the **API** button (top right) shows the call behind it as cURL, TypeScript and the matching MCP tool, with your IDs filled in.

</Steps>

## Finding your way

The sidebar starts with what you check every day, **Home, Inbox, Missions and Projects**, then one entry per module. Click a module to show its pages beneath it; one module is open at a time, and the one holding the page you are on opens by itself. Signing out forgets where you were, so you always come back to Home. Hover any page and click the pin to keep it under **Pinned** at the top (on a phone, pin from **Settings → All tools**).

To run Outflare from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, click **Connect your AI** at the top of the sidebar. It gives you one URL to add to your app; see [Connect your AI](/docs/agents/connect/).

- **CRM**: leads, lead lists and do not contact (people and companies who are never emailed).
- **Visitors**: companies that visit your website.
- **Outreach**: sequences, mailbox allocation and sending reports.
- **Deliverability**: infrastructure plans, mailboxes, domains, warm-up, sending servers, and teams and senders.
- **Lifecycle**: product and marketing email from your own provider: broadcasts, journeys, templates, topics and sent emails.
- **SEO**: site audits, fixes, experiments, keywords, rankings, backlinks, competitors and AI visibility.
- **Social**: your X and LinkedIn accounts, and Reddit.
- **Paid ads**: your ad accounts.
- **Analytics**: outcomes and cost per meeting, website analytics, tracking and links.
- **Playbooks**: ready-made recipes that use the modules together.
- **Company**: what Outflare knows about your business, your brands and your ideal customers.
- At the bottom, **Connectors** connects the services Outflare uses, and **Settings** holds the workspace and its members, AI apps, digests, the developer pages (API tokens, webhooks, logs, the audit log and **All tools**, which explains each tool and links to its guide) and your account.

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd>) to jump anywhere, or <kbd>G</kbd> then a letter, such as <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> for sequences.
