Outflare started as a self-hostable cold-email sequencer (v1, below, all shipped) and has grown into a go-to-market platform: website analytics, SEO, the inbox, leads and lists, email campaigns, lifecycle email, social, paid media, brands, customer records, and agents and missions across all of them. The [modules on the home page](/#modules) show what is live and what is being built.

What runs in the open-source engine and what is Outflare Cloud only is on [Open source and Cloud](/docs/project/open-source-and-cloud/).
