# Outflare 2026.11.28

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-28 04:26 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-28

## New

- **Open source** · Analytics: A brand can now keep each tracker click's full referrer, full user agent and IP address: turn each on under Tracker, Setup, and choose how many days to keep them. They show in the click log and as three new breakdowns in the Report. All three are off until you turn them on, and turning one off deletes what was kept. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A new alert, Campaign tracking stopped: when a tracker campaign keeps getting clicks but its page loads or its conversions, which came steadily before, have stopped, the owners and admins hear once, so a removed snippet or a silent conversion feed is caught the same day instead of in next week's report. (02:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Your site's own server can now report the ad clicks it sees arrive (`POST /v1/site/landings`). Tracking health then shows, per network, how many clicks reached your site but never ran the website script, and how many of them were crawlers. (04:26 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · SEO: Content: each brand can now set the languages its site is written in, a default language, and where each page type goes on the site (section, address pattern, layout to follow). A new page then gets its language and address by itself. Set it under Content settings on the Content page. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: New customer revenue can count a customer's signup from any of several events (`start_events`), so signups recorded under another event name, such as dates brought in from an earlier system, count too. The report page lets you add more than one signup event. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Brand search: a competitor whose name is a common word is no longer credited with every search for that word. Each competitor's domain is read beside its name, and a name searched far more than its domain, against the other names you follow, is marked "common word" and counted by an estimate. It needs five followed names to compare. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Tracker report: percentages now show two decimals (5.00%). Grouped by offer, the clicks that never went on to an offer are named "No offer reached" instead of "(none)", and an offer's lander views, lander clicks and lander CTR are left blank, because every click with an offer came through a lander click and the rate always read 100%. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Removing a partner now comes with your removal email picked and shown as that partner will get it. A program remembers its removal notice, and if you have none yet you can create one in the same dialog from a starting wording, then reword it under Templates. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Your server can now name the tracker click a person came from: send `properties.ofclk` with their `userId`, and the browser that landed with that click joins them, so a funnel follows one person from the ad click into your app. Funnels with a very busy first step no longer time out, and the events list refuses too large a page clearly. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads now shows what each Google campaign bids on: its custom goal and the conversion actions in it, or its standard goals. Conversions by type also counts an action in campaigns that don't bid on it, so you can see what Google credits beyond what bidding learns from. (04:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The conversion send log now says which click each conversion went with and where the click id came from. When an ad platform refuses a conversion because its click is too old, Outflare sends it once more without the click id, so it can still be matched on the person's hashed email or phone. (04:26 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels, trends and the other analytics pages no longer answer "internal error" when the database has no shared memory left for a parallel query: the question is asked again in a way that needs none. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Zeropark cost and targets: a traffic source that passes the campaign's long id ({long_campaign_id}) is now matched to Zeropark by it. Until now the short {campaign_id} number was used even then, which Zeropark refuses, so no cost or targets arrived. (02:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A click or sign-up tracked on an ad's landing page no longer starts a second visit without the ad's click id, so paid visits are counted once and keep their click id. In the paid ads report, a paused campaign whose customer paid again keeps its row, so the rows add up to the totals. (02:24 UTC)

## Security

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Security improvements to sign-in. (02:24 UTC)
