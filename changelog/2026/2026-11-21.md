# Outflare 2026.11.21

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-21 14:08 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-21

## New

- **Open source** · Partners: A new Partnerships menu puts working with other sites in one place, in the order the work happens: Find, Deals (every offer, link order and agreement on one board), Live, Affiliates, Payments (everything you owe in one list) and Results. Old Presence, Partners and Link order links still work. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Targets view: each page's target keyword with its Search Console position and change, a 12-week trend, tracked rank by country, other pages competing for it, and whether it is in the title and H1. Tiles count targets in the top 3, top 10 and 11–20 and filter the table. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions: see who names your brand or your tracked competitors in videos, podcasts, Reddit posts, forums and news, with the quote, who said it, their audience and a score, under Social → Mentions. It reads what your Reddit watches, news searches and competitor scans already found, and is off until you turn it on. (02:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partnership offers open as a full page and can start from a saved template. Each brand sets, on Partnerships → Payments, what happens when a paid link is removed after payment (restore or refund, a shrinking refund, or a warning), and every offer carries the rule it was sent with. (04:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Site owners can suggest changes to a partnership offer up to three times: the price, their page, the link text and the publish date. Where the link points, that it's a normal link, that it stays up and that nothing is paid before it's live stay fixed. Accept the latest round to make it the agreement. (04:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has holdout tests, on the Experiments tab: keep your ads out of a few countries for some weeks, and Outflare compares sign-ups there with everywhere else to say how many sign-ups the ads really add. Outflare only counts; you exclude the countries on your campaigns. (07:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has a new Creatives tab. Every ad is shown as a large preview and scored step by step (hook, hold, click, sign-up, pay) against your own usual rates, with a verdict: scale, keep, fix, stop or too early. Switch between gallery and list, filter by verdict, and pause an ad from its card. (08:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Keyword targets can carry the page's search intent, page type and a Markdown summary of what the page holds, and a whole keyword map imports from a CSV file. SEO overview, Targets gives every page a status (on target, cannibalized, wrong intent, missing content) with the page that ranks instead and suggested fixes, and lists up to 10,000 pages per site. (09:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Cannibalization view across a brand's sites: competing searches marked new, ongoing or gained a page against the 28 days before, resolved ones listed, same-language and cross-language searches apart, the page to keep (which you can now choose), split clicks, an estimated gain and the fix on the fixes list. (09:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Country pages view: pages made for a country, city or network, each with its target country, Search Console numbers in that country against the 28 days before, the tracked rank there and whether its keyword is tracked, grouped by template with median positions. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Technical view: issues from the latest site crawl counted in unique pages and templates (languages folded together), new and fixed since the crawl before, sorted by the search clicks of the pages they touch, with robots.txt changes, indexing, sitemaps and Core Web Vitals from real visits. (09:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Links view: referring domains week by week against your biggest tracked competitors, linking domains won and lost a week, links lost in the last 30 days with bought ones first, bought links whose live check failed or found another anchor or page, and the anchor profile of live links. (09:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Start here in Paid ads, Creatives: four steps to a brand's first ad with what is next, and starter skills (Problem first, One number, Before and after) added with one click, each with two prompts to split test. (12:52 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Tracker: traffic sources, affiliate networks and log rows now open in the page with a Back button and Cancel, instead of a side panel, and a log row shows names and dates in place of ids. The report's custom columns are edited in a card above the table, so each new column shows as you add it. Flows get the same Back button. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Tracker offers and landers: delete one or several at once, even while links use them (they come off those links, and reports keep their numbers). A row now opens its details in the page with a Back button and its own address instead of a side panel, with Edit, Duplicate and Delete on every row and a search box. The API and MCP get delete for both. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Each rule under Send conversions now shows the ID of the pixel, dataset or conversion it sends to, with a copy button, so you can check it against your site or the ad platform. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: "Everyone: our site already collects consent" now covers ad audiences as well as conversions. A brand that chose it has people added to its ad audiences in every country as consented to personalised ads, except anyone who withdrew. The confirmation in the app now says so. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Treg can now find a person's LinkedIn profile from their name and company's domain, and Blitz can read a company's size (its employee range and how many people work there on LinkedIn) from its LinkedIn page. Both run on your own connection and are billed to your own account. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Meta Ads: a conversion rule can now send InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, AddToCart, ViewContent and Search, so the steps before a purchase reach your pixel too. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads tables have a tick on every row and a "select all" in the header. Tick campaigns to pause, resume, change the budget of or remove them together; ad groups and ads to pause or resume them (and remove ads on Google Ads); keywords to remove them. One confirmation lists each one with its status and what it spent in the last 30 days, and removing follows the removal permission. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Removing an ad campaign, ad or keyword is now a permission: a role setting for people, the `ads.remove` scope for API tokens, and Off, Ask or On for each agent, optionally only for paused campaigns, disapproved ads or items that spent nothing. Refusals name the missing permission, and each removal is audited with a snapshot. (00:43 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The agent chat has a copy button under every reply, and on each code block and table in it. Each conversation shows a short ID in its header: copy it to share the chat, or copy or download the whole conversation as Markdown, with the API calls behind each reply. Tables whose cells contain a "|" no longer break apart. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Every error is now an event you can keep on your own server. Once someone is signed in, the web app sends its own events (an error shown, a crash, a page opened) to the server, so they reach your events workspace with or without PostHog. Errors and warnings the server only wrote to its log become events too, with where in the code they were logged. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads syncs how far people watch your Meta and TikTok video ads: plays that count as watched, the quarters reached, and from them a hook rate and a hold rate per campaign, ad set and ad. Turn the columns on in the tables under Columns. (05:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads, Settings, Tracking has a new dry run: would matching visits by network and device find more ad customers? It reads the visits you already have and says how many more people an ad would be credited with, and how often that guess is right. Nothing is collected, joined or changed. (06:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Every person now has a Lead source category, Inbound or Outbound, worked out from their Source, so you can filter People and the Sales queue by it. People who arrive through a Sales channel get Source Inbound and the channel as their Sub-source. (10:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions on YouTube: with YouTube on under Social → Mentions → Sources, each scan searches YouTube for videos naming your brand or a tracked competitor through your own web search connection, then reads each new video's captions, so a mention shows who made the video, its views, what was said around the name and the second it was said. (12:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Alerts: several alerts of the same kind at once now come as one email that lists them, instead of one email each. A lander or offer that stops answering is emailed to the person who added it, not every owner and admin. (13:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: The Partnerships tab that holds every offer, link order and agreement is now called Offers, so it isn't confused with Deals in CRM. Old links to the tab still open it. (13:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Publishing a query endpoint now writes its description for you, in plain English, from the query's filters, parameters, order and row limit, and keeps it up to date as you name the parameters. Change it as you like, or press Write it again. Endpoints show their description in the list, and the API's new describe call writes one without publishing. (13:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions now find podcast episodes that name your brand or a competitor, on your own free Podcast Index key, and leave out what brands post on their own channels. You choose per brand or competitor what Mentions listens for. (14:08 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: An extension's AI calls now report what they cost on a router such as OpenRouter (not 0), with cached input at its cheaper rate. Through OpenRouter, Anthropic's models now cache the stable start of a request, as a direct Claude connection already did. Extensions can list the models a workspace's AI connection offers. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Treg's profile lookup by name now takes its "no such person" answer as nobody found, instead of a failed request. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: The connect dialog keeps a long key and a long error inside the box. When Meta has switched off API access for the app a token was made for, Meta Ads now says so in plain words, with what to do next. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads: a sign-up whose last visit kept the ad's click id but lost its campaign (a sign-up page on another domain, or a click Google's click report leaves out) is now credited to the campaign of the earlier landing with the same click id. Sign-ups already recorded as "Campaign unknown" this way are filled in on the next run. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads tables set to "All" now list every campaign, ad group and ad, including paused ones that never spent. Before, a campaign with no spend in the dates shown was left out whatever the status filter said. The API takes `idle=true` on the campaign, ad and performance lists for the same. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Country and language names show in English, as the rest of the app, instead of following the browser's language. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Data, Catalog opens at once: the list of tables shows straight away and the row counts and dates fill in after, instead of the page waiting while every table is counted. Agents and the API can ask for the same with `numbers=false`. (01:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Importing a Sales conversation for a person who has since been merged into another now continues on the merged person's conversation, instead of being refused with a conflict on every later send. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: A request the caller closed before the answer (a closed tab, a page left mid-load) is now logged as `client_closed` instead of a 500 server error, so the request log and error counts show only real failures. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Searching Slack from a Sales conversation now says what to change when Slack refuses the search token (it must be a person's token with the search:read scope), instead of reporting Slack as unavailable. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The sidebar's badge check is no longer refused once a minute for people and tokens kept to some brands. They get no badges, which are about the whole workspace. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Object storage's start-up check no longer fails on its own file name, storage calls retry a failure that passes, AI visibility says once when a chosen connection is gone instead of failing every prompt, reading a long Slack sales post no longer cuts the AI's answer short, and crashes from browser extensions are no longer counted as the app's. (13:36 UTC)
