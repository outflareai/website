# Outflare 2026.11.19

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-19 12:34 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-19

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: The Sales queue's source and channel filters now take several at once: pick any two or three sources, or channels, and the list shows conversations from any of them. (09:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: People research can be told which roles to look for first, such as head of IT or CTO, so a company's functional heads are found before its other executives. (09:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales alerts: Outflare can post every new Sales conversation to a Slack channel you pick, with the person, their website, the source and the fields that matter, and keeps the message current with emojis for seen, assigned, talking, handed over and archived. You choose which sources are posted. New conversations also shows how many arrived since the last one you looked at. (10:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Tracker → Setup now shows the brand's tracking domain right on the page, so you can add and verify it without leaving Ads. It is the same domain your emails use, one per brand, and both Domains and Tracker → Setup now say so. (12:34 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales feed: a Slack post that arrives in the same moment as a channel's first read now shows up on the next read, a couple of minutes later, instead of waiting for the hourly check. (12:14 UTC)
