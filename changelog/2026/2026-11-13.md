# Outflare 2026.11.13

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-13 19:36 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-13

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales teams can work a shared new-lead queue, open full conversations, save personal email templates, keep notes through handoffs and confirm customers without recording a payment. Personal email sending requires each person's own consent. (11:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: People can send email from their own Gmail address through Outflare. It checks every recipient against your opt-outs first, keeps the sent email on the contact, and never sends twice. It uses your Google Workspace connection, once it is allowed to send. (14:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: ChatGPT Ads joins Paid ads: connect your ad account with your own OpenAI Advertiser API key to see spend, clicks and conversions per campaign, pause, resume and set budgets with your approval, get budget suggestions, track ChatGPT ad clicks (oppref), and send sign-ups and purchases back through OpenAI's Conversions API. (19:36 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Meetings read from your team's calendars now follow the same privacy as their email: private to the calendar's owner unless they share the contact, or team sharing shows those the AI check finds to be about work. Events marked private in Google are never saved. (12:33 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales queues show the prospect’s reply, source links and arrival time before you open a conversation. Managers start on All leads with status and teammate filters; Sales keeps the API panel closed until opened and gives browser tabs readable names. (12:41 UTC)
