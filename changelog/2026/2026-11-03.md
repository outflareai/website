# Outflare 2026.11.03

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-03 11:24 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-03

## New

- **Open source** · Customer records: Every source's value of a field is now kept: a record shows what your CRM, enrichment and your own data each say, with **Use this** to pick one. A person's edit wins over the other sources for 180 days by default, then the best other value takes over, as a change in the record's history. Change the days per brand or per field (or set them to never), and **Pin** an edit to keep it for good. (00:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Lists can now be built from a saved query: pick "A saved query" when making a smart list, and its members are the people in the query's `record_id` column, refreshed every 15 minutes (up to 50,000). If the query fails, the list keeps who it had and says why. Any list of people can also be saved as a static copy of who is in it now, from the list's menu ("Save a copy"). (00:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: A workspace setting lets only owners and admins edit people, companies and other records; everyone else can still see them. Imports and syncs keep running. (00:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Accounts: record every external account your brand's agent may act through (social profiles, community and review sites, partner platforms, sites with no API), how each signs in and what it depends on. Every use is tracked with its outcome, failures become incidents with an owner and a next try, and secrets live in a sealed vault that is never read back and that only a person can fill. (00:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Accounts: when a brand's agent meets MFA, a CAPTCHA, a passkey or a consent screen, it asks for a person, who takes the account over for a few minutes and hands it back with the identity checked. Offboarding archives a brand's accounts, empties their vault, revokes the agent's grants and lists what the owner still has to do at each platform. (01:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Browser workers: for sites with no API, a brand's agent queues a bounded task that runs in a real browser on your own servers, within the hosts, account and ceilings you set. It signs in from the vault without the model ever seeing a secret, asks a person at MFA or a CAPTCHA, and logs every step. (02:14 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Five more channels for your brand's agent: newsletters (broadcasts to your subscribers), website conversion (A/B tests, experiments, flags), competitor watch, and, through accounts and browser workers, listings and marketplaces and forums and launch sites. Each shows what it needs before it is ready. (02:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Company → Accounts: see your brand's external accounts and their health, fill the vault, resolve incidents, take an account over when a sign-in needs a person and hand it back, watch browser tasks, and offboard a brand's accounts. (08:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Google Ads: a sign-up on your own app now joins the visit whose ad click brought them, so the click is credited and sent back to Google; Google clicks know their campaign even without a tracking template, the Google tag's cookie fills in a lost click id, and Gmail addresses match better in enhanced conversions. (09:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Missions are retired. What each mission did stays readable on the Missions page, and changing one, or sending a change with the `Outflare-Mission` header, now answers that missions are retired. Agents make changes with their own token on the action layer, and playbooks run as agent runs on the Agents page. (09:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Google Ads: connecting no longer asks for a developer token, which Google retired in September 2026; signing in with Google is enough. (10:43 UTC)
