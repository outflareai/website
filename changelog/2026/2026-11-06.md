# Outflare 2026.11.06

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-06 16:03 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-06

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Your pages in AI visibility can check pages with your own AI model: it reads a page the way an AI answer does and says whether it answers up front, which claims are unclear, and what to change, with a score out of 100. Check competitors' cited pages the same way to compare. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility has a new Your pages tab: whether AI assistants can read, quote and trust your own pages, what to fix, and the pages AI answers already cite first. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Site audits now check whether AI answers can use your pages: quoting blocked (nosnippet, short max-snippet, Bing noarchive), questions with no direct answer, articles without an author or date or not updated in two years, and structured data that doesn't match the page. Googlebot, Bingbot and more AI crawlers join the AI crawler list. (14:26 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Customer records: Brands no longer share any people, companies, leads, lists, email topics, sequences, support conversations or websites: older items saved with no brand now belong to the first brand, and news topics and fix guidelines are copied into every brand. (08:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: With a brand agent, the Agents page opens straight to its chat. Waiting changes, history, what agents may do and API tokens are in one "Approvals and history" menu instead of a row of tabs. (10:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence is now its own item in the menu. Found and Opportunities are tables you can sort by domain rating, traffic, AI citations or competitors, filter, and scroll through without pages. Opportunities can show one row per site, and both can hide your competitors' own sites. (10:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Website tracking keys, visits, customer events, webhooks and support addresses saved before they had a brand now belong to one brand, so no brand sees another's. (10:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: A competitor's placements and Presence's Where competitors appear now list the most valuable links first, not the biggest sites. Each shows how it's got (can't buy, earned, paid or free), a 0 to 100 value with reasons, and a filter. (10:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: A/B tests: the visual editor has a bar with a version switcher, undo and redo, and a saved status, plus tabs for the page's parts and the version's changes. Double-click text to type on the page, drag a part to move it, and ask your AI model to change the page or the part you picked, then keep or undo what it wrote. (11:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility uses the Competitors page as its one competitor list. Brands that AI answers name next to you wait there for a Yes or No, a competitor added in the AI visibility setup goes there too, and one you said No to stops being suggested. (11:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: A sending domain, mailbox, lead list or sequence can no longer be taken out of a brand to be shared: move it by adding it to the brand it belongs to. (11:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: A/B tests: describe a change to a no-code test's page, such as "add a line about premium plans and a Try it button under the header", and your AI model writes real changes to that page for you to keep. A run that takes too long now stops with a clear reason, and you can stop one yourself. (11:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence Opportunities shows how a link on each page is got (can't buy, earned, paid or free to get), and you can filter by it. (11:32 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Brands that AI answers name next to you are matched to their websites by themselves. Ones already on your competitor list are recognised by name, and the rest go to the Competitors page to say Yes or No, asking "Is this their website?" when the match isn't certain. (11:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: The sidebar shows how many agent changes wait for your yes or no next to Agents, for the brand you're in, so you see it from any page. The workspace stats (`GET /v1/workspaces/{ws}/stats`) return the same number as `actions_waiting`. (12:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Each competitor's search data is now read at most once a month on an SEO connection, however it is asked for (the weekly snapshot, Refresh all competitors, or one competitor's Read), so many tracked competitors don't spend your Ahrefs or DataForSEO budget every week. Change it under "Read each competitor at most once every" in the SEO tool's settings. (12:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Developer setup now shows when payments sent from your server can't be matched for certain: orders without an order_id, payments joined by a guess or with different amounts both ways, ids reused for renewals, retries under new ids, amounts sent in dollars instead of cents, and payments going quiet. Payments saved twice before can be merged in one click. (12:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence Opportunities suggests the English version of a page instead of its translations, so a site with /fr/ and /de/ copies shows up once, on its English page. (13:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO sites, audits, keywords, competitors, backlinks, fixes and deploys now belong to one brand each, under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`. The old workspace routes answer 410 and name the route to call instead. The SEO overview shows one brand, or each brand in turn when all brands are picked. (13:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The AI visibility Sources table now shows each page's address first and its site next, and leaves out ChatGPT's own image links. Click a page to see when answers first cited it, the site's ratings, the links to it, the questions it answers, and whether it links to or only names you and each competitor. (16:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility now sorts the brands AI answers name: a product of a listed competitor ("Zyte API") counts as that competitor, tools that aren't competitors go under "Named, not competitors", and every name it isn't sure of shows its best guess at the website. Each name is sorted once and remembered. (16:03 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · SEO: Every tracked competitor now gets its search numbers (Domain Rating, keywords, visits, linking sites) each week, not just the first 50, within your SEO connection's budget. A competitor your provider can't read no longer stops the rest, and domain ages that didn't load are tried again within hours. (09:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility counts every competitor on the brand's list, however long, so the setup and every run include them all; before, the list stopped at 25. (09:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: A payment or refund sent as an order event and as a transaction at the same moment is now kept once, with its person. Before, it could show twice on the Transactions page, and Developer setup could warn about payments with no person. (10:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: When your product sends a verified email that only one record holds unverified, such as an import, a lead or an unsubscribe with no user id or other email of its own, that record is now merged into the person instead of being listed as a possible duplicate. Its consent and opt-outs come with it. (10:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Refresh all competitors now reads search numbers even right after Ahrefs refused one report your plan doesn't include, which used to mark the whole connection broken. The page also says how many competitors have their search numbers, domain age and site read so far. (11:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The indexability report lists every page in a site's sitemaps, including those past the crawl's page limit, and shows each page's full address as a link that opens it. (15:02 UTC)
