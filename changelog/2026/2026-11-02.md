# Outflare 2026.11.02

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-02 11:00 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-02

## New

- **Open source** · Customer records: Products and lifecycle stages: list the products each brand sells, and Outflare keeps every company and person in a stage per product (trial, active, at risk, churned), so a customer who cancels one product but keeps another is never treated as churned. Each move is an event journeys can start on. Find it under People → Products. (00:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Agent actions: turn on the action layer for an API token and set what that agent may change on its own. Everything else waits for a person, who approves changes one at a time or in batches. Every change the agent made is kept. (01:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Broadcasts and sequences can hold back a share of their audience on purpose, and then show what they added: who converted and what they paid in the 30 days after, against the people held back, and whether the difference is real. Set it when you draft a broadcast or in a sequence's settings. (03:33 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Slack (knowledge): a read-only Slack connection for the support assistant, which reads only the channels you pick to find answers your team already gave. It is separate from the Slack connection that posts alerts. (04:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Support assistant: Outflare answers customers' chats and emails in a few short sentences from your approved knowledge, asks one question back, or hands the conversation to your team, drafting for your team to send. When it can't answer, it looks on your website and in the Slack channels you pick, and suggests the answer in Knowledge for you to approve. (04:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Agents page: see what your agents changed, approve what waits for you one at a time or in a batch, let changes like one you approve run from then on, and set what each agent may do on its own. Mark a token as an agent under API tokens. (04:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A playbook can run as an agent run: Outflare's agent carries it out in turns on the action layer, and the mode you choose (copilot, autonomous with a spend cap, or dry run) sets what it may change on its own. Its changes show on the Agents page, and you write to the run and it answers in its next turn. (05:36 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Inbox: Support assistant Auto mode on Outflare Cloud: switch it on per channel in Support settings and the assistant answers customers' chats and emails on its own when every check passes, signed as an AI assistant, and hands everything else to your team. (08:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Smart lists keep their history and can hold companies: see who joined and left a list and when (also on each person's timeline), make a list of companies from their fields, events and product stage, and exclude a list of companies to leave out everyone who works there. Lists can now pick people by lifecycle stage per product. (08:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Extensions can set a quota that decides whether a workspace may add more brands, mailboxes, tracking domains or records, or send more email. Refused additions answer `402 quota_exceeded`; refused emails wait and try again. Self-hosted servers have no quota and nothing changes. (10:05 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Agent actions: approve a pattern once. When you approve an agent's held change, you can let changes like it run on their own from then on, within a daily limit. Held, approved and rejected agent changes can also go to webhooks. (03:33 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A grant that lets an agent run changes on its own can cap what those changes spend on your own accounts a day and a month. Spend booked while a change runs is linked to it, each change shows what it spent, and once the day's or month's spend plus what the next change is likely to cost would pass the cap, changes wait for a person. (04:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Start a playbook from the app and Outflare's agent runs it: pick copilot, autonomous with a budget, or a dry run. The Agents page's Runs view shows each run's turns and changes, lets you write to it, and pauses or stops it; an automation's last run opens there too. (06:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A grant that lets an agent run paid ads changes on its own gets spend caps of $50 a day and $500 a month unless you set your own, so a first autonomous grant on ad spend can't surprise you. (07:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Analytics → Channels can count only people who signed in on your own product, leaving out browsers known only from a click on an email link, and each credited visit now says whether it was tied to the person by a sign-in or an email click. (08:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: New `agents.read` and `agents.run` scopes cover Outflare's agent: its runs, the playbooks it runs and writing to a run. Tokens and roles with the mission scopes keep working as before while missions are retired. Extensions can start agent runs for the caller. (09:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Website chat: visitors can say whether the support assistant's answer helped. "No" hands the chat to your team right away, and the chat shows when the assistant is typing a reply. (10:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: The link and PR autopilot pages open an autopilot's agent run on the Agents page, where you write to it, pause or stop it; autopilots started earlier still open their mission. (10:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: The support assistant's report in Support settings now shows how often it answered, left conversations to your team, had drafts sent as written and was rated helpful, against the 30 days before, with the commonest reasons it handed off. You're alerted once a day when answers it found wait for review, and the digest has a Support section. (10:32 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The analytics summary answers people and tokens kept to some brands with their brands' sites, like the other analytics reports. (10:58 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads: campaigns, keywords and search terms now include today's numbers for ad accounts in time zones ahead of UTC, such as Europe, late in the UTC day. (08:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: The one-click DNS approval ("Approve in Cloudflare") now finds a domain's DNS provider on hosted installs whose resolver didn't answer cleanly for a subdomain, where it answered "lookup failed" and offered only the API connection. (10:45 UTC)
