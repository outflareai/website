# Outflare 2026.11.16

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-16 16:10 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-16

## New

- **Open source** · Paid ads: TikTok Ads now shows what is below each campaign: its ad groups and ads, with spend, clicks and conversions per day, each ad's text and landing page, and whether its link carries your tracking template. You can pause and resume an ad group or an ad from a campaign's page, and set the daily budget of an ad group that has its own. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: On Google Ads campaigns that bid by hand (manual CPC), you can now set an ad group's bid and a keyword's bid from Outflare. Use Set bid in the Manage menu on a campaign's page; the Changes tab shows the bid it replaced. The API and MCP can also make a change that may only lower a bid. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Customer accounts: if your product has workspaces or teams, they are now their own records (Accounts), with members and their roles, separate from People and Companies. Each brand starts with smart defaults for its business type (who it sells to, whether it has accounts) and can change them. A person can also have a preferred email that sends go to. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Sales opportunity on a support conversation hands it to your sales team in one step: it moves there with a note, you keep it under Watching, and the customer's owner gets it in Needs attention. When your AI spots an opportunity in a conversation, the Inbox says so and offers the hand-over. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Online and Away in the Inbox: say when you are taking chats, and a new website chat goes to someone online, moves to the next person after 30 seconds with no answer, and carries on by email when nobody is free or the visitor has left. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Saved replies: write an answer once and drop it into any reply from the Saved replies menu, in support conversations and in replies to outreach, with the person's first name and your own filled in. Replies to outreach also get a Draft with AI button that writes an answer from your knowledge for you to edit. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Alerts can now post to Telegram: connect your own Telegram bot under Connectors, then pick a chat, group or channel for each kind of alert under Settings → Alerts, beside its Slack channel and email. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Destinations: send your product events on to a webhook, Slack, Google Analytics 4 or HubSpot as they arrive, each on your own connection, with a log of what was sent, skipped or refused. Find it under Events. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Ask a question: type what you want to know at the top of Dashboards and get a chart you can open in the builder or save. It runs on your own AI model and sees only event names and descriptions, never people or their data. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Libraries for sending product events from your servers: Node, Python, Go, Ruby and PHP. Each has identify, track, page, screen, group and alias, sends in batches in the background and retries when the server is busy. (08:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Outflare for Chrome: a side panel that shows what you know about the person an email is from in Gmail, or the company whose website you are on, and adds the ones you don't know with a note or a to-do. The toolbar icon turns green on a company you know and red on a do-not-contact site, checked inside the browser. For now it is loaded from the `extension/` folder. (08:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Libraries for sending screens and events from mobile apps: iOS, Android, React Native and Flutter. Apps send with a public site key that has no origins, keep events on the phone while it is offline, and say which app version and phone each event came from. (08:33 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Snapchat Ads and Microsoft Advertising now show what is below each campaign: ad squads or ad groups and their ads, with spend, clicks and conversions per day, each ad's headline and landing page, and whether its link carries your tracking template. They are read only for now; pause, resume and budgets stay at the campaign level on these two. (09:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Destinations: write your product events to a table in your own BigQuery or Snowflake as they arrive, and press Send a test event on any destination to see it work before real events come in. (09:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Pinterest Ads and Reddit Ads now show what is below each campaign: ad groups and their ads, with spend, clicks and conversions per day, each ad's landing page, and whether its link carries your tracking template. They are read only for now; pause, resume and budgets stay at the campaign level on these two. (09:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracker: enter what a campaign cost by hand. For a network whose cost Outflare can't read, Tracker → Logs → Cost updates takes a campaign, the days and the amount (or a CSV of them) and shares it out evenly over the campaign's clicks, so every report has cost and the total is exactly what you entered. (09:54 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracking plans: in the event dictionary, mark the properties every event must carry, and see which events arrive without them or with properties you never listed. Nothing is dropped. (10:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: Recommended donors. Pick a page to link to and the catalog ranks donors for it, with the reasons: topic, language, DR, organic traffic, price per 1,000 visits and links to the competitors that rank for the page, minus link farms and donors already used. Add the best to a quote request in one click; nothing is ordered on its own. (11:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Taboola campaigns now show their ads: each item with its headline, picture, landing page, whether Taboola approved it, and its spend, clicks and conversions per day. Taboola has no ad groups, so the ads sit directly in the campaign. They are read only for now. (11:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Session recordings and heatmaps: watch a visit to your website back as the visitor saw it, and see where each page is clicked and how far down it is scrolled. Both come from the snippet you already have, are off until you turn them on under Analytics → Recordings, and never record what a visitor types. Recordings need file storage on the server. (11:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: an order now shows the conversation with its donor: what Outreach sent to the site or its intermediary, their replies with the Inbox label, and the prices written in those replies, so the agreed price is one glance away. (11:33 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Outbrain and LinkedIn campaigns now show their ads, with spend, clicks and conversions per day: Outbrain's promoted links with their title, landing page and whether Outbrain approved them, and LinkedIn's creatives with their name, status and review. Neither platform has ad groups, so the ads sit directly in the campaign. They are read only for now. (11:33 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: label the donors you most want links from as Best link. The catalog shows them first, can show only them, and the label can be set from a cell's right-click menu, the donor card or a best_link CSV column. (11:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: record a link you already bought in one form (article, page, anchors, price and currency), or import past orders from a CSV. Outflare adds the site to the catalog, opens the order, saves the price, approves it if you are the brand's approver and records the publication, which starts the live-link check; Paid stays a separate step. (12:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders catalog: sites whose metrics look like a PBN (high DR with almost no traffic or keywords, few referring subnets, thousands of outgoing links) get a "Likely PBN?" badge with the reasons, a filter to review them, and Flag as PBN in the right-click menu and on the donor card. It only suggests: the PBN flag stays yours to set. (12:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders catalog: import donors from an Excel (.xlsx) file or a Google Sheet link, not only CSV. A sheet shared as "Anyone with the link" or published to the web is read without connecting Google, then imported in batches with the same column matching and intermediary for every row. (13:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboards: pin a saved report as a tile. A report saved on Tracker → Report (tracker clicks or site visits) can sit on a dashboard beside your charts, showing its top rows and totals. It is for your signed-in team: on a shared link the tile shows only the report's name. (14:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Trends and dashboards can now show a brand's search and link-building numbers: Search Console, Bing and Google Analytics clicks, impressions, CTR, position and visitors; live, new and lost referring domains; and link-order spend, commitments, publications and payments. They read data Outflare already stores, so a dashboard can put organic search next to sign-ups. (16:10 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Archive a Sales conversation directly from the queue, with Undo for accidental clicks. Archived conversations keep their messages and notes, and every archive and reopen remains recorded with who made the change. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Discovery view now has the full lane board from your connected Lead Portal: filter the search lanes by state, page through them, and open one to see how it searches, why it was proposed, what it learned and what it found. Agents can read it with the get_sales_discovery_lanes tool. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: A teammate you loop in on a conversation is now told. Their Watching view shows how many are new, the Inbox shows a message with an Open button, and if they haven't opened it after five minutes they get one email with a link. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: The Inbox now tells you when something new arrives. New chats and messages show up on their own with an Open button, and a "Sound and notifications" switch plays a chime and shows a browser notification when the Inbox is in the background. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: The Inbox warns you when a teammate has the same conversation open, or is writing a reply in it, so two people don't answer the same customer. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: One People list: everyone, cold contacts included, with a Source (Cold, Inbound, Event, Sign-up, Invited, Customer) set once when they are added and a Marketing email yes/no column. Cold contacts get no marketing email until they opt in. Outreach's Leads is now the Cold filter, and you can add people to a sequence straight from the list. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Right-click a conversation in the Inbox (or use its More button) to assign it to anyone, move it to a team or loop a teammate in. The same three are on the bar that shows when you tick several. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Work a conversation as a team in the Inbox. Move a reply to a team, loop a teammate in so it shows under their new Watching view, and add private notes that only your team sees, on replies to outreach as well as support conversations. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Inbox rows have quick actions: right-click a conversation (or use its More button) to open it in a new tab, mark it unread, assign it to yourself, snooze it, close it or copy its link. Tick several rows to act on them together, and undo a close, snooze or assignment from the message that confirms it. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: The Inbox now shows support conversations and replies to your outreach in one place: pick a support folder or a sales view in the views column, and the list and the conversation open beside it. Saved views of replies sit in the same column. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Mailbox health now counts the emails, replies and bounces your connected Instantly or Smartlead account sends from each mailbox, read from your own account about hourly. A new endpoint and agent tool give one mailbox's activity by day. Warm-up mail is never counted. (08:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: The iOS library now installs in Xcode from the repository's address (File, Add Package Dependencies), and every library has the same version as the Outflare release it comes with, so you can tell at a glance which release a library belongs to. (09:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracker logs: a postback to a traffic source can be sent again. **Send again** on a row of Postbacks out (or `POST .../traffic-sources/{id}/postbacks/{send}/resend`, MCP `resend_traffic_source_postback`) queues it with the status and payout it had, to the source's postback URL as it is now, so a send that failed on a wrong URL goes through once you fix the URL. (09:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Sales reporting now shows discovery searches, tool calls, retries and recorded costs from a connected Lead Portal. Compare lane yield and inspect screening and grading evidence, with missing prices clearly marked. (10:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders catalog: filter by category, subcategory, audience country, article type, DR, price and traffic ranges, referring domains, keywords, outgoing domains, date added, and price per 1,000 visits. A "Sort by" list adds best value, price, DR, traffic, referring domains, keywords and newest first, and Reset clears everything. (10:28 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: On the Agents page, each change in a task now says what became of it, with Allow once, Always allow and Don't allow on the row. The chat opens a new conversation unless the latest is still going, and a dropdown in its header switches conversations. (10:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: the live-link check now compares the link text on the donor page with the ordered primary and second anchors, and the Checks tab marks it as matching, partly matching or not the ordered anchor. It is a warning only and never marks a link lost. (11:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracker report: three new columns for landers. Lander views (clicks sent to a lander), lander clicks (those that went on to an offer) and lander CTR show for every grouping, sort, work in custom columns and are in the CSV, so you can compare landers by how many visitors they pass on. (11:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Pause and resume a single ad on Taboola, Outbrain and Reddit (and a Reddit ad group) from the campaign's page, the API or MCP, leaving the campaign and its other ads running. An agent's pause follows its grant; a resume always waits for a person. (11:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Recordings and heatmaps have more settings: record only the pages you list or leave some out, drop recordings of very short visits, and cap how many visits are recorded a day. A heatmap whose page can't be shown yet now says why, with a button to turn recordings on. (12:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Delete recordings in bulk: tick several and delete them, or delete every recording older than a number of days. A page's heatmap, or every heatmap, can be cleared to start counting again, such as after a redesign. (13:39 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: Agent conversations can be pinned to the top of the list or archived out of it from each conversation's ⋯ menu, and the list no longer scrolls sideways. The task count next to the agent's status opens each task with where it stands. (13:39 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales conversations now stay in step with the Inbox and the CRM. Taking a conversation makes you the person's Owner, confirming a customer wins their deal, hand-offs from support show with New conversations, and the Inbox links your Sales queues. Sales screens say conversation, person or company in place of "lead". (14:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Recordings start playing when opened and mark every click, typing, new page and pause on the progress bar; the list names known visitors and filters by website. Heatmaps take their own picture of each page, so they work without recordings, show only the busiest areas by default and list the ten most clicked spots by name. (14:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Adding a rule under Send conversions back now lets you search your own events by name instead of typing one from memory, picks the amount for you (a payment sends its own amount), and works out whether the number is in dollars or cents. Rules can now be edited, and a payment rule with no value is fixed in one click. (15:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Recordings and heatmap pictures hide API keys, tokens and passwords shown on a page, in the visitor's browser before anything is sent: known key formats, user:password@host addresses, values after labels such as "API key" or "Password", and long runs of mixed letters and digits. (15:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Recording settings have "Hide your own keys": type a made-up key that looks like the ones your product shows, and everything with that shape is hidden in recordings and heatmap pictures. Only the shape is kept. (15:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A Google Workspace connection can now create new users in your own tenant, when you turn on "Allow creating users" and authorize the Directory user scope. Each new user takes one of your own licences. (15:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: On Website analytics, Search, each page and search query now opens a page of its own: a page shows the searches that showed it, and a search shows the pages it showed, each with clicks, impressions, click-through and average position over the period you picked. Works with Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and through the API and MCP with `page` and `keyword` on the report. (16:10 UTC)
