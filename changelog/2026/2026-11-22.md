# Outflare 2026.11.22

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-22 22:36 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-22

## New

- **Open source** · Analytics: Lifetime value: a new Analytics page shows what a customer is worth, from their first payment on, net of refunds. Split it by first product, first channel or any customer field, follow each month's new customers in a cohort table, save it and put it on a dashboard. Agents run it with `run_lifetime_value`. (10:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Distribution: a new Analytics page shows how customers are spread by what they paid, how often they pay or the days between payments, with the median beside the average. Split it, click a range to see who is in it, save it or put it on a dashboard. Agents use `run_distribution`. (13:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Retention has a Money view: of the customers who first paid in a month, see what the same customers paid in each month after, as a share of their first month, as amounts or per starting customer. Split it, save it or put it on a dashboard. Agents read it as `kept` from `run_lifetime_value`. (14:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Scenarios: a new Analytics page for what-ifs. Put inputs such as sign-ups or a price on sliders, write plain formulas between them, and see the forecast month by month against things as they are today. An input can read today's value from a saved report. Save one to a dashboard. Agents use `run_scenario`. (15:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Balances: a new Analytics page shows what customers have paid for and not used yet, such as wallet money or credits. Say what goes in and what comes out, then see what is held now, how much sits untouched, the movement month by month and who holds the most. Agents use `run_balance`. (15:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Outflare now warns you when a product event that came in most days stops arriving: Brand setup lists it under Needs attention, and it is sent as an alert, so you hear about a broken sender before your funnels and reports read too low. (15:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Write the first email to a person whose Sales conversation arrived from one of your Sales channels, right on the conversation page: add a subject, use your templates or Personalize with AI (which reads what they sent in the form), and send it from your own connected mailbox. Their answer comes back into the same conversation. (19:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales sequences: write up to five emails with day gaps (or let AI draft them), check each one for the person, and Outflare sends them from your own mailbox in the same thread until they reply. Sales managers set the sending days, hours and daily cap in Sales settings. (19:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Saved reports can carry tags. Give a dashboard, funnel, trend or any other saved report a few words of your own, such as a launch or a weekly meeting, then narrow All reports to a tag with one click. The API and the MCP tools take `tags` when saving or changing a report. (21:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Connect any remote MCP server to your workspace, mark each of its tools as a read or a change, and agents can use it: reads run at once, changes wait for a person, and a new Read-only switch on any connection makes sure nothing ever changes on the other side. (21:59 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Settings now has a search box: type a word such as "passkey" or "webhook" and open the exact setting, including settings that live on a tool's own page, such as recordings. The Settings menu is sorted by who a setting belongs to: Brand, Workspace or You. (09:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: People, Companies and Deals now have one toolbar row: search, Filters, your saved view and Save view, then the count, table or board, sort, Columns and export. Show (Active, Held for review, Archived), Marketing email and Company moved inside Filters, and the Filters button counts them when they are set. (09:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Home is calmer. One list, For you today, holds what needs you, and now also numbers that crossed an alert you set on them (with a button that opens the report) and the last day's alerts, each with a button that opens the thing. Jump back in leads to what you had open last. Channel, PR, outreach and mailbox numbers are folded under More numbers. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Search (⌘K or Ctrl K) now finds more than pages: type a name and it lists matching people, companies, saved reports and dashboards, and settings down to a part of a settings page, and opens the one you pick. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Small things that now work the same everywhere. Rest on a page in the menu and a card says what it is for, with something to ask the agent there. Rest on any id, key or code and a copy button shows at its end. A number shown against the period before now also says what it was then. And Home has one Needs setup list for connections to fix and set-up steps left, each with a button that goes there. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Analytics has an All reports page: every saved dashboard, funnel, trend, retention and paths question and saved Tracker report in one list. Search it, narrow it by type or owner, see when each last changed and when you last opened it, and start a new one from the same place. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Brand profile: a fact that holds a whole list can now be split into separate facts, so each line can be confirmed, retired or allowed for customers on its own. Lists inside a fact now show as lists, in the overview and in each section. (10:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboards: a saved query can be a tile. Add tile lists the brand's saved queries, and the tile shows the query's chart or its rows as a table, so any list or number you can ask for sits beside your charts. Agents add one with `add_dashboard_tile` and `saved_query_id`. (12:46 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Creator deals: a payment you record on a deal is now also kept as a paid fee under the creator on Partnerships, Payments and in your costs, so what you paid a creator is in one place. Outflare still moves no money. (14:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Every screen has a link of its own: pages sit under their area (for example /tracker/campaigns), and a page's tabs, an item's tabs and its layout are part of the link, so a pasted link opens exactly the screen you were on. A list's search and sorting travel in the link too. Links you saved before still open. (16:07 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Everything else: On Outflare Cloud, the agent's open task, Agent Mail's tabs and the outreach mailboxes' tabs each have a link of their own, so a pasted link opens exactly that screen. Links you saved before still open. (16:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: More screens have a link of their own: the Team page's org chart, the log entry open in Logs, the record open in Coverage with its tab, and the prospect open in Link building. Links you saved before still open. (17:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales conversations: the queue's Source and Channel rows are one From row (apps, channels and Email reply, any of them), Unassigned and Assigned to me show how many new conversations wait in them, a Slack search that isn't set up no longer shows reps an error they can't fix, and the menu's Recents keeps only your latest Sales conversation. (19:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales conversations hold mail from each rep's own mailbox, so a workspace admin no longer sees every conversation just by being an admin. The workspace owner and Sales managers still do, and only the owner can make someone a Sales manager. (20:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Creator deals: Outflare now checks the link for you. Once a week it reads each video's description through your own page reading connection and marks the link in place or missing, and alerts you when a link that was there is gone. "Check now" on a deal does it straight away. A video you mark by hand is left as you set it. (21:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Fewer false errors: a pipeline stage that will retry after a provider blip, a desktop app reporting the same expired sign-in again, a sequencer campaign that stays not ready and a donor site that doesn't answer are each recorded once as what they are, not as repeated errors. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales: choose who the first email goes to: the person's main address, another of their addresses, or one you type. Every recipient rule still applies, the answer lands in the same conversation, and later replies and sequence emails go to the same address. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales: the number beside New conversations now counts what arrived since you last opened it, and clears when you open it. On the page, each source in the From row shows how many of its conversations are new since your last visit, and the filter for conversations that began from a campaign reply is named after the sequencer they came from. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Creator deals: each video you add to a deal now also appears in Presence as a placement of your brand, and Presence's page check is what reads it for the link. The deal's screen is the same; you can change how often a video is read on its placement. (22:13 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Paid ads: The Paid ads Overview and Campaigns tables no longer show the notes written for the API (about micros and field names) under the numbers. On a brand's Agents page, Rules and the switches for removing ads now list only the agents that can act for that brand. (15:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions now also clear videos from a brand's or competitor's own channel that earlier scans kept, and recognise channels such as "Northpeak Official" or "@northpeakhq" as the company's own. (19:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: On Revenue, an account with no name now shows as "No name" and still opens the account, in Spending less, Spending more, Renewals and Revenue at risk. Before, the line had nothing to click. (21:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Fewer false alarms: an AI visibility run whose model refuses the account's key now records it once per run instead of once per sample; a busy rate limit when syncing guard rules is recorded as a refusal, not an error; an ad suggestions signal read that failed for a passing reason is tried again on the next run; and the MCP server keeps a session open while its event stream is still connected. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The Sales queue no longer fails to load when one conversation's latest message or note can't be read: that row shows no preview and the rest of the queue loads. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: SQL queries no longer fail now and then right after a restart, when several queries for the same workspace start at once. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Errors: when grouping is improved and two error issues become one, a status you set on either of them (ignored, resolved) now always carries over. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Website tracking keeps the last events and recording a visitor sends as they leave a page, and heatmaps take pages with very long addresses. Looking up a record by an identity that isn't valid text now answers with a clear error, and the app no longer stops on a key press from a browser extension or autofill, or keeps calling the server while offline. (21:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Fewer false alarms: a write that succeeded on its second try is no longer a warning, waiting agent changes alert only when about to expire, a failed Sales send alerts once, a failed company lookup isn't retried on every page, and error events name the route. (22:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Search Console: page-by-page syncs of large sites with long addresses no longer fail every day; rows are read in smaller pages. (22:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Website and app tracking: a full batch of 20 events with large properties is no longer refused as too big (the limit is now 512 KB a request), and a session recording or heatmap picture of a heavy page is split into parts the server takes, instead of the whole part being lost. (22:36 UTC)
