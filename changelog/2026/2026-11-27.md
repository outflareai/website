# Outflare 2026.11.27

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-27 07:11 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-27

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Live flow has a second view, Sales process: the companies open for the team to contact and the conversations that came in, joining into taken, handed to an account manager and customer, each number one click from its conversations, and By person with what each teammate did. Its numbers are the Team report's for the same period. (01:13 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: A sequence can now be set to "Only these senders". Outflare then never fills a shortage with someone else's mailbox, and one attached by hand in the sequencer shows under Needs attention with a one-click Detach, which now also works on a running EmailBison campaign. (02:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Content: a brief now holds what a page was written to: who it is for, its outline, the questions it answers, what the pages that rank cover, and the facts it may state, each with its source. Questions the writer could not answer show above the text before you approve. A page can also list its other language versions, so your site can print hreflang. (07:11 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Customer records: A choice field such as Seniority no longer looks empty when it holds a value that isn't in its list: it shows "Senior · not in the list" so you can pick the right option. Values written as an option's name or a common other word ("Head of", "C-level", "founder") are saved as that option, and lists your team made get "Add option" right in the picker. (00:07 UTC)
