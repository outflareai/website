# Outflare 2026.11.27

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-27 12:57 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-27

## New

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Feedback inside Outflare: send a note about any page with a screenshot (paste, drop, choose or capture the page), kept private or shared as a thread in your team's Slack, whose replies show under it. Settings, Feedback lists every item with its status: New, Planned, Doing, Done or Won't do. (07:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: My board: Sales conversations open as a board of your own columns. Drag a card, or use Move to…, to set its stage; share chosen columns with colleagues, who see the cards and can add their own. Dropping a card on Handed to account manager or Lost hands it over or archives it. (09:17 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Live flow has a second view, Sales process: the companies open for the team to contact and the conversations that came in, joining into taken, handed to an account manager and customer, each number one click from its conversations, and By person with what each teammate did. Its numbers are the Team report's for the same period. (01:13 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: A sequence can now be set to "Only these senders". Outflare then never fills a shortage with someone else's mailbox, and one attached by hand in the sequencer shows under Needs attention with a one-click Detach, which now also works on a running EmailBison campaign. (02:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Content: a brief now holds what a page was written to: who it is for, its outline, the questions it answers, what the pages that rank cover, and the facts it may state, each with its source. Questions the writer could not answer show above the text before you approve. A page can also list its other language versions, so your site can print hreflang. (07:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Live flow now starts the email branch with Emails found: every address found for the period's companies, split into first email, follow-ups, support@ and not sent yet, which add up to it. When discovery is paused, a line above the tree says why. (08:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The number beside New conversations counts the conversations nobody has taken, and stays live as you work. Sales > Team lists at the top who has no Sales role yet, each with a role picker. (08:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: On a person's Sales view, anyone with a Sales role can write in a plain box under the thread, with templates as one button beside Send. Without a mailbox the box asks you to connect one, and on a colleague's conversation one line says who owns it, with Take over while it is new. (08:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales managers connect their own mailbox and can write in any Sales conversation, a colleague's too, which stays the colleague's. Anyone with a Sales role can write in a new conversation nobody has taken, and sending takes it. A reply from your own mailbox now works on a new conversation whose thread came through EmailBison and starts it, without sending the introduction first. (08:49 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Where a server runs a daily review of the searches, the Search terms card says when it last ran and what came of it, with a button to run it now. (09:53 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: New conversations now lists only people who showed interest: a reply labelled interested, meeting booked or question, or a hand-over post. Everyone else waiting, such as a referral or a signup posted in Slack, sits under a new Others list with where they came from, and moves to New when an interested reply arrives. The menu's number counts New only. (09:53 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Inviting someone now has its own page, like a member's: pick their role, a different role on some brands if you want, and the teams they join. They get all of it when they accept. The API takes `brand_roles` and `team_ids` on a new invite and shows them on pending invites. (09:53 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Paid ads: The daily search review on Paid ads, Search terms now runs for every brand with Google Ads, with or without a brand agent. The card shows when it last ran and what it found, and a Review now button runs it straight away. (09:53 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: People with a Sales role get a Sales block at the top of the left menu in every area: New conversations, Others, My conversations, Sales reporting and First messages, one click away. The Sales page's own list is shorter: the daily lists on top, with personal and team set-up folded into two groups. (10:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The tracker's Report is quicker to slice: tick rows and pick Drill into to turn them into a filter and regroup by anything else, or click a chip to change the first level. It can now break down by browser version, OS version, device model, referrer and month, and shows tracking fields by the name your traffic source gives them. Report, Campaigns and Logs have a Refresh button. (12:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Search terms opens on the searches worth a look first: neither added nor excluded, no purchase, and at least 1 spent. Clear all shows every search. Where a review of the searches failed, the card says why, such as an AI account out of credit. (12:57 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Paid ads: When the daily search review can't finish, the Search terms card says why: the AI account is out of credit, over a limit, or didn't answer. (12:57 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Customer records: A choice field such as Seniority no longer looks empty when it holds a value that isn't in its list: it shows "Senior · not in the list" so you can pick the right option. Values written as an option's name or a common other word ("Head of", "C-level", "founder") are saved as that option, and lists your team made get "Add option" right in the picker. (00:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Brand search: follow any tracked competitor from a search box (before, only the first 40 were offered), with a link to your competitor list. Following, stopping and editing a brand's searches now show at once and save in the background, and searches are chips you add with Enter and remove with one click. (08:30 UTC)
