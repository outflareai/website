# Outflare 2026.11.01

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-01 12:22 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-01

## New

- **Open source** · Customer records: Undo a merge, and undo an import: a record's page lists its merges with Undo merge, which puts back everything the merge moved. People → Imports lists every CSV import, CRM import and send of product events; Undo… shows what it made and what changed since, then undoes only what's untouched or everything, restorable for 7 days from Recently deleted. (09:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Four new playbooks assess sign-ups for partnership potential: a review across partnership types, SDK and bandwidth suppliers, integration partners, and resellers or agencies. Each returns evidence, confidence and suggested next steps for review. (10:07 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Partners: Partner programs can refuse credit for website visits that came from paid ads or social media, for partners who may not bid on your brand or post on social networks. (09:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partner programs can block countries: website visits and new customers from them don't credit partners. A website visit's country now comes from the visitor's address, looked up in an IP database that ships with Outflare (IP geolocation by DB-IP), when your own proxy doesn't pass it. The address itself is never stored. (10:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Each brand now has its own people and its own catalog: the same email or user id in two brands is two separate people, never merged, and record types, fields, option lists and event definitions belong to one brand, with every new brand starting from the default set. Approving a held event name needs only the customers permission. (11:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: The partner portal can load your own website script, so partners' visits show in your website analytics, and Google Analytics 4 alongside it, held back until the visitor allows analytics. Pick a site key and, if you like, a measurement id in the portal settings. (11:39 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: People, companies, their history, lists, imports and duplicates now live under each brand, with API routes under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`, and every customer page works inside the brand you pick. Query runs for one brand, and each custom record type is a table of its own. (12:22 UTC)
