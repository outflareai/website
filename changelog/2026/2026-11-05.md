# Outflare 2026.11.05

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-05 13:05 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-05

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility has a score from 0 to 100 for your brand and each competitor, weighted by how many people ask each question, with a range that shows how much it can wobble and which weeks were a real change. A grid shows the score for each awareness level and question type. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility now says whether each answer recommends your brand and each competitor, only lists it, or warns against it, with the answer's words, and what kind of sites the answers cite. Brands the answers name that you don't count yet show up as suggested competitors you can add in one click. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility questions now say how much the person asking knows (the problem, the kind of solution, a competitor or your brand) and what they ask for (how do I, how do I fix, who's best, alternatives, X vs Y, best supplier, about you), with persona, topic and monthly demand. Your own AI model can draft questions from your brand, competitors and Search Console searches for you to keep or drop. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility can now ask ChatGPT and Gemini on their own screens, as a signed-out person in your cohort's country sees them, through your own DataForSEO account (about US$0.004 an answer). Each answer shows the searches the assistant ran, every page it read and whether it cited it, and every brand it named. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: How AI describes you lists what the answers say your brand is known for and what they hold against you, for you and each competitor, with where it comes from. Mark a claim as not true and it goes on the fixes list with the pages to correct. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Keep one AI visibility question set per market, asked and drafted in that market's language and place, and switch markets at the top of every page. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility is its own menu: a guided three-minute setup (your brand, competitors, drafted questions, assistants and the cost per run), an Overview that says whether you're winning in AI answers and what to do next, and pages for questions, competitors, sources, how AI describes you, traffic and settings. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Sources ranks the pages AI answers cite or read by how much being named on each is worth, with the competitors named there; each one is a Presence opportunity. Brands the answers name that you don't track are suggested on the brand's competitor list, with one click to track or dismiss. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Share a brand's AI visibility by link, with a password and expiry if you like, for anyone to see read-only; download the questions and placements as CSV; and get the score, the questions gained and lost and new objections in the weekly digest. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: A brand's website is read for what it offers when you add the brand, through your own Geonode connection and AI model: a summary, the industry and bullets for products, services, features, integrations, capabilities, who it serves and use cases, each linked to its page, on the brand's page for you to edit. AI visibility drafts its questions from it. (12:50 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Partners: Every partner program belongs to one brand, and its partners, links, conversions, rewards and payouts are that brand's alone. A brand's backend reports conversions to its own brand route, which credits only that brand's partners. (02:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Each brand has its own attribution, ad consent and visitor settings, under `/v1/brands/{brand}/.../settings`, and two brands of one workspace can use the same flag key, exclusion group, conversion key or attribution model key. The workspace settings routes are deprecated and act on the first brand. (02:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Email topics belong to one brand: each brand lists and uses only its own, and another brand's topic is not found. Topics that were shared by every brand moved to the workspace's first brand. Partner groups and reward rules now carry their program's brand and move with it. (03:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Two brands in one workspace can now use the same campaign slug, email topic key, template handle, lead email address and lead list name. Each stays unique within its brand. (04:40 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The brand agent can take on "Win more with A/B tests": it finds the pages where visitors drop off, builds test versions with no code, and asks you to start each test and ship each winner. (06:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Each brand has its own AI visibility settings (models, samples and monthly limit) and link building settings (pitch cool-down, weekly reads and their budget share). A second brand's opt-out of an address no longer applies to every brand. (07:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Each brand gets its own daily or weekly digest, with its own schedule and recipients, and every number in it counts only that brand. Two brands can also use the same partner program name. (08:12 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility puts the questions where AI answers name your brand but recommend a competitor on the fixes list, with what to do, and confirms the fix once answers recommend you. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI visibility counts, once a day on your own DataForSEO account, how many of the real AI answers DataForSEO keeps mention your brand and each competitor, with your share and the mentions gained or lost since the day before. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Pages that AI answers to your questions cite, without naming you, now appear in Presence opportunities, scored by how often they are cited and how many competitors those answers name. Pages the assistants read but don't cite are listed too. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Drafting AI visibility questions with a DataForSEO connection now starts from the real questions people ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews about your brand and competitors, and weights each question by how many times a month people ask AI assistants about it. (08:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Competitors → Overview has a Refresh all competitors button that reads every tracked competitor now on your own connections, and shows each one's Domain Rating, keywords, domain age and sitemap pages. (09:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Every mailbox and sending domain belongs to one brand and sends only for that brand's sequences. Mailboxes that were shared moved to the brand whose sequence they send for (or their domain's, or the workspace's only brand); any left with no brand are listed under Company → Brands to add to one, and send for no brand until then. (09:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Competitors: Yes and No now sit right beside each suggested site, and the daily look asks your SEO connection for 50 competitors instead of 10 (and up to 25 from your rankings), so the list keeps growing. Up to 50 tracked competitors a site get their weekly numbers. (09:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Platforms like GitHub, YouTube and Reddit are no longer suggested as competitors, and unless you add one by hand, your SEO connection's budget is never spent reading them. (10:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Support conversations take the brand of the mailbox they came to (or the workspace's only brand), so each brand's inbox shows its own customers. A partner link on a brand's tracking domain or portal, and a conversion reported on a brand's route, only match that brand's partner and promo codes. (10:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Search insights: the AI referrals report now works without GA4. When no GA4 landing-page outcomes are synced, it counts visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and other assistants in the referrers of your PostHog, Plausible or Matomo connection, for the whole site. (11:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: PostHog: leave out your project's internal and test users (bots, your team, a crawler with a fake referrer) in Outflare's reports. Tick it for a site on the Analytics page; every report, the daily sync and conversions by landing page then use PostHog's own filters, and the last 90 days are read again. (12:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence has a Found tab: every page that links to or mentions your brand, grouped by site. Each page is read once to check it links to you or names your website, so same-name pages about something else stay apart. About us or Not us settles a page or a whole site in one click, and the Overview counts everything found. (12:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Competitors suggests more competitors every day from who ranks for your keyword gaps (read on your own SERP connection), and **Find more competitors** reads a batch now. Click a keyword gap to see everyone who ranks for it, with Yes, No or Track beside each site. (12:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Site crawls now tell links in the text apart from menu, sidebar, header and footer links. Each crawled page shows how many pages link to it from their content, and each link says whether it is boilerplate, so you can see which pages your own writing backs, not only which ones every menu repeats. (12:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The Overview's trend has a Compare by switch: your score week by week against each competitor, by assistant, by market, or by the questions' awareness level, type, topic or persona. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Each tracked question shows whether AI answers name you, how often they recommend you, the change since the period before and the last weeks as bars, so you can see traction after working on a placement. Sort by the questions not naming you, biggest drops or biggest gains; the Overview lists what you gained and lost. (12:50 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · SEO: Presence and rank tracking no longer show Google's own redirect links (google.com/goto?url=...) for search results: each result is resolved to the page it leads to, with its real site, and existing finds are fixed on the next daily run. The Presence report now also counts every page and site found. (11:18 UTC)
