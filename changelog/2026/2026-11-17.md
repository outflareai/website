# Outflare 2026.11.17

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-17 20:41 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-17

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Recommended donors can now be sent straight to an Outreach sequence: Find emails and send to a sequence has OutflareBot find each site's advertising or editorial address (never data-protection, legal or no-reply mailboxes) and adds the best one to a sequence: the one used last time, or a new draft made right there. (06:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Internal and test people: list your team's email domains and your test accounts under Analytics, switch it on, and funnels, trends, retention, stickiness, lifecycle and paths leave them out. Nothing is deleted, and turning it off counts them again. (09:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: New API call and MCP tool to exclude a search from a Google Ads campaign or ad group: `POST /v1/brands/{brand}/ads/campaigns/{id}/negative-keywords` (`add_ad_negative_keyword`), with exact or phrase match. It is queued and listed like any campaign change. (10:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Catalog sites now say whether to skip them: too much search traffic on the home page or on one to three pages, or more links out than a real publication gives. Check in Ahrefs reads only the five numbers that decide it, and recommended donors leave such sites out. (11:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has a daily budget ceiling: set the most your running campaigns may spend in a day under Setup, Limits. A raise past it is refused for agents and API tokens, and asks you to confirm a second time. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Agents and the API can now read anything in a Google Ads account live: every campaign setting, ad group, keyword, negative, ad and asset, whether or not it ever had an impression, plus impression share reporting. A second call lists what can be asked for. Both only read. (14:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Optional funnel steps: mark a middle step Optional and people who skip it still count at the next step, so a sign-up step no longer hides payers who already had an account. The step shows how many did it and how many skipped it. (15:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Audiences under Paid ads: pick one of your lists of people once and send it to several ad networks. Outflare makes the list on Google Ads and Meta, adds people as hashed emails and phones, updates it daily, and shows each network's status and match numbers. Networks that take an upload, such as ChatGPT Ads, get a ready file. (18:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Every recommended action under Paid ads now opens its own page with the numbers behind it and what it would change. Say Good advice or Bad advice and why: Outflare saves it to the brand's memory, so agents follow it next time. (18:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Connect Blitz with your own API key to find decision makers at a company and their work emails from LinkedIn profiles. When a person's LinkedIn profile is known, the email step now asks by profile first (Blitz, then Treg), and asks again about people it could not find before the profile was known. It also asks about more people at once. (18:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads warns you when an ad network accepts your conversions but counts none of them: a new alert under Actions when a week of conversions sent with the network's own click id shows as zero for that conversion action, with a button to the send log. (19:37 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · SEO: One donor catalog for every brand: Import donors can send a broker's file to every brand at once, and the Catalog tab copies an existing catalog to the other brands with its prices, metrics and flags. Each brand's orders stay its own. (00:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads, Actions is now one table: every recommendation and alert with its type, why, the expected effect and one main button. Alerts open the page that fixes them, raises show the budget before and after and the most they can add a month, and recommendations have an obvious On/Off switch with a preview of what you'd get. (09:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has a new layout: six tabs (Overview, Actions, Campaigns, Customers, Experiments, Setup), one date menu with Today and a custom range, and an Overview where every number reads the same source. Your own brand's campaigns are judged apart from the rest, the top actions are listed first, and a three-step funnel shows clicks, sign-ups and first payments. (09:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: The SEO links pipeline in Presence has a Researching stage before Prospect, for sites you are looking at but haven't decided to pitch. AI apps connected to Outflare now see the tools to add a site to a pipeline and move it between stages straight away. If you set the pipeline up earlier, apply the SEO links kit again to add the stage. (09:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The send log can ask Google Ads what it did with a conversion after accepting it: processed, still processing, or refused and why. Press Ask Google Ads on a sent row. (10:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads, Campaigns: each campaign shows its daily budget, sign-ups, new customers and cost per customer against your target, with Pause on the row and a link to it in Google Ads. Tick several campaigns to pause them or change their budgets with one confirmation. Inside a campaign, Pause and Resume are buttons, and a search you're paying for has Exclude. (10:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales managers and workspace admins can now see each SDR and account executive's activity by day, plus a log filtered by teammate, action, person or company. Seen actions and successful AE transfers now retain history for reporting. (11:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Treg finds more work emails: it now asks Blitz, Filtrat, Kitt and Anymailfinder in turn instead of Treg's own router, which often ran out of time. A Treg connection can also be your verification service (Filtrat, with BounceBan as a second opinion). One person can now use up to four credits on a Treg connection. (11:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Donors now have to meet a base minimum: DR 30, 5,000 organic visits a month and no more than 45% of the traffic on one page. Recommended donors also work without choosing a page: they cover the top five suggested pages, three donors each. (12:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A campaign whose ads send people to more than one page shows Landing pages: its ads added up by page, with spend, clicks, conversions and cost per conversion. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A search that brings customers can be added as a keyword from a campaign's Search terms: Add as keyword, exact or phrase, with a confirmation that it can spend more. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A disapproved Google ad now says why: the network's own reasons, such as "Destination not working", show on the ad's row and in the alert under Actions. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads Experiments is now one table: each test with its status, what it tests, how far along it is, the trial's cost beside the campaign's and the result, with Keep the trial and Stop on the row. Results of past changes are a table too. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads Setup shows a real status on every line, problems first: the target cost per new customer, conversions, refused sends in the last week and tracking each say Working or Fix with what is wrong. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads Customers no longer says "Campaign unknown". A click from today says its campaign arrives tomorrow, and a click from a campaign that is not in a connected ad account says so and how to fix it. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Opening a Google Ads campaign shows its room to grow (the searches it missed because the budget ran out, or because others ranked higher) and where its money goes by device, country, network and hour. Also on the API and MCP as campaign signals. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads Overview has a chart of cost per new customer against your target, day by day, so you can see when it is safe to raise budgets. `GET /v1/brands/{brand}/ads/performance` returns the same numbers per day as `daily`. (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A campaign's Targeting now names every place it shows in and every place it is kept out of, with Google's ids for agents and the API, and says plainly when it runs everywhere. Campaigns and portfolio strategies can bid for a share of searches (Target impression share) or for clicks, and each bidding change is checked against Google afterwards. (14:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Search ads now look like they do in Google's results: three headlines, the site and two descriptions, with every line and its length one click away. This shows on a campaign's ads and on new ads waiting for your approval, which you can now edit before approving. Existing ads fill in on the next sync. (14:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels, trends, retention and paths each have their own link, and edits stay a draft until you press Save changes, with Cancel and Save as a copy. Funnel steps can be dragged into a new order, and ad clicks show as a labelled reference bar with its own switch. (15:58 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The agent chat beside a page now knows which page you're on. Ask "how is this campaign doing?" from a Paid ads campaign and it looks at that campaign. Its paid ads know-how covers the new page: room to grow, search terms, disapproval reasons and the daily budget ceiling. An ad it drafts shows in the chat as it will read in Google's results. (15:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Error capture is now one switch at the top of the Errors page, with a plain line that says whether errors are being captured on your site, or what is stopping them. On Website tracking, what the script does on its own (errors, clicks, form submits, A/B tests) has its own switches that save at once, without Edit. (16:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads tables now say whether an enabled Google Ads campaign, ad group or ad is really serving, in Google's words (Eligible, Limited by budget, Learning, Not eligible), with the reasons on hover. Campaigns also show Google's Optimization score. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Change bidding on a Google Ads campaign now offers Target impression share: pick where on the page the ad should show, the share of searches, and the most to pay for a click. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: An agent or the API can now set every place a Google Ads campaign shows in and is kept out of in one change, with a preview of what would be added and taken out. Exclusions you don't mention stay as they are, the change is refused if the places moved since the preview, and it is read back afterwards. A campaign can also be renamed. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads' Setup tab is now Settings, with its pages (General, Tracking, Send conversions, Send log, Ad accounts, Change history) in a row under the tabs, so each is one click from any other and there is nothing to go back out of. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads tables now work more like Google Ads: campaigns, ad groups, ads and keywords show only enabled ones to start with (a Status filter brings back paused and removed, and is remembered), statuses read Enabled, Paused and Removed, two total rows sit under each table, numbers line up under their headings, and every amount shows its cents. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The chart on Paid ads now has four number tiles, like Google Ads: click one to draw or hide its line, and pick another metric (clicks, cost, average CPC, click rate, conversions and more) from its menu. Your choice is remembered in your browser. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: An agent can now take back its own change while it waits for your approval, and point to the newer request that replaces it, so an out-of-date request can no longer be approved by mistake. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboard reports are easier to change: click a report's title or Edit to open it full screen with Save changes, Cancel and Save as a copy, and use its menu to rename, duplicate or open it in Trends. A report can now keep its own dates instead of following the dashboard's. (17:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels, Trends, Retention and Paths now open on a list of what you have saved, with a button to start a new one; click a row to open it. A funnel step's time limit no longer spills out of its box. (18:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Connecting ChatGPT Ads now asks for the Pixel ID, conversion key and website address on the same form as the Advertiser API key, so conversions can be set up in one go. (18:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Customers from ads now show their campaign, ad group and keyword within a few hours of the click instead of the next morning: Outflare reads Google Ads' click report on every sync, not once a day. (18:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Errors page is easier to act on: tabs for Open, Fixed, Ignored and Archived, search and filters, and ticking several errors to mark them fixed, ignore, archive or delete them at once, with Undo. Each error has a "Copy fix prompt for AI" button that hands your coding assistant the error, its stack, pages, browsers and release, plus "Copy error" for tickets. (18:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales conversations now open by themselves when someone you added to an EmailBison campaign through Outflare replies interested, books a meeting or asks a question. The conversation arrives in New conversations with the whole thread, and a later reply from the same person joins it. (19:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions sent to ChatGPT Ads now carry the person's IP address (when your server passes it on as `context.ip`), their browser and their country, so OpenAI can match more of them to an ad click. The browser comes from the event, or from the person's latest visit recorded by the Outflare site script, so your server doesn't need to send it. (19:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: The agent's chat panel can be made wider: drag its left edge (or use the arrow keys) to give the conversation more room. The width is remembered in your browser and stays the same on every page; double-click the edge to put it back. (20:00 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: On a Paid ads action, Ask the agent why opens the brand agent's chat with the question already asked, so it explains the recommendation with its numbers and suggests alternatives. The agent also reads what you said about earlier advice (Good advice, Bad advice), answers questions about your audiences, and can propose a campaign's places or a new name for you to approve. (20:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Products can belong to a category, such as proxies or web data, and a metric can count one category's products only, such as GB used across every proxy product in 30 days. Trends can now be split and filtered by a product's category and by a field of the person or their company, such as their plan or industry. (20:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A Treg connection can now read a person's LinkedIn profile (through Fetchin) and say where they work now, on your own Treg balance, so a tool that finds people can check someone still works at the company before contacting them. (20:41 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The agent's chat has a Stop button. While the agent is replying, press Stop to end its reply (after a typo, say), then send a new message. The stopped message stays in the conversation, marked as stopped, and the agent doesn't answer it. Anything the agent had already done stays done. (20:41 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Connectors: The Treg connector now reads an answer without an address as "no address found". Before, some of those answers were taken for a broken service, retried, and could switch the connection off for a while. (00:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A slow email lookup no longer stalls the email step. When a connected finder such as Treg gives no answer in time for a person, that counts as no address from it and the step moves on, where before it asked again up to five times and could switch the connection off for a while. (06:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A campaign's page now opens from its link even when the campaign is paused and spent nothing in the dates you're looking at. Before, it said "Campaign not found". (13:21 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Listing the fields of a Google Ads resource for a live read (`query-fields` with a resource) was refused by Google. It now answers. (16:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: **Connect Gmail** in Sales (My email) and in CRM email no longer fails with Google's "redirect_uri_mismatch" error. Both now return to the same Google address as "Continue with Google". (17:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A signed-in connection such as Google Search Console no longer breaks when the provider refuses a token early: Outflare renews the sign-in and checks again first. When it really has expired, the connection says so in plain words, with a Sign in again button that now works on an expired connection and also mends the other connections to the same Google account. (17:58 UTC)
