# Outflare 2026.11.15

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-15 19:13 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-15

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting preview sits beside Sales workspace: explore seven milestones, hourly or daily volume, each tool’s cost per lead, Sales mailbox capacity and closed deals. All figures are clearly marked as example data until reporting is connected. (01:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales teams can work a shared new-lead queue, open full conversations, save personal email templates, keep notes through handoffs and confirm customers without recording a payment. Personal email sending requires each person's own consent. (01:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Zeropark campaigns can now be paused, resumed and given a daily budget from Paid ads, and a single target paused or resumed through the API and MCP, each as a queued change on your own account. (16:58 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Two people can share a deal: set a Second owner and their share, and the deal is split between them for quotas, the forecast and commission. A month's commission downloads as a CSV file, one row for every line, for payroll. (00:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The Sales team page shows how each person handles their signals: how many they closed this quarter, how many were won, how long it usually takes, and how many are waiting or late. (00:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: One list of people and one of companies. CRM → People and Companies now also show scores, records held for review and archived ones (with Restore), and the bulk edit you can undo. Customer data keeps Events, Duplicates, Products, Transactions, Metrics, Scores, Routing, Imports and Recently deleted, and no longer has a second list. (00:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Picking a teammate looks the same everywhere: the Owner control on a record, "Assign to…" in CRM lists and Coverage now share one searchable list with photos. People in the team directory who have no sign-in can own records too, and their deals count towards their quota and commission. (00:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales queues, lead details and reporting lists show the original uploading agent or named API key. Results compares each uploader’s pipeline progress and discovery submission quality, keeping unknown origins and revoked identities visible without exposing credentials. (01:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Compare Sales milestones as lines or bars and open an hour or day to inspect every selected stage. Connect your Lead Portal for pipeline progress, costs per verified lead and mailbox capacity, alongside Outflare SDR pickup and confirmed customers. (01:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: More full pages have an address of their own: a journey step being edited, a commission plan, and the signal types, credit and conversations pages. Back closes them, a refresh keeps them open, and you can send someone the link. (08:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A playbook of your own can now also hold scores, routing rules, emails and metrics, and its page has "Did it work?": each of its journeys that holds some people back is compared with the ones it held back. (08:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Routing now knows when your team works and how much each person can take. Under the routing rules, set a teammate's working days and hours, the most people and companies they own at once, or mark them away. New records go to someone at work first, and never to someone who is away or full. (08:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboards are editable in place: press Edit on any tile to change its event, filters, breakdown, chart type or name, build new charts straight from Add tile, and change the dates, comparison, name and share link without leaving the page. (11:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The side menu fits better: open several groups at once without the others closing, fold away the headings you don't use, close every group with one button beside Search, and the menu remembers how you left it. Rows are a little tighter. (12:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Journeys now ignore events that arrive more than a day after they happened, such as imported history: they are kept for reports, but start, move and end no journey, so a backfill never emails people about the past. Automations, signals and lifecycle stages already worked this way. (15:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboard filters: add a filter such as plan is pro at the top of a dashboard and every chart on it shows only those events, on share links too. Tiles also move and resize by dragging. (15:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reps can now use the company's Google Workspace connection for their Sales email instead of signing in to Google themselves. In Sales, My email shows **Use Google Workspace** to reps the CRM email settings allow, and taking someone off those settings disconnects their Sales email too. (17:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Two new alerts under Settings → Alerts: one when a connected ad platform stops syncing (a refused sign-in, or three failed syncs in a row), and one when an ad platform starts refusing the conversions sent to it. Like every alert, each can go by email, Slack or Telegram. (17:59 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: "When this fires, do…" on a rule no longer fails with an error: the step you pick opens in the new journey, ready to fill in. Choosing a step is a bigger screen with groups down the side and a search box. On the automation map, many sources that send the same events fold into one card. (00:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Site audits no longer report email links hidden by Cloudflare's email obfuscation (`/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection`) as links to a broken page, and reading a page now finds those addresses. (17:01 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Website analytics on a brand now reads only that brand's own sites: the live reports never fall back to another brand's Search Console or Google Analytics site, and offer to pick the brand's site when it has none. A site read by two connections counts once, and signing in to Google again renews the connection instead of adding a copy. (17:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Sitemaps from Search Console no longer go stale when a sync can't read some of the search data: a sitemap you submit shows after the next daily read, or straight away when you sync the connection yourself. (17:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Sign in again on a connection now signs that same connection in, instead of adding a second one. It keeps its accounts and history, so a Google Ads connection from before Outflare asked for Data Manager can grant it without counting your ad spend twice. (18:05 UTC)
