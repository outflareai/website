# Outflare 2026.11.23

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-23 22:57 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-23

## New

- **Open source** · Connectors: Connect Linear, Notion or ClickUp, read only, and agents can search and read your issues, tasks, projects and docs when they need them, so they know what the team is working on and what it decided. Nothing is copied into Outflare, and nothing in your tools changes. (00:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Two-factor sign-in: add an authenticator app under Your account and type its code after your password, with ten backup codes for a lost phone, and "Remember this device for 7 days" so a trusted browser isn't asked every time. An owner can require it for everyone in the workspace, and reset it for a member who is locked out. Signing in with a passkey already counts. (03:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Dashboard templates: the Dashboards page now offers ready-made dashboards you add in one click. The first is a management report built from your payments: revenue, new customers, revenue by product, order value, lifetime value and money kept. Every report in it is yours to change. Agents use `list_dashboard_templates`. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Team directory: owners can now keep contact details, contracts, pay and payments for everyone who works for the company, people and organisations alike (employees, freelancers, contractors, agencies, service providers), with a change log, a monthly pay summary and a CSV export. Only owners can see it, enforced on the server. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Finances: a new section that shows the whole company's money in and out across every brand, in US dollars, with profit, margin, the last 12 months, each brand and where the money goes. It adds up what Outflare already records, so nothing needs connecting. Only owners see it unless an owner gives the Finances permission. (04:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A connection to an outside MCP server can be kept to owners: only owners see and use its tools, and an agent can use them only while it answers an owner. (05:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Our team: everyone in the company now sees who works here, with their photo, position, department, what they look after, work email, Telegram, GitHub and Slack. Members are linked to the staff directory by work email, so nobody is entered twice, and adding someone to Staff can also invite them to Outflare. (07:46 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Roles per brand: someone can now be on a different role for single brands, such as a viewer of the workspace who is a Member on one brand, or a Member who only views another. Open a person under Settings, Members to pick a role for each brand. On that brand its role decides what they can do, and API tokens they made follow it. (08:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels can start at the tracker's own clicks: pick "Clicked a campaign link" as a step and it counts every click the tracker redirected, before any page loads. The next step then shows how many of those clicks reached your pages, so you can see how many were lost before the first page, by campaign, traffic source, country or one of the click's custom fields. (18:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has a Payback tab: see day by day whether ad spend is on pace to come back, how long a customer takes to pay for themselves, and which campaigns have room for more budget, as profit or revenue, filtered by network, campaign type, name or campaign. Company has a new Cost and margin page, where you set your cost of goods once, for everything or per product. (20:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Discovery lanes now show companies that fit but have no named person as "Reachable another way", with the channels found for each: a shared inbox, a messenger, a contact form, a partner page or a social page. Each lane shows how many of its companies can be reached that way, and you can filter a lane's companies by it. (22:12 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Social: Mentions can leave out a namesake: add words under "Skip if it also says" for the brand or a competitor, and posts, videos and episodes that also say one aren't kept. New mentions that say one are cleared on the next scan. (00:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tags behave the same everywhere. On saved search reports and brand assets, as on other saved reports, a tag that repeats another in different capitals is dropped, extra spaces are tidied, and an empty tag is ignored rather than refused. (02:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Lifetime value and Distribution reports now have a Filter button, so you can count only some payments, such as one product or one first channel. On Lifetime value, click any number of customers to see who they are, with what each has paid. (02:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Support is now an area of its own in the menu, with Conversations, Saved replies, Reports, Help centre and Settings as pages. The Inbox holds only replies to your outreach. Old links to a support conversation or the help centre open in their new place. (02:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A chat can show an AI reply that is sent through a tool while it is being written: Outflare's AI layer now streams tool calls as well as text, for Claude and OpenAI-compatible models. (03:20 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The agent's chat shows a reply while it is being written, and long conversations keep working instead of being cut off. You can rate a reply, ask for it again, edit your last message, see the sources behind a reply, get charts in replies, and print a chat or save it as a PDF or Word file. (03:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Lifetime value can count from the first time a customer did an event, such as signing up, instead of from their first payment. Customers who never paid count too, so the average is what a sign-up is worth. Pick it under "Customers who first" on the Lifetime value page; agents pass `start_event` to `run_lifetime_value`. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Balances can be split by a field of the customer or their company, such as plan or country. Pick it under "Split by" to see what each group holds, how many customers it has and how much sits untouched, and click a group to list its customers. Agents pass `breakdown` to `run_balance`. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Staff now suggests people, details and leavers from your Google Workspace, and payments from a bank statement you pick as CSV (read in your browser, never uploaded); nothing changes until an owner accepts. Owners can also edit Staff's lists, such as person types. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Every saved report has its own address. Lifecycle and stickiness questions and saved Tracker reports now open straight from All reports, a link or a reload, where before they opened the page and left you to find the report. Revenue and a campaign's spend also show the amount in the period before next to the change. (04:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Distribution can show how usage is spread, not only payments: pick "Times they did an event" or "Usage: a number on an event" to see how many customers use a little and how many a lot, and how much of all usage the top tenth carry. Agents pass `event_count` or `event_total` with `event` to `run_distribution`. (06:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Anyone who can add tasks can now note a decision on a goal, including agents. Changing or replacing a decision still needs the permission to manage goals. (07:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Balances shows how long the amount held would last: "Lasts about" is what is held now divided by the last 30 days' rate of use. Agents read it as `days_of_use` from `run_balance`. (07:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The Agents page has a side column in place of its row of tabs: what waits for you, the history and what agents may do each open from it, and each has its own address. (08:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The menu now follows the parts of a business: Customers, Growth, Support and Analytics for the brand you picked, then the company's own sections. Email, search, social, ads, partnerships and automations sit together under Growth. Replies to outreach moved from the top of the menu to Growth, Outreach, Replies; old Inbox links still open them. (08:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Support has its own permission, separate from the Inbox: a role can now see and answer support conversations without seeing replies to outreach, or the other way round. Everyone who could use the Inbox keeps Support until you change their role under Settings, Roles. API tokens use the new `support.read` and `support.manage` scopes. (13:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Finances can show the company's money in your own currency: an owner picks it on the Money page (US dollars until then), and every brand's money is converted at each day's exchange rate. Cost categories now have the same names on the Money and Outcomes pages. (13:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales templates can be for every source or only for conversations from chosen Sales channels, and writing a message starts by picking one (or writing from scratch). Templates and sequences fill in more fields, including the person's own form answers, with fallbacks, and the sequence preview shows which fields were empty. (13:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales lists and conversations show each lead's email address under the name, with a copy button, and the lists can be searched by email, name or company. From a person's page, a Sales rep can email them or add them to a sequence: it opens the rep's own Sales conversation with them, or says which teammate holds it. (14:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales templates and sequences are written in wider editors with a preview beside them, buttons that put {first_name}, {company} and {my_name} in at the cursor, and a fallback for each field when a person has no value. Write with AI has larger boxes, and the composer warns when a message still holds a {field}. (14:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Coming back to Sales from a person's page, or from a conversation, shows the list as you left it, at once: the same tab, filters, search and place in the list. A person's page opened from a conversation has a link back to it, and a conversation opened from a person's page has a link back to the person. (15:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales templates and sequences keep directions for AI, which Write with AI and Personalize with AI follow. AI writes merge fields with fallbacks, the composer fills them in and shows what will be sent, and a send or sequence start with a field that has no value and no fallback is stopped with a fix offered on the spot. (16:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: CRM → Lead finder shows each day: companies judged, how many passed and their grades, how many have a verified contact, what was spent and what one verified contact cost. A cost that includes spend with no price yet is marked as a minimum. (16:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: CRM → Lead finder says plainly when a search is waiting for tomorrow because it reached today's spend limit, and how many companies are waiting. (17:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Scenarios: a scenario on a dashboard now has its sliders on the tile. Move one to try a number right there; nothing is saved, and one click goes back to the saved scenario. (20:25 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions can listen to what's said in podcasts: pick a speech to text connection under Sources, Podcasts (Groq's free plan is enough), and episodes with no published transcript are written out on your own account, so names said only aloud are found with their second. (21:13 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Headline numbers on Channels, Outcomes, Organic conversions, Sending reports and AI answer share now show what they were in the period just before, on a "Before:" line under each number, so you can tell at a glance whether a number went up or down. The AI answer share API returns the earlier totals as `previous`. (21:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Balances: each customer's page now shows what they hold of every balance you have saved, with when they last added to it and last used it. The API's balance customer list takes `record_id` for one customer. (21:34 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Social: YouTube and podcast mentions now search competitors with a real name first, and each scan carries on where the last stopped, so every competitor gets searched over a few scans instead of only the first ones in alphabetical order. (00:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales emails from your own mailbox, including sequences, are no longer refused because the person is on a customer list, a partner list or a sequencer's outreach blocklist. Those only keep people out of cold email. A refusal now names the address and the reason, such as unsubscribed or bounced, and clears when you pick another address. (10:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Podcast mentions read transcripts written by speech to text tools as JSON, and quote what was said instead of the transcript's code. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Distribution: Total paid now starts as what customers paid, with refunds left out, so no range starts below zero. A new Refunds control switches between gross and net, and with refunds taken off the first range reads "Under $10". The Management report template's tile counts the same way. (13:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: A contact without a person's name, such as a company's shared inbox, can now be added to an EmailBison campaign: EmailBison requires a first name, so the company's name is sent in its place. When EmailBison refuses one person's details, only that person is left out; the others in the batch go in and the run no longer pauses. (19:47 UTC)
