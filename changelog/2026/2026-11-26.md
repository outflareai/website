# Outflare 2026.11.26

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-26 18:36 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-26

## New

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Searches to exclude from a whole Google Ads account can be suggested with a reason, by a person or an agent. They wait at the top of Paid ads, Search terms, with their cost and the exclusion list each goes on, and nothing reaches Google Ads until someone presses Exclude. (11:54 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Paid ads: Every morning, each brand's agent looks over the brand's Google Ads searches on the company's own AI model and suggests the ones to exclude from the whole account, with a reason and the exclusion list each goes on. They wait at the top of Paid ads, Search terms; nothing changes in Google Ads until a person presses Exclude. (11:54 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Grade job applications against your own rubric, on your own AI connection. Each application gets a score per criterion with quotes from the answers, knock-out flags, a recommendation and interview questions. Try a rubric before you save it, grade everyone again when it changes, and record where you disagree. A grade only advises: it never rejects or emails a candidate. (17:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: See partners who run their own ads to your brand: Paid ads lists visits from ad clicks your ad accounts didn't buy, by partner, with their commission. A program can hold those partners' rewards for review ("Review partners' own ads", on for new programs). Remove a partner in one step: suspend them, forfeit what they haven't been paid and send them your own notice, while their links keep working. (18:36 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Live flow shows the companies found in a period as a small tree: discovered, researched and fit, then a branch for each outreach channel (email from verified to in a campaign to replied, LinkedIn, and other with the most common messengers). Each node opens what is behind it: a step's parts, or a part's companies. (00:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions now go to their ad platform as soon as they are matched, without waiting for the rest of the round that also handles refunds and verdicts. (14:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: read a catalog site's age from its domain's public registration record when no file gave it, for free, from the site's card or the API. A brand can set the lowest age of a site it buys a link on; younger sites are then marked Skip, and sites whose age is not known are marked Needs data. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Creating an API token now lists every permission with a plain name, what it allows and its exact API scope, such as `customers.read`. Search by any of them, or paste the scopes an integration asks for and turn them all on at once. (16:18 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: A shared outreach blocklist connection now takes the company and project your brand sends under, and gets every unsubscribe and opt-out the brand records written to it, each once and retried until it lands. Outreach and marketing email asks it again as it goes out: a blocked address is skipped, and an email waits while the list can't answer. (16:18 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions sent to Google Ads reach it within a minute or two again, instead of waiting about 15 minutes behind the check for refunds. A conversion Google gave no verdict for within a day is now checked again every hour for a week, so a late answer from Google shows in the send log instead of "no verdict". (11:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The "An event stopped arriving" alert no longer fires for Outflare's own server events, such as the one sent when stored secrets are re-encrypted after a key change. Those follow restarts and server settings rather than how your product is used, so their stopping was never a problem to fix. (12:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Large Google Ads accounts sync their search terms again: big report pages are read in full. Purchases and lifetime value per search, and the account's exclusion lists, now load even when another part of the sync fails. Search terms shows one network at a time, Google Ads unless you pick another. (14:59 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Paid ads: The daily search review never suggests excluding a search that brought a conversion, so a search that sells is never suggested while its purchases are still syncing. (14:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions reach their ad platform within minutes again: matching now checks only the events not sent yet, instead of every event of the last week on each round. (16:18 UTC)
