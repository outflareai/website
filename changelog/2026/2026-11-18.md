# Outflare 2026.11.18

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-18 21:38 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-18

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Your connections can do more: find a company's LinkedIn page and read a person's profile with Blitz, find who works at a company with Parallel, check whether EmailBison already emailed someone, ask your email finders one provider at a time, and fall back to your second verification service when the first can't answer. (00:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview now opens on Search Console's Performance report, read live from your own Search Console so the numbers match, with comparisons and every Search Console filter. It adds branded or non-branded, questions, query length, position, minimum impressions, won or lost rows, weekly or monthly charts and CSV export. (09:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Two new connections say who must not be contacted, on your own accounts: a product account lookup (is this person or their company already a customer) and an outreach blocklist service. Each answers clear, blocked or unknown for every address, and anything but a clean answer is unknown, never clear. (12:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The website script can load your ChatGPT Ads pixel for you: turn on "Load the ChatGPT Ads pixel" on a site's script and it loads the pixel of the brand's ChatGPT Ads connection, counts page views, and passes the signed-in visitor's hashed email and ID, with no pixel code on your site. (12:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales can now read the Slack channels your other tools post into, such as a website form. Each post becomes a person and company, a Sales conversation or a signal, and the queue shows which tool each one came from, filters by it in one click and marks the new ones; a Slack search on every conversation shows what your team already said; Outflare only reads Slack, using your own Slack app. (14:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: A new menu to try: a short rail of areas (Home, Inbox, Agents, Customers, Marketing, Analytics, Data, Company) with a menu beside it that shows only the pages of the area you are in. Press "Try the new menu" at the bottom of the sidebar, and "Old menu" on the rail to go back; your choice is remembered in this browser. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales reporting's Discovery view shows the discovery lanes of the brand's own lead finder on Outflare Cloud, live: what each lane sent, what was already known, and where every company is, from being checked to a person in the campaign, with why companies stopped. A lane opens as its own page with its companies and people. Discovery agents get MCP tools to send companies and read their lanes. (21:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Audiences can now be people who should never see your ads, such as everyone who was refunded. Choose "Never show them ads" on an audience and Outflare excludes its list on every active Google Ads and Meta Ads campaign, and on new campaigns within a day. This is how refunds reach Meta, which can't take a conversion back. (21:06 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Microsoft Advertising campaigns now show keywords and search terms, take negative keywords and ad group pauses, and have refunds taken back from sent conversions. X campaigns show their line items, which can be paused. Audiences now sync to X and Reddit as well as Google and Meta. Fixed pausing and budget changes on Microsoft campaigns. (03:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A list's page now links each person to their record and shows their email, lifetime value and sign-up date. A Columns menu picks which columns show, and Save as my default keeps your pick for every list. (08:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A list's page has Edit name and description, so any list can be renamed and described where you read it. (10:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The Performance report on SEO overview now looks like Search Console's, with date buttons, Add filter, check-box totals and an axis on each side of the chart. It also gains "24 hours", hour by hour, compared with the 24 hours before. (10:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Recommended donors now hide sites already in one of your sequences, add checkboxes with Select all, and show each site's light and top-page traffic share. Orders need a green light before you approve or publish, and a site Ahrefs checked in the last 30 days is never pulled again. (12:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The Campaigns table has a Room to grow column: the share of searches each Google search campaign missed last week to its budget and to rank. Resuming a paused campaign is now checked against your daily budget ceiling, like a raise. (14:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Assigning a Sales conversation now makes that teammate the person's Owner, replacing any Owner set earlier, and unassigning it clears the Owner when it was still that teammate; each change is in the person's history. A conversation that has been taken can also be passed to another SDR or account manager by its owner or a manager, once no email for it is still being sent. (14:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Recommended donors in Link orders can now show 25, 50, 100 or 200 picks, so you can tick them all and find emails and send them to a sequence in one go. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Products add themselves from your events: an event that names a product and its category (`product` and `product_category`) adds the product with that category the first time it is seen, and keeps the category in step with later events. Events without a category are held for review as before. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Connect your AI app with tool groups to list a whole area's tools up front, such as `https://app.outflare.ai/mcp?tools=core,analytics,seo`; every other tool stays one search away. Tools marked read-only can now never make a change: Outflare refuses it, so apps that run reads without asking stay safe. (15:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversion rules on payments can now send first payments only, so an ad platform counts each new paying customer once and renewals or top-ups add nothing. Choose it under Paid ads → Send conversions back → Payments to send; repeat payments only is there too. (17:02 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Adding people to an EmailBison campaign now checks the campaign first (active, with sequence steps, sending days and connected senders) and holds people while it isn't ready or has used its senders' daily capacity. Someone EmailBison already holds is left out instead of added, and new contacts go in together, with a note saying where they came from. (19:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: The Product account lookup and Outreach blocklist connectors can now check a company by its domain alone, before anyone there is known: the lookup asks for the company's domain, and the blocklist checks it against the service's daily domains export. The Lead finder shows companies' A to D grades beside their fit. (19:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Smart lists can group rules with all, any or none, and test page visits, other lists, email consent, event counts and properties. The new builder has searchable pickers, reads the rules back as a sentence, and can write them from a description on your workspace's own AI model. (20:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales: a conversation now shows every email you exchange with its person from your connected Gmail, in any thread. Before, only replies inside the original thread appeared, so a new email you wrote them was left out. Mail that includes anyone outside the conversation still stays out. (21:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Keywords in Paid ads now show who they brought, on Outflare's own count: sign-ups, the share of them that paid, new paying customers and what each cost. Search terms show the same share for the keyword they matched, so searches that bring people who never pay stand out. (21:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads' Send log now opens with a "Sent against counted" table: for each conversion action, how many conversions Outflare sent, how many carried the network's click id, and how many the network counts, with a status when it counts under half or none. (21:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The columns you save as your default on a list or a Paid ads table now stay with your account, so the table looks the same in every browser and on every device you sign in on. (21:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Errors are easier to fix and far less noisy: one bug is one error in every browser and build, browser-extension and scanner noise is filtered (plus your own ignore rules), and each error shows the share of visitors it hits, its site, release and what the visitor did before. Emails about new errors are now off until you switch them on. (21:06 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Email finding and verification through Treg: a provider that is busy for a moment (HTTP 429) is asked again in place, then later, instead of being read as an empty balance. A busy finder no longer stops a run, and a busy verifier no longer stores a good address as risky from the built-in checks. (10:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Email finding through Treg: a provider that is busy because everyone's calls fill it no longer slows your connection down, so the next providers in the chain are asked straight away. Only a rate limit on your own account slows the pace. (10:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales: taking a conversation now sends your introduction even when your own address is on the workspace blocklist. Before, a rep a sequencer's blocklist listed by address could never be copied on their own conversation, so the introduction failed on every retry. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: AI apps that ask before changes, such as Claude, can now read with any Outflare tool without an approval prompt: the new `call_read_tool` runs only tools that read, and `call_tool` stays the way to change things. Reading dashboards and their tiles is now in the core tool set. (14:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: An analytics or Search Console site nobody placed now belongs to the brand whose website it is, instead of the connection's brand or the workspace's first brand. When one Google sign-in reads the Search Console properties of several brands, a brand's search reports and dashboards no longer list another brand's pages and clicks. (15:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads recommendations, verdicts and the Bidding strategies table now show money in your brand's currency, like the rest of Paid ads, when an ad account is billed in another one. A brand's target is also compared with spend in the same currency. (15:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Treg: when Treg has no room for a call it is asked again after 2, 4 and 8 seconds, then the same call goes to your other Treg connection, for finding and for verifying an email. Verification now waits for Treg's slower answers, and a busy Treg no longer slows your connection down. (19:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Background work no longer waits behind a bulk email verification. Verification now runs on its own few workers, and addresses a verifier could not answer are asked again after five minutes instead of at once, so a slow or busy verifier can no longer hold up syncs, sends and alerts for half an hour at a time. (19:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A connected sequencer's draft campaign can now be linked to a sequence before anyone starts it there: name it in `link_campaigns` on `POST /v1/workspaces/{ws}/platforms/{id}/sync` (or the `sync_platform` MCP tool) and it comes in as a draft sequence, so it can be chosen as a campaign. Syncs still link live campaigns by themselves and leave other drafts out. (20:29 UTC)
