# Outflare 2026.11.18

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-18 09:30 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-18

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Your connections can do more: find a company's LinkedIn page and read a person's profile with Blitz, find who works at a company with Parallel, check whether EmailBison already emailed someone, ask your email finders one provider at a time, and fall back to your second verification service when the first can't answer. (00:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview now opens on Search Console's Performance report, read live from your own Search Console so the numbers match, with comparisons and every Search Console filter. It adds branded or non-branded, questions, query length, position, minimum impressions, won or lost rows, weekly or monthly charts and CSV export. (09:30 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Microsoft Advertising campaigns now show keywords and search terms, take negative keywords and ad group pauses, and have refunds taken back from sent conversions. X campaigns show their line items, which can be paused. Audiences now sync to X and Reddit as well as Google and Meta. Fixed pausing and budget changes on Microsoft campaigns. (03:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A list's page now links each person to their record and shows their email, lifetime value and sign-up date. A Columns menu picks which columns show, and Save as my default keeps your pick for every list. (08:16 UTC)
