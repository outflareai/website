## Improved

- **Open source** · Connectors: The account lookup and outreach blocklist connections now pass on the wait a service names with HTTP 503 and Retry-After, so callers can treat it as busy rather than down. (16:38 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A rate limit answer (HTTP 429) from Geonode, Parallel, EmailBison, Blitz or an email verifier is now waited out, honouring its Retry-After, instead of being read as an empty account; only out of credits (HTTP 402) still stops work for a person. Long provider calls (Treg, Geonode, Parallel) are no longer cut off after 30 seconds, and a finder call that timed out is never remembered as "no email". (16:38 UTC)
