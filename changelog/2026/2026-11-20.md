# Outflare 2026.11.20

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-20 21:55 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-20

## New

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Data catalog: Data → Catalog, and the MCP tool describe_brand_data, show every table of a brand's data that Queries and agents read with SQL: what it holds, where it comes from, how many rows and how fresh, and what each column means, with its unit, what it joins to, how often it is filled and its common values. Agents now get run_query and the catalog in their default tools. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The send log in Paid ads shows Meta's match quality: a score out of 10 for each event you send, how it moved against the week before, which identifiers went with it and what Meta says to fix. A low or falling score raises an alert. (19:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Creatives tab in Paid ads: a brand's creative library, talking videos, skills and prompts, and brand kit now have pages in the app, each one click from the others. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Brand profile: a new first page under Company that keeps what your agents know about the brand (company, offer, customers, personas, positioning, proof, competitors, objections, voice, goals, channels and rules) as separate facts. What an agent finds waits for a person's yes before it is ever said to a customer, and every change is kept in a history. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Talking videos: over the API and MCP, a brand can make a video of a person saying a script on its own ElevenLabs and fal.ai accounts, with an upscale, the look of a phone camera and captions, then fix or remove a word without making the whole video again. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Skills and creatives: over the API and MCP, a brand can save a recipe for one kind of ad and make ads from it on its own AI account. Recipes are versioned, two to four prompts can run side by side as a split test, and each ad waits for a person's yes or no. Outflare lays the words and logo over the picture, so one picture gives every size. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Pictures, video and voice on your own AI accounts: over the API and MCP, a brand can ask its own OpenAI, fal.ai or ElevenLabs account for a picture, a video or a voice recording, and the file is kept with the brand's files. fal.ai and ElevenLabs are new connections under Connectors. You choose the model, each request is sent once so nothing is paid twice, and you pay the provider directly. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Brand files: each brand can keep its logos, fonts, product shots, screenshots, example ads, voices and pictures of people in one place, over the API and MCP, for ads and other content to be built from. A voice or a person's picture can't be used until someone confirms that person agreed. (21:55 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Connectors: The account lookup and outreach blocklist connections now pass on the wait a service names with HTTP 503 and Retry-After, so callers can treat it as busy rather than down. (16:38 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: A rate limit answer (HTTP 429) from web data, Parallel, EmailBison, Blitz or an email verifier is now waited out, honouring its Retry-After, instead of being read as an empty account; only out of credits (HTTP 402) still stops work for a person. Long provider calls (Treg, web data, Parallel) are no longer cut off after 30 seconds, and a finder call that timed out is never remembered as "no email". (16:38 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Agents can export a query's results as CSV over MCP (export_query_csv), up to 10,000 rows, for lists longer than run_query returns. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Agent work, tests, journeys, social and content in SQL: Queries and agents can now read agent runs and missions with their updates and reports, the action log, what agents learned, A/B tests, insights and alerts, journey steps, social accounts and posts, content, email templates and suppressions. Each table is explained in the data catalog. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Customers, sales, support and partners in SQL: Queries and agents can now read every record's field history, merges, lists and members, scores and signals, sales leads and commissions, CRM tasks, meetings and emails (without content), MRR by day, support conversations (without bodies), and partner programs, referrals, rewards and payouts. Each table is explained in the data catalog. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Search, SEO and AI visibility in SQL: Queries and agents can now read Search Console clicks, URL inspections, Core Web Vitals, crawls, rank checks, competitors, AI visibility prompts, answers, mentions and citations, AI crawler visits, web analytics, page speed, heatmaps, site visitors, experiments and session recording details. Each table is explained in the data catalog. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracker conversions now say whether the traffic source was told, and if not, why (suspect, filtered by the postback rules, no traffic source and so on). A postback that carries both the network's own click id as cid and Outflare's ofclk credits the click, and a send that went out without the network's click id is marked. (17:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Preview where a tracker click would go. The offer and lander forms, the API (`GET /v1/brands/{brand}/destination-preview`) and the MCP tool `preview_destination` show the final address for a URL as you wrote it: the click id and campaign tags Outflare adds, anything it replaces, and what it leaves alone. A preview counts no click. (17:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Owners and admins can now delete a published query endpoint, from the Endpoints list or its own page, after a confirmation. Its address stops answering and can be used again, its call log goes with it, and the saved query and the audit record stay. Turning an endpoint off still pauses it instead. (17:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Agents can read the creative studio through SQL: the brand's files, provider requests, talking videos with their stages and versions, creatives, skills, lessons and each creative's history are in Query and the data catalog, behind `content.read`. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Talking videos and creatives keep everything you say about them: a fix waits for your keep or go back, every version can be gone back to, you can like one or say "not this" with a reason, make variations of one that works, and teach lessons (how to say a word, rules for prompts) that every later creative follows. One history per creative shows all of it. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Pictures, video and voice keep their recipe and take references: every request stores what was sent to the provider and what came back, can be made again with one thing changed, and can name brand files (a frame of a real video, a person, an ad you liked) to make the new file from, so a person or a scene stays the same. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The box that fills in a smart list from a description is now a clear text field with its own heading, so it is obvious where to type. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: The new menu (a rail of areas with a menu for the area you are in) is now the menu everyone starts with. The old single sidebar is one click away: press "Old menu" at the bottom of the rail, and your choice is remembered in this browser. Data's menu also links straight to Events and their sources, and Transactions. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Alerts: mute or unmute many kinds of alert for yourself in one click. Narrow the list with Show or search, then Mute all for me, or tick only the ones you want; Undo puts them back. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Tracker campaigns: the campaign URL now sits at the top of a campaign's page with a Copy URL button, on every tab. The page has a clear Back to campaigns button, its tabs are named Settings, Landers and offers, and Tracking parameters, and Settings uses the full width. (21:55 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A query for one brand now shows only that brand in every table: visits, spend, backlinks, reviews, social posts, link prospects, link orders and organic conversions used to show the other brands you can see too. (17:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions sent back to ad networks now carry the ad click id from the person's own earlier events, such as the click id your server sent with their sign-up, so a payment sent from your server can be tied to the ad that brought them even when no website visit was linked to them. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: A payment counted from an Order Completed event now takes the product the order names, so a company's first order moves it to active on that product. A payment sent first without a product picks it up from its order. (21:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Long forms in pop-up windows, such as connecting Google Workspace, now scroll, so the buttons at the bottom can always be reached. (21:55 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: A brand's agent no longer loses the website knowledge and findings when the brand's notes are long. The notes are shortened first, and the agent is told what was left out. (21:55 UTC)
