# Outflare 2026.11.11

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-11 18:14 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-11

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Brands can now manage a paid-link catalog and place link orders through the API (and the MCP tools that call it). Amounts are the donor price only, in USD or EUR, never summed together. Approving an order and marking it paid is limited to the person set as that brand's approver, and paid only after the link is published and a live-link check has passed. There is no commission or markup. (01:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The app now has a Link orders page in the SEO section, next to Backlinks, for paid placements from the brand's own donor catalog. Amounts are the donor price only; Approve and Paid are limited to the brand's designated approver, and recording Paid does not send money. (08:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Broadcasts can go to a saved list, and you can upload a CSV of people (by email or your own user id) into a list from the broadcast editor. A broadcast can also be a service message, which skips marketing choices and the unsubscribe link but never reaches suppressed addresses. (15:32 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Business details on About your company: save each brand's legal name, tax or company number and postal address once. Lifecycle email adds the address to its footer (or place it with {{brand_address}}), sequences can use it too, and the brand's help centre, cookie policy and agents show it. (15:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Delete a brand for good: on the brand's page (Company → Brand), choose Delete brand and type its name. Everything in the brand goes with it, and nothing moves to your other brands. Owners and admins only; your only brand can't be deleted. (16:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Every CRM card now opens with a relationship check: whether the company is already a customer, has an open deal or sits in another pipeline, which teammate has emailed them for a warm intro, and whether the last conversation went quiet. (17:01 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Google Ads: create a website conversion and copy its tag, manage account callouts and portfolio Target CPA and Target ROAS strategies, remove campaigns, ads and keywords (you only, after a confirmation), see all conversions, keyword bid estimates and search term match types, pick 30 days or any dates, and download every report as CSV. (17:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads: one click sets up Outflare's conversion feed on a Google Ads account: three conversion actions (sign-up, first payment, repeat payment) sent through Google's Data Manager API with each person's new or returning customer type, a daily-refreshed Customer Match list of paying customers, and the new customer acquisition goal. Google Ads no longer asks for a developer token. (17:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Admins can now read the team's work email into the CRM through their own Google Workspace connection, so nobody has to sign in one by one. The same filters, sharing rules and AI privacy check apply, and it can start from each mailbox's whole history. (17:34 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · SEO: Paid link orders can now start from the brand's already-saved competitor link gap, stay in sync with live-link checks, and suggest equivalent catalog donors when a link is lost. A passing check is what allows pay (the donor price only; no money is sent). (07:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview now leads with non-branded clicks (with CTR and position), search conversions, keywords in the top 3 and top 10, AI mention rate, and share of voice versus tracked competitors, all from stored data. Snapshot tiles stay on one SEO connector so the arrows compare like with like, and missing query history is no longer read as zero branded clicks. (11:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: In a keyword gap's "who ranks" panel, sites marked "Not a competitor" now have a Track button too, so one click makes any ranking site a competitor. (11:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Paid link orders can now be queried as `coverage_link_orders` and `coverage_donor_profiles` (with `coverage.read`), and the product guide is in the docs. Catalog import is CSV only; a Google Sheet is not imported. SE Ranking does not feed referring-domain candidates. (12:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Each brand's catalog (record types, fields, option lists and events), channels, alert settings, record lookup, recipient checks and mailbox senders are now only on the brand's own routes. The old workspace routes answer 410 with the route to call instead. (13:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The SEO overview shows each number on its own row with a full chart: values on the y-axis, each week on hover, the period before drawn beside it, and the biggest weekly rise and drop. Pick any dates, from 4 weeks to 2 years, and the API's `period` compares them with the weeks before. (13:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Sidebar pages are now real links: right-click, Ctrl/Cmd-click or middle-click any of them to open it in a new tab. (13:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Inside a brand, Company → Brand now shows only that brand's own settings, never a list of your other brands. Switch brands, or add a new one, from the brand switcher at the top. (13:59 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Kits with smart lists, rules and journeys can now be set up for any brand, and each brand gets its own copies. Two brands can also have smart lists with the same name. (14:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Site audits now count distinct pages with errors, warnings and notices, so a crawl card is no longer inflated by rules that fire once per language pair. Notices are hidden until you choose to show them, and URL templates that differ only by a locale (such as /de and /fr) are rolled up into one row with a locale breakdown. (15:32 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: Connections with no brand now show what tells whose they are: the account they signed in to, what the provider reports (such as Google Tag Manager's containers and the sites they tag, or Cloudflare's zones), who connected them and when, and a suggested brand you can assign in one click. (16:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads findings are now judged in cost per new paying customer: rules for budgets, pauses, targets, negatives, keywords, ads and exclusions read Outflare's own sign-ups and payers. Every change waits for you to approve it (in Outflare Cloud, savings can apply on their own with an undo), and shadow mode shows what the rules would do first. (17:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads sync every hour and re-read longer windows as the platforms revise their numbers. Outflare alerts you when spend is far off its usual, the month is off pace, a campaign stops serving or brings no sign-ups, ads are disapproved, conversions are refused or tracking breaks, and it keeps Google's own change history beside its changes. (17:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: New ads are written only from a brand knowledge sheet you have reviewed: check what Outflare read from your website and mark it "Looks right". A read that can't run says which connection to add, the website is read again daily once an ad account is connected, and each draft lists warnings (a price the sheet lacks, a superlative, a competitor's name, too few headlines). (17:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Making a broadcast is now a full page in four steps (kind, email, who gets it, name) with a preview of the email beside it. Uploading a CSV of people moved to Lists, New list, with an example file and a check of what was read before anything is saved. (17:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Consent banner: add `data-reopen="off"` to the tracking script to leave out the round button in the corner, when your site has its own "Cookie settings" link that calls `outflare.showConsent()`. (17:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: "All brands" is gone: the app always shows one brand, and its stats, reports, outcomes, attribution and email pages read only that brand. Every brand, archived brands and records in no brand are under Settings → Brands. (17:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Desktop app: the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar now shows which version of the Outflare app you have, with Check for updates. A waiting update still shows as Update available in the sidebar. (17:34 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Sending transactions or customer events at the same time for the same people no longer fails now and then with a 500 error: a write that loses a database deadlock is retried instead. (14:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Every Outflare tool now works in ChatGPT and other apps that cap how many tools they show. Apps see about 50 core tools, including email templates, send email and broadcasts, plus `find_tools` to search all of Outflare's tools and `call_tool` to run any of them. Connect to `/mcp?tools=all` to list every tool instead. The MCP server now reports the Outflare version it runs. (14:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Email providers and sending domains that belong to no brand are shared, so every brand's Email providers page now lists them, marked Shared, instead of asking you to connect a provider. Disconnecting the default provider makes the next one the default, so sends always have a sender. (14:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Failed Ahrefs (and other SEO) reads no longer count against the connection's budget, and a refused report is not retried and billed again every hour. Competitor suggestions run weekly with the snapshot, not every day. (16:04 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: The screen where you approve an AI app, and the API token and connected app settings, now include Lifecycle email (templates, broadcasts, journeys and sending) and Coverage. Before, an app like ChatGPT or Claude could never be given these permissions, so its email template and send tools failed. Turn Lifecycle email on for an app you already connected in Connect your AI. (16:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Competitor suggestions are fetched once per weekly cycle: the weekly snapshot still asks the provider, and a second suggest job is no longer queued in the same cycle. (17:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Desktop app: email previews (in email templates, sent emails and the help centre) show again instead of a blank box. (18:14 UTC)
