# Outflare 2026.11.11

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-11 13:48 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-11

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Brands can now manage a paid-link catalog and place link orders through the API (and the MCP tools that call it). Amounts are the donor price only, in USD or EUR, never summed together. Approving an order and marking it paid is limited to the person set as that brand's approver, and paid only after the link is published and a live-link check has passed. There is no commission or markup. (01:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The app now has a Link orders page in the SEO section, next to Backlinks, for paid placements from the brand's own donor catalog. Amounts are the donor price only; Approve and Paid are limited to the brand's designated approver, and recording Paid does not send money. (08:19 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · SEO: Paid link orders can now start from the brand's already-saved competitor link gap, stay in sync with live-link checks, and suggest equivalent catalog donors when a link is lost. A passing check is what allows pay (the donor price only; no money is sent). (07:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview now leads with non-branded clicks (with CTR and position), search conversions, keywords in the top 3 and top 10, AI mention rate, and share of voice versus tracked competitors, all from stored data. Snapshot tiles stay on one SEO connector so the arrows compare like with like, and missing query history is no longer read as zero branded clicks. (11:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: In a keyword gap's "who ranks" panel, sites marked "Not a competitor" now have a Track button too, so one click makes any ranking site a competitor. (11:16 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Paid link orders can now be queried as `coverage_link_orders` and `coverage_donor_profiles` (with `coverage.read`), and the product guide is in the docs. Catalog import is CSV only; a Google Sheet is not imported. SE Ranking does not feed referring-domain candidates. (12:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Each brand's catalog (record types, fields, option lists and events), channels, alert settings, record lookup, recipient checks and mailbox senders are now only on the brand's own routes. The old workspace routes answer 410 with the route to call instead. (13:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: The SEO overview shows each number on its own row with a full chart: values on the y-axis, each week on hover, the period before drawn beside it, and the biggest weekly rise and drop. Pick any dates, from 4 weeks to 2 years, and the API's `period` compares them with the weeks before. (13:48 UTC)
