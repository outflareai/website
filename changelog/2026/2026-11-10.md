# Outflare 2026.11.10

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-10 13:04 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-10

## New

- **Open source** · Analytics: Redirect links can send their clicks to several pages: give each a weight for its share and an optional daily click cap, and once every page has had its clicks for the day, the rest go to the link's own page. Link scanners don't count against a cap. (08:13 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Rules on redirect links: send the clicks that match a path's conditions (country, device, network, IP range, hour, first or returning visitor, custom fields) to its own pages, in order, with the link's other pages for everyone else. (09:01 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Redirect links can send people on with a 307 or a meta refresh that hides the referrer, and a tracking domain can send unknown links to a page of your choice. Destinations, rules and these modes now run only on your own tracking domain; on the shared domain, links go to their own page. (09:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Conversions by click id: give each affiliate network a postback URL on your tracking domain, and every conversion it reports is recorded against the redirect click it came from, with its payout and status, even when no person is known. Duplicates, late postbacks and unknown callers are handled per source. (10:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Tracker conversions by pixel and upload: put a pixel or script on a thank-you page when a network can't send postbacks, or upload a network's report as CSV or through the API. Rows with only a transaction id update conversions already recorded, for approvals and reversals reported later. (11:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: A conversion log for tracker postbacks and pixels: see every call from the last 30 days with what it sent and why it was or wasn't recorded. Recorded and changed conversions are also webhooks, tracker_conversion.created and tracker_conversion.updated. (13:04 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Partners: Running a partner import again now brings over a partner's new email from Tolt. The new address replaces the old one when that was the only, unverified email on their record, and is added beside a verified one. (08:43 UTC)
