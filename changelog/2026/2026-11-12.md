# Outflare 2026.11.12

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-12 14:30 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-12

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Link ROI: see the donor price of each published link against already-stored Search Console, ranking and referring-domain changes on the target page, 30, 60 and 90 days around publication. The figures do not show that the link caused a change. (01:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI answer share: see mention rate and share of voice from already-stored AI answers, by topic, with a weekly trend and citations of compare pages. This does not start a new run. (02:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has Offers, Landers and Flows tabs: offers with a token picker, a missing click id warning, payouts by country, caps with a fallback and a test preview; landers with each call to action's click URL and page script; and flows edited like a campaign's paths. (08:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has Traffic sources and Affiliate networks tabs: start from a gallery of network templates, preview the campaign URL as you edit its parameters, and send conversions back with a postback that can't be saved half filled in; each affiliate network shows its postback URL to paste into the network. (08:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has a Setup tab: a checklist that ticks itself as you set up conversion tracking, a postback URL builder in each affiliate network's own tokens with its pixel, script and server call, and a test conversion that says what happened and why. (09:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has a Logs tab: clicks, lander clicks, conversions, and postbacks in and out, newest first with Live refresh, plus the postbacks that weren't recorded, grouped by why with the fix. The API and MCP list clicks and lander clicks too. (10:03 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Playbook automations belong to a brand: each brand sets up, approves and runs its own, and an event automation starts only on its own brand's events. Automations that covered every brand now belong to the first brand. Adding a website visitor's company to the CRM, and the company IP lookups find, stay within the visitor's brand. (00:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Each brand has a reporting time zone. Attribution and paid ads reports count days in it (with `?timezone=` for one report), and tracker caps and path rules by day or hour follow the brand's midnight. Ad platforms' own numbers stay in their accounts' time zones. (01:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Insights, dashboards, site analytics, email reports and the digest now count days in the brand's reporting time zone, so "today" and a chart's days are the brand's. Periods stay whole across daylight-saving changes, and `?timezone=` reads any of them in another zone. (02:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders can now target article and product pages: a new Targets tab suggests pages from stored competitor data, you tick the ones to use, and each order gets a primary anchor from SE Ranking plus an optional second anchor (brand, URL, or another keyword). (08:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: On Link orders Targets, the sources line still shows how many stored keywords, targets and competitor pages the suggestions come from, but no longer lists every tracked competitor by name. (09:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker's Report can show tracker clicks instead of site visits: clicks, conversions from your affiliate networks, payouts and the cost traffic sources reported, by campaign, traffic source, offer, lander, path, custom field or any click detail. (10:43 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: import an intermediary's full database (up to 32 MB, sent in batches with progress). Headers like "Traffic / Organic" now map, and the donor card shows the full metrics: referring domains, backlinks, keywords, outgoing links, geo and more. Donors get their own Intermediary (searchable), Theme and Site age fields. (12:01 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The lander script now carries a honeypot: a link no person can see or reach, so a bot that follows every link on a lander flags its own click as suspect, keeping it out of caps and reports. (12:53 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: right-click any catalog cell for site actions. Find email shows the addresses already stored for the site (people, link-building contacts, Outreach leads, notes). Send to a sequence adds a stored or typed address to one of the brand's Outreach sequences with the usual suppression and exclusion rules. (12:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Direct-mode tracker clicks, recorded by the site script, are now checked against the brand's user agent click filters too, so they are flagged as suspect like redirect clicks. (13:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: X Ads and Reddit Ads can now be managed from Outflare: pause, resume and set campaign budgets (with your approval), get the same budget and pause suggestions as Google and Meta, read each network's own conversions, and send sign-ups and purchases back through X's and Reddit's Conversions APIs (Reddit's newer v3 API once you add your pixel ID). (13:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: A sent broadcast now shows its recipients: one row per person with what happened to their email (delivered, bounced and why, marked as spam, skipped and why) and when it was sent, delivered, opened and clicked. Filter by status with one click, search for a person, or download a group as a CSV. The API and MCP list the same with `show` and `q`. (13:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The sidebar groups its modules under seven headings (Customers, Email and outreach, Search, Social, Ads, Measure, Company). Home puts the outreach email numbers under their own heading. The Agent page uses plainer names: All changes, Playbook runs and Rules, where a grant is now called a rule. (14:30 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Analytics: When one person makes two payments of the same amount minutes apart, a copy of the second that arrives before its twin is now kept as its own payment. Before, a guess could join it to the first payment, which already had both copies, and Developer setup reported a payment matched only by a guess. (01:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Business details on About your company no longer show example values in empty fields, and an address in a city-state such as Singapore no longer names the country twice. (13:32 UTC)
