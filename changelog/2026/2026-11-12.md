# Outflare 2026.11.12

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-12 08:44 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-12

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: Link ROI: see the donor price of each published link against already-stored Search Console, ranking and referring-domain changes on the target page, 30, 60 and 90 days around publication. The figures do not show that the link caused a change. (01:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: AI answer share: see mention rate and share of voice from already-stored AI answers, by topic, with a weekly trend and citations of compare pages. This does not start a new run. (02:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has Offers, Landers and Flows tabs: offers with a token picker, a missing click id warning, payouts by country, caps with a fallback and a test preview; landers with each call to action's click URL and page script; and flows edited like a campaign's paths. (08:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: The Tracker has Traffic sources and Affiliate networks tabs: start from a gallery of network templates, preview the campaign URL as you edit its parameters, and send conversions back with a postback that can't be saved half filled in; each affiliate network shows its postback URL to paste into the network. (08:44 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Playbook automations belong to a brand: each brand sets up, approves and runs its own, and an event automation starts only on its own brand's events. Automations that covered every brand now belong to the first brand. Adding a website visitor's company to the CRM, and the company IP lookups find, stay within the visitor's brand. (00:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Each brand has a reporting time zone. Attribution and paid ads reports count days in it (with `?timezone=` for one report), and tracker caps and path rules by day or hour follow the brand's midnight. Ad platforms' own numbers stay in their accounts' time zones. (01:56 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Insights, dashboards, site analytics, email reports and the digest now count days in the brand's reporting time zone, so "today" and a chart's days are the brand's. Periods stay whole across daylight-saving changes, and `?timezone=` reads any of them in another zone. (02:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders can now target article and product pages: a new Targets tab suggests pages from stored competitor data, you tick the ones to use, and each order gets a primary anchor from SE Ranking plus an optional second anchor (brand, URL, or another keyword). (08:06 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Analytics: When one person makes two payments of the same amount minutes apart, a copy of the second that arrives before its twin is now kept as its own payment. Before, a guess could join it to the first payment, which already had both copies, and Developer setup reported a payment matched only by a guess. (01:20 UTC)
