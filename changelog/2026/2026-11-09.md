# Outflare 2026.11.09

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-09 16:24 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-09

## New

- **Open source** · Everything else: Outflare for Mac, in testing: the Outflare you use in the browser, in a window of its own, in the dock, signed in once from your browser, with system notifications and a badge. Test builds are unsigned for now; the download page follows. (08:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Each partner now has their own page, with their numbers, referred customers, conversions, rewards, payouts and history in one place. Searching and sorting the partners list now covers every partner in the program, not only the newest 100, so you can find an older or imported partner by code, email, name or id. (10:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Deliverability: Check any domain's SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX and domain blocklist standing without an account, with the exact DNS record to add for each problem. It powers the free checkers on outflare.ai and is open to anyone at `GET /v1/tools/domain-check`. (10:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Each partner reward now has a proof trail from the click or visit to the sale, rule, approval and payout, and you can download it for a payout or a partner. The Rewards and Conversions lists show and search the partner, customer and sale, and bulk approve leaves out rewards your old platform still pays. (12:50 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Partnership pipelines: Presence's Partnerships tab has a board each for SEO links, sponsorships, integrations, affiliates and comparison sites. Add a partner by website; when one goes live it becomes a tracked placement, and affiliates move on their own when they join your partner program and make their first sale. (14:30 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Everything else: Outflare for Mac updates itself: when a newer version is ready, an Update button appears at the bottom of the sidebar. One click installs it and reopens the app; nothing else to do. (10:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: Test events you send to a webhook are marked "Test" in its delivery list, with the receiver's status code, so you can show that the endpoint works. A failed test no longer counts against the endpoint. It doesn't start a failure streak, raise the error rate or turn the endpoint off on a 410. (10:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence's Found list can mark a site as spam (its pages leave the list, and pages found on it later stay hidden) or check a page again in a week, for a site that's offline. Follow-ups, formerly the Work queue tab, are now a view on the Found page. (10:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence opens on Found, and the Overview tab is gone (Reports has the same numbers). Found shows every page as its own row with a link that opens it in a new tab, says what the page shows in its own column, and asks "Yes, track it" or "No, hide it" with a line explaining what each does. (10:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Knowledge, memories, help centres and the support inbox (conversations, the assistant, SLAs, chat widgets and handoff topics) now belong to one brand each, under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`. The old workspace routes answer 410 and name the route to call instead. Their pages ask you to pick a brand. Support mailboxes and the workspace's assistant and SLA defaults stay on the workspace. (11:13 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: Click a task on the Agent page to open it: its whole plan, where it is, its results, what waits for your OK, every change the agent made for it, and a link back to the conversation it came from. (11:26 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The agent's activity and turns are under History on the Agent page, and its tasks and standing instructions are under Settings, so the top row is just Chat, Waiting for you, History and Settings. (12:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: The Agent page's top row is shorter: Chat, Waiting for you, History and Settings. History holds every agent's changes and Outflare agent runs; Settings holds what agents may do. Each picks its view from a row of buttons. (12:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Paid ads now belong to one brand each, under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`, and the old workspace routes answer 410 with the route to call instead. The Ads page asks you to pick a brand; connecting ad accounts and assigning them to brands stay on the workspace. (12:15 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The brand agent now tells your team when a task is finished, when it needs your OK on a change, and when it gets stuck. Each one shows in Alerts and is emailed to your owners and admins; choose who gets them in Settings, Alerts. (12:15 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The brand agent's alerts now open exactly what they are about, on the right brand: a finished task opens that task's page, and a request for your OK opens the changes waiting for you. (13:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Ideal customer profiles, senders, inbox tags and saved inbox views now belong to one brand each, under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`, and the old workspace routes answer 410. A profile shared by every brand is now copied into each brand, and two brands can use the same profile name. (14:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: AI accounts now shows the workspace's receipt email address at the top, with a copy button. Outflare makes it by itself the first time anyone opens the page, and admins can press Generate email address if that fails. (14:19 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Reviews: SourceForge is now a review site. Outflare finds each brand's SourceForge listing for you to confirm, alongside Trustpilot, G2 and Capterra, then reads its rating and reviews every day. (14:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: A partner portal's link can now be readable, such as `/p/northpeak/`, instead of the program's id. Program settings → Portal suggests an address from the program's name; links with the id keep working. The Portal tab can also put the portal on your own domain, such as partners.example.com, with one DNS record. (14:22 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Presence's Found list marks sites whose links to you look like spam (Possible spam, Likely spam), using your SEO tool's own spam judgement such as Ahrefs' and Outflare's link checks, with the reasons on hover. Nothing is hidden until you choose Spam site. (14:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Approvals, agent runs and API logs each have brand routes under `/v1/brands/{brand}/` that show only that brand's items. The workspace routes still work for now; next they will keep only the team's own items, such as invites and connections. (14:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: New SEO partnership prospects get the site's domain rating filled in from what your SEO tool already read (backlinks and competitor scans), so you can see who's worth pitching first. (15:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: The AI receipts forwarding steps now cover receipts from claude.ai and chatgpt.com, drop GitHub, and say that receipts sent from a stripe.com address need the Gmail filter. (16:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Approvals, agent runs and API logs now show only the picked brand's on the Agents and Logs pages. The team's own, about no brand (such as invites, members and connections), are on a new Settings page, Team activity, and on the workspace routes, which no longer show a brand's. (16:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Program settings → General has a "Review each application" switch, so a program can approve each new partner by hand or accept everyone who applies. Every new program, referral programs included, reviews each application by default. (16:24 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Agents and missions: A web search an agent asks for now belongs to the brand it searches for: `POST /v1/brands/{brand}/search-results` (MCP `search_web` takes `brand`) replaces the workspace route, so the approval waits under that brand alone on the Agents page, and its cost and event carry the brand. A change that names no brand says "Every brand" on its card when a brand is picked. (08:51 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Agents and missions: The Agent page opens again. Since the last update it showed "Something broke on this page" as soon as the agent loaded. (11:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: After an update, an open tab or a page your browser kept from before no longer shows a blank screen. The app and the partner portal reload once and open the new version. (11:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Outcomes counts only outreach costs. Ad spend, searches, rank checks, SEO reports and site crawls stay out of cost per meeting. Searches and SEO reports now count toward the brand they ran for. The list of charges shows only the brand you're looking at. Costs that belong to no single sequence are no longer described as shared out when no emails went out. (12:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Reviews: SourceForge reviews are now kept with their date, title and text, not only the listing's score and count. (15:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: If Gmail's forwarding confirmation reaches your receipts address before you link the Gmail address in Outflare, linking it now shows the Confirm in Gmail button. You no longer have to ask Gmail to send it again. (16:07 UTC)
