# Outflare 2026.11.04

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-04 17:28 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-04

## New

- **Open source** · SEO: A GitHub Action for sites kept in a repository: it opens a pull request for each page approved in Outflare and reports the page live once merged, from your own repository and token. (07:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Visitors now lists everyone on your site, anonymous visitors included, and opens each one's journey: visits, pages and events. A person's timeline shows when and how they went from anonymous to known, visitor links join a phone where they never sign in, and Website tracking walks you through serving the script from your own domain and checks it works. (11:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Website analytics now shows what Outflare's own tracking tag counted: visitors, visits, page views, bounce rate and visit length by day, with top pages, sources, websites, countries and devices. It works with no analytics tool connected, and sits next to GA4 or PostHog when you have one. (13:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads opens at three levels: every network, one network, or one ad account, picked from a trail at the top of the page, and every tab narrows to it. A new **Changes** tab lists every pause, resume and budget change at that level and whether the platform shows it. (13:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads suggests changes to your campaigns: raise or lower a daily budget, pause a campaign that spends without converting, or resume one that did well, each with why. Nothing changes until you approve it; approved changes are measured long enough to judge and marked Worked, Didn't work or Not sure. (13:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: On Google search campaigns, Paid ads suggests keywords to add (searches that convert), negative keywords (searches that cost money and never convert), and keywords worth an ad group of their own, and shows Google Ads' own recommendations for an account. Each waits for your approval and is measured like other suggestions. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels: follow people from an ad click or website visit through product events and conversions to a first payment, with a time limit on any step, and see where they drop off, broken down by campaign or source, over time, and the people behind every step. Save funnels for the team, and agents can run them too. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads has a Tests tab: try a new target cost per conversion on a Google Ads campaign, or a new daily budget on a Meta campaign, on part of its traffic, see which side did better, then keep it or roll it back. (15:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Change how a Google Ads campaign bids, and add or remove audiences and countries on Google Ads and Meta campaigns, from the campaign's page. Paid ads also suggests a target cost per conversion for Google campaigns with enough conversions. (15:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Share a dashboard by link, with a password if you like: anyone with it sees the dashboard read-only and live, and you can take the link back any time. Set an alert on a saved trend (above or below a value, or up or down by a percentage) and hear once when it crosses, like every other alert. Put a dashboard's trends in the weekly digest. (16:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Funnels now take exclusion steps and run by company, save a step's people or drop-off as a list, and show what converters did differently. A new Paths insight draws where people went step by step, and annotations mark a day on every chart. (16:54 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The brand agent can write new ads, ad groups and Google search campaigns as drafts. You approve one under Paid ads, Suggestions, it is made on Google Ads or Meta paused so you can check it, and you turn it on. Nothing spends until you do. (17:28 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Paid ads: Send a conversion such as a sign-up from both the website and your server with the same event id, and Outflare sends it to ad platforms once. The website script's `outflare.track()` now takes your own id as `messageId`. (09:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads: Fix tracking links shows its progress while it runs, then how many URL suffixes it changed and each one Google refused with its reason, or why it failed. Also by API and with the `get_ad_tracking_run` agent tool. (11:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: A conversion sent only from your server, for someone your website never saw, now takes its country from the event itself (`context.location.country` or `context.ip`), so it is no longer skipped as "country unknown". (11:58 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions sent from both the website and your server count once even when the two copies don't share a `messageId`: they are matched on the event name, the person and the time (within 15 minutes), and the server's copy is sent. Paid ads → Tracking health warns when this happens, with the one-line fix. (14:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Self-hosting: Your own server's events can be kept under one brand of your events workspace, the one whose website tag is on your site, so each person who uses Outflare joins the visits they made before signing in, even if they signed in long ago. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Outflare tracking on Website analytics now shows which website each page is on, with a picker to see one website (such as your app's subdomain) on its own. Visitors opens on Everyone, so anonymous visitors show first. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Each brand has its own event catalog: two brands in one workspace can send events with the same name, such as `order_completed`, and each brand approves and defines its own. Events from a second brand are no longer held because another brand uses the name. (15:35 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · Social: Find links waits long enough for a brand website behind a bot check: the second read runs as a Geonode job for up to a few minutes, and a read that still doesn't get through is shown as blocked or too slow instead of a Geonode outage. (08:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Browser tasks that can't start the browser now stop within their time limit with a clear error, instead of waiting for good and holding a browser worker. (08:27 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: A mailbox whose mail server keeps refusing its sign-in now asks you to connect it again after 5 failed syncs in a row, instead of failing quietly every two minutes. A mailbox that keeps failing is tried less often, and a server that is only busy no longer counts against it. (08:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Deliverability: Opt-out syncing with EmailBison, Instantly and Smartlead keeps going when reading their blocklist fails: your opt-outs still go out, their list is read every 6 hours instead of every few minutes, and a failed try waits 30 minutes. Website tracking no longer answers with an error when a company lookup for a visitor fails. (08:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: When the app can't reach Outflare (offline, a dropped connection), pages now say so instead of loading forever, and the SEO overview keeps checking back on a first SEO read. A brand's link search that fails because the page reader is out of credits or down now says which. (08:55 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: A review page the page reader hasn't finished reading after an hour now shows as a slow page and is tried again later the same day, instead of being sent again every hour. When X, LinkedIn or the page reader is down, each account tries again on its own schedule instead of the whole read repeating several times over. (09:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: The notice that some connections or accounts have no brand shows once at the top of the page again. Moving between pages could add another copy each time, pushing the page down. (09:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Journeys, rules and lifecycle stages react to an event only when the event's own brand has approved its name. Approving a name in one brand no longer lets it drive automations in another. (14:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Reviews: a review page Geonode can't read (the site fails, or puts it behind a login) now shows why on the page and is tried again later the same day, instead of being retried every hour as a Geonode outage or left for a day as a wrong address. (15:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: One-click DNS approval: a DNS host that fails to answer once is asked again, and a domain whose DNS host doesn't support one-click approval now says so instead of showing a lookup error. (15:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Inbox sync handles bursts of new mail on slow mail servers: it reads new messages a few at a time and keeps what it has read, so replies and bounces no longer arrive late when one sync runs out of time. (15:09 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Tolt imports no longer stop over one object Outflare can't store, or over a Tolt page that fails: the object is listed under issues and the rest come over, a failing page is asked for again in smaller pieces, and the import waits longer between attempts while Tolt is down. (15:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Connected AI apps no longer get "the MCP server is starting" errors during updates and restarts, and leads or unsubscribes that weren't linked to their person records because of an update are now linked. (15:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Self-hosting: The database no longer grows with every tracked website visit: finished background work that runs once per batch of events is now cleaned up after 14 days like other finished work, and the events workspace keeps successful API and MCP calls once an hour per person instead of every one. (15:17 UTC)
