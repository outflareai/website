# Outflare 2026.11.24

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-24 23:22 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-24

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales AI fields: write a field such as {ai_use_case} with a prompt and a research source (what you already know about the person, or their company's homepage). Each person gets one sentence from your own AI model, shown with the passage it rests on and its cost, editable before it sends. A sentence the research doesn't support is never invented. Reps have a daily limit you set. (02:48 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Add an activity: record a call, a meeting or a chat on Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord or LinkedIn on a person, with when it happened and a comment. It shows on their timeline and in their Sales conversation, counts as contact (so a lead qualified on a call can be introduced to an account manager by a new email), and can stop a running sequence. (02:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Add to pipeline: put a site or company on any pipeline in one click from an opportunity, a found site, a competitor's page or a CRM record. Opportunities and CRM pages now link to the pipeline cards a company has and show where else its site appears. (11:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partnerships is now a partner CRM. It opens on all your partners, a dropdown picks a pipeline and shows its board, and you can rename a pipeline and add, remove, rename and reorder its stages. A partner opens on its own page with its stage, what needs attention, activity, emails, chats, tasks, notes and people. Old Presence links still work. (12:30 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Teams can carry access: give a team a role on every brand or on single brands under Settings, Members, Teams, and everyone in the team may do what it allows, on top of their own role. Taking someone out of the team takes that access away straight away. (14:46 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A Sales conversation can have more than one person: when a colleague joins the thread, a rep can add them (Outflare suggests whoever wrote in it). Their mail with the rep stays in the conversation, labelled with who wrote it, and a reply can go to any of its people, each checked against their own opt-outs. (15:18 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Conversion view: organic landing pages compared with the rest of their template, with the sign-ups and revenue each would add at the template's median, and its page speed, scroll depth, clicks, heatmap and session recordings beside it, so you can fix the pages that already get traffic but don't convert. (15:38 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales emails can now be formatted: bold, italics, bulleted and numbered lists, and a link on a word, in the reply box, the first email, templates and sequence steps. The person gets it formatted, with a plain-text version for mail apps that show text only. Emails nobody formats are sent exactly as before. (16:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Owners can close a whole module, such as Support or Finances, to everyone but owners, or open it to chosen roles and teams to view or edit, under Settings, Members, Module access. A closed module is gone for everyone else, and for the agents and API keys they made. (18:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: New goals, projects and tasks open with a "Describe it" box: type it in a sentence and your own AI model fills in the form for you to check. Every date now follows one format, set for the company under Settings, General, with a personal choice under Your account. (19:39 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has an AI crawlers view: which AI crawlers each site's robots.txt lets in, any newly blocked since the last crawl, llms.txt and content-use signals, and your key pages with what keeps each from AI answers, such as a blocked AI search crawler or content shown only with JavaScript. (21:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has a Google updates view: each Google ranking update of the last year against your search clicks before and after, by page template, with the pages that won and lost and the domains that moved in your tracked top 10, plus the weeks clicks fell, marked as during an update or not, so you can tell Google's change from your own. (21:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: SEO overview has an Indexing view: every page Outflare knows grouped by template and language, how many are in a sitemap, can be indexed and are indexed, why the rest aren't in Google's own words, which pages fell out of the index this week, and the 30-day trend. (21:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Recruitment: keep the jobs you hire for, each with its own questions and pipeline, take applications in from your careers page or hiring agent over the API, and move candidates through stages with a full history. Access is per job, and candidates only ever see the status wording you choose. (23:22 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales AI fields: a sentence may name a few known products written like a domain (such as Node.js), and one refused for holding a link now says why. Answers that don't confirm what they found are never used as a sentence, a company site that couldn't be read is tried again sooner, and a failing page reader says so instead of reporting the site as unreadable. (09:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Several email addresses per person in Sales: see and add a person's other addresses in the conversation, choose the main one, and merge the person another address belongs to after seeing their conversations, account and payments; undo it if wrong. Mail from any of their own addresses lands in the conversation, and a shared inbox such as support@ never joins two people. (10:07 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: A Sales conversation now has the person beside it: where they stand, their details, and their history and their company's, so you can qualify and write without leaving. Timelines read better everywhere: page views close together are one website visit that opens to its pages, payments and sign-ups stand out, and days are headed. (10:40 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Rows in lists and tables are real links now: right-click "Open in new tab", a middle click or Ctrl/Cmd-click opens the item, task, page or person in a new tab, and a plain click still opens it in place. (11:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: The conversion send log now shows what Google did with each conversion after accepting it: one Google refused while processing turns failed, with Google's reason, instead of staying sent. A failed conversion can be sent again once its cause is fixed, and Google still counts it once. (14:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: Always approve: when you approve an agent's change, tick "Always approve this from now on" and changes of that kind run on their own from then on, for every item, not just the one you approved. Approving several at once lets you pick per kind, and connected AI apps now show in the rules. (14:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: The Mentions table has an Added column showing when each mention was found, next to when it was said, and lists the newest finds first. (16:11 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: When your knowledge has no answer to a customer's question, the support assistant now also looks in the docs, issues and tasks of your connected Linear, Notion and ClickUp, read only, and suggests what it finds in Knowledge for you to approve. Switch it off per brand in Support settings. (18:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: A partner's page now lets you add, change or remove its company. Companies get their logo from their own website, shown on partners, pipeline boards and deal boards. Owner dropdowns show each person's picture, every card and link opens in a new tab with Ctrl or Cmd, and Ask AI opens the agent's chat beside the partner where your server runs an agent. (18:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · Connectors: You can now save an MCP server connection before its server is switched on, one key at a time: it shows as not connected, with the HTTP status as the reason, until the server answers. Its address is sealed like a key. (21:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales conversations count as seen when they were really on your screen or you opened them, with nothing to click: New labels and unread numbers clear for you alone. Each conversation says who saw it and when they opened it, and Team activity counts seen, opened and copied. (22:58 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: A Sales conversation a teammate passes you is listed at the top of your lists until you open it, with an email if you haven't within a few minutes. Assignment counts now match the filtered list, Seen shows at once, and every Sales rep can see Team activity. (02:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Changing a logged activity's channel now renames its title too, unless someone wrote their own title. (09:45 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Pipeline items that failed or were skipped can be put back in the step they stopped in and tried again, also on a run that had finished. Before, an item that gave up while a provider was down could not be run again. (13:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Adding someone to a Sales conversation no longer lets a reply reach them when they are on your blocklist, and everyone copied on a reply passes the full opt-out check. Someone taken off a conversation no longer gets a copy that was already queued. (18:15 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: When a rep emails someone added to a Sales conversation in a separate thread, replies and sequence emails in the conversation keep going to the conversation's own person. (19:10 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: In a Sales conversation with people added to it, emails its own person isn't on no longer decide who replies and sequence emails go to or what they answer, including in conversations already affected. AI drafts are told who sent each message and leave those emails out. (21:17 UTC)
