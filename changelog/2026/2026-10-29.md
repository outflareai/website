## New

- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: Journeys now open in a visual flow builder: the trigger at the top, steps running down, checks splitting side by side, live numbers on every step, and problems marked before you go live. Live journeys change through a draft you publish, and people already in one can finish on their version. A new Rules page reads each rule as a sentence, dry-runs it, and starts a journey from it. - **Open source** · Outreach: Journalists: the news watch now reads each new article's byline, finds the author's address at the outlet with the free email finder, and keeps them as a person record with what they wrote. Make a media list of the journalists who cover a topic as a smart list that keeps itself up to date. (07:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Pitch link prospects from Outflare: your own AI writes an opener for each page, you approve it, and the contact goes into one of your sequences. Replies are read for you, agreed links are checked until they're live, and results show live links per pitch. (07:42 UTC)
