# Outflare 2026.10.30

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-10-30 08:06 UTC.

## New

- **Open source** · Analytics: Exclusion groups for feature flags: flags in one group split its visitors, so no visitor is in two of their A/B tests at once. The A/B test checklist warns when another test runs at the same time outside the group, and a new interactions check shows whether two overlapping tests changed each other's results. (07:57 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Partners: Partners: reward rules are now flows, as in Tolt. Each has AND/OR conditions on the partner, the customer and the transaction, its own approval (automatic, after N days or manual), whether it adds to other cash flows or competes with them, other rates for some partners or plans, and can move the partner to another group. A Flows tab edits them. Existing rules keep working unchanged. (08:06 UTC)
