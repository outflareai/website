## New

- **Open source** · Analytics: Exclusion groups for feature flags: flags in one group split its visitors, so no visitor is in two of their A/B tests at once. The A/B test checklist warns when another test runs at the same time outside the group, and a new interactions check shows whether two overlapping tests changed each other's results. (07:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Tracking health on the Paid ads page shows, for your workspace, how each network's paid clicks arrive (with their click id, only the template's ad ids, or untracked), which campaigns spend without a tracked visit, and which platform is refusing the conversions you send back, each with what to fix. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Tracking templates: paste one into Google, Meta, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Reddit, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Taboola or Outbrain (Paid ads → Tracking templates) so each paid click lands with its campaign, ad set and ad ids. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Alerts: a hot reply or a hot website visitor raises an alert under Settings → Alerts, sent as the alert.raised webhook, counted in the digest and emailed to the person it is for or to owners and admins. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Your own attribution models: start from any of the five and give each channel its own window (paid ads 7 days, say), leave channels out, or set the position weights and time-decay half-life. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Define your own conversions: any event, filtered on its properties (a call over a minute, a Pro plan payment), with a fixed value or one from the event, and each ad network's name for it. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Exclusions: journeys, broadcasts and sequences can leave out people on chosen lists, with chosen tags, or at a tagged company, such as "Competitors" or "Active deal: hands off". (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Feedback and What's new: the Feedback button in the top bar turns a bug, idea or question into a GitHub issue or discussion under your own account, and a post on X, with links to good first issues for anyone who wants to build it. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Fix tickets: every problem a site audit finds becomes one ticket with all the pages it affects. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Change a page for one version of a split without code: Edit visually opens a page of your site with that version and an editor over it, where you click anything to change its text, hide it, or change a link or an image. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Playbooks can now set up a whole automation for you: its email drafts, lists, rules and journeys, all as drafts you check and turn on. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: HubSpot reply push: pick reply labels on your HubSpot connection, such as hot, and each matching reply adds the contact with a note quoting the reply and a link back, plus, if you choose, a follow-up task for the lead's owner. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Journeys can now act, not just email: update a field, add or remove a tag, put people in or out of a static list, start or leave another journey, add them to a sequence or stop their whole company's sequences, suppress or unsubscribe them, and alert their owner. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Journeys can ask your own AI model about a person: sort them into choices such as hot, warm or cold, or write a short note. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Journeys can create or update people in your HubSpot. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Reviews, a page of its own: your brands' Trustpilot, G2, Capterra and Google reviews, read every day with your own Geonode, a review score that weights stars by how many and how recent, its direction (growing, steady, slipping), and Draft answer with your own AI model for a person to post. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Slack connector: connect your own Slack and post each kind of alert, such as hot replies, to a channel you pick under Settings → Alerts. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Snapchat Ads: sign in with Snapchat to read each ad account's campaigns and daily spend, impressions, swipes and purchases, and send your conversions back to a Snap Pixel through the Conversions API. (08:17 UTC)

Only their hashed email and phone number reach the platform, and adding follows your ad consent setting. (09:19 UTC)

You approve each automation once, with spend limits per run and per month; if a limit is reached or a connection goes missing, it stops and tells you why. (13:09 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Partners: Partners: reward rules are now flows, as in Tolt. Each has AND/OR conditions on the partner, the customer and the transaction, its own approval (automatic, after N days or manual), whether it adds to other cash flows or competes with them, other rates for some partners or plans, and can move the partner to another group. A Flows tab edits them. Existing rules keep working unchanged. (08:06 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partners: New flow starts from a template: one-time, recurring, first 12 months, "10% of the first sale, then 2% for 12 months", or a fixed amount per sign-up. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: A/B tests: a checklist before you start says what would stop a test measuring anything, such as pages that don't show the flag yet, and one click ships the winner to everyone and ends the test. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: A/B tests are registered when they start: the hypothesis, goals, plan and the flag's versions and shares are kept as they were, and the hypothesis can no longer change. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Make your own attribution models and define your conversions in the web app, under Analytics → Channels: windows per channel, channels left out and weights for a model, and for a conversion its event, conditions, value and each ad network's name for it, or start from a preset. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Site audits have full crawl settings: how deep to go, whether to read sitemaps, follow nofollow links or ignore URL parameters, which pages to include or leave out, whether to run pages' scripts (through your own Geonode connection, on a proved site), and the hour, day and time zone a scheduled crawl starts. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Removes the database tables left over from agency accounts, which were removed in v0.26.0. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Get started and Home now offer "Follow the builder": follow the person building Outflare in public on X. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Journeys can send a transactional email, record a person's consent to a topic, assign an owner (one member, or spread evenly across several), and open or move their deal to a stage. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: CRM → Lead finder is rebuilt for big runs: a front page with what needs you and each search's results, and runs whose companies and people load a page at a time with filters and bulk actions. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Meetings are now events on each person's timeline: meeting.booked, meeting.held, meeting.no_show and meeting.canceled, from your calendars, CRM, the app or the API. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Partners: partners who haven't given payout details are easy to find ("No payout details" on the Partners tab, and a payout_details filter in the API), and the portal asks them to add theirs. (08:17 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Site audits start from your selected brand: its website is filled in, and Outflare first looks for a proof you already have (Search Console owning the site, or your Outflare tracking tag on it), so most sites are proved with nothing to add and get the larger crawl limits. (08:17 UTC)

- **Open source** · Analytics: Redirect links: the website tracking snippet ties each click on a redirect link to the visit it started, and every link and campaign shows how many of its clicks reached your site. (08:23 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: The permissions for ideal customer profiles and moving records between brands are now called `profiles.read` and `profiles.manage`, and the API reference lists those endpoints under "profiles" instead of "projects". The old names `projects.read` and `projects.manage` still work: tokens, roles and apps that hold them keep their access, and requests may still send them. (09:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Support inbox: forward your support@ address to your brand's support address (such as northpeak-support@) instead of connecting the mailbox. Each email becomes a conversation, and replies go out through your email provider from the address the customer wrote to. On Outflare Cloud. (09:03 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: The last numbers from Outflare's old built-in warm-up are gone: `GET /mailboxes/{id}/warmup`, the 7-day warm-up counts and inbox rates on mailboxes, domains, stats, reports, mailbox health and the infrastructure overview, and the ignored `daily_target` and `reply_rate` settings. Warm-up runs in a connected sequencer, and its score is on each mailbox's `warmup.platform`. (09:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: The API drops everything left from projects and agency accounts: `project_id` fields, the `project.*` webhook events, the old scope names `projects.read` and `projects.manage` (use `profiles.read` and `profiles.manage`) and the whoami token `kind`. Inbox filters use `tag` (not `label`), and report rows use `name`. (11:00 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partners: while Tolt still pays a program you imported, its imported commissions keep Tolt's status and never go into a payout here, so nobody is paid twice. Switch over on the Payouts tab when Tolt stops paying. The import summary now shows how many it read, and partners by status. (11:37 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Partners: the Tolt import also brings Tolt's groups (with each partner in theirs, and the default group) and flows (rates, duration, conditions, approval and custom rates). A flow with something Outflare can't map yet isn't imported and is listed for you to add on the Flows tab. (12:39 UTC)
- **Open source** · Lifecycle email: The automation map can now show what reaches one person: pick someone, and it marks the events they did, the lists they're in and the journeys they went through, and lights up what those set off next. You can also narrow it to everything that leads to one sequence, list or tag. (12:51 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Each partner group has its own sign-up link: partners who apply through it join that group. (13:05 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Every workspace now starts with a brand, named after the workspace, and workspaces without one got one. Brands are becoming the owner of all their data, so two brands in one workspace never mix: their own API calls live under `/v1/brands/{brand}/`, starting with reading the brand itself. (15:24 UTC)
- **Open source** · Customer records: Brands keep their customer data apart: every person, company and record, with their details, history and consent, and the fields and types you add to the catalog, now belong to one brand. People with no brand go to your first brand, and duplicates are only found and merged within a brand. (15:47 UTC)
