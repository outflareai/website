# Outflare 2026.10.28

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-10-28 11:07 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-10-28

## New

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Every DNS record a brand needs, in one list: the site's proof, its email domain's records and its tracking domain. Add every missing one with one button through your own Cloudflare, or approve them at your DNS provider one group after another. (07:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Get started's star and follow step asks, optionally, how you heard about Outflare: one tap from a short list. (07:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: The Connectors page shows who built a connector that Outflare doesn't look after: a **Verified** badge for a verified publisher, **Community** for everyone else, with the publisher's name when you connect it (integrations/README.md). (08:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Email finder: under CRM → Email finder, propose a likely work email for someone at a company and check whether an address is good to send to. It learns the shape each company uses from your own contacts, so finding is free; checking an address uses your own connected verifier only when it has to. Also available over the API and to agents (`email_pattern`, `find_emails`, `verify_addresses`). (08:57 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: Five more partner playbooks on the Creator partnerships board: find creators who already cover your competitors, sponsor newsletters and podcasts that pay back, seed your product to creators, renew the creator deals that pay back, and recruit affiliates and creators from your own sign-ups. (09:10 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Get started now sets up one brand from start to finish: the brand, your AI key (with why Outflare needs it), the website script and every DNS record in one step, the tools for your goal, star and follow, then a first mission. Home shows one set-up checklist at a time. (07:47 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Added your DNS records by hand? Press "I've added them, check now" in brand setup or Get started, and Outflare looks for every record straight away and shows which are in place and which are still missing. (09:10 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Importing from Attio now shows its first screen within about half a minute on large workspaces, or a clear error if Attio doesn't answer, instead of loading forever. (08:57 UTC)
