# Outflare 2026.11.25

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-25 17:46 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-25

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Bring a WhatsApp or Telegram conversation into a person's history: paste the messages, add the exported file or upload screenshots, check and correct every message, and save it. Exports are read for free; screenshots are read by your own AI, with the cost shown first. (01:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales managers get a Team report: for any period, how many leads came in and from where, who saw, opened, was assigned, messaged or passed on what, and which leads nobody has touched, with the list behind every number. It replaces Team activity for managers and counts assignments for who received them. (02:12 UTC)
- **Cloud** · Workspace and settings: Owners can move a brand to a new company or to another company they own, with all its data and history. The page shows what moves, what stays and anything to sort out first. (13:36 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Outreach: Discovery shows two more channels a company can be reached on, QQ and WeChat, in a lane's companies and on the Channels page; a QQ number or a WeChat id with no link is shown as text. (00:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: What support hands to sales is now marked New until you have seen or opened it, beside New conversations in Sales and with a dot in Support and the Inbox. Each row says who on the team saw it and when they opened it. (02:42 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Paid ads, Payback: the Day to day chart shows what each day's ad spend brought back as a percent of that day's spend, with a line at 100% for a day that paid for itself, or as amounts over bars of spend. Hover a day to see every number for it. A line with no numbers yet says so. (11:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Each role now opens on its own page, with a level per area and the single permissions inside it. Adding people to teams (`teams.members`), editing teams (`teams.manage`) and resetting someone's two-factor (`members.two_factor`) are permissions of their own; roles and API tokens that could do these before keep them. (11:08 UTC)
- **Open source** · Outreach: Sales reporting's Discovery view has a Pace section on Outflare Cloud: the new companies that arrived in a profile's flow per 15 minutes over the last 3, 6 or 24 hours, how many fit, and when the last one arrived. (11:26 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: Recruitment now has its screens, under People: a list of your jobs, each job's applicants as a table or a board you can drag between stages, filters and bulk moves, a side-by-side comparison, and an application page you can work through with single keys. Job setup holds the hiring brief, the questions, the stages and who takes part. (11:44 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: The Sales Team report counts what each rep saw and opened among the conversations support handed to sales, its lists load faster, and a day starts at the right moment where the clocks change at midnight. (12:31 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: each brand now sets its own limits for which catalog sites are worth a link (lowest rating and traffic, how much traffic may sit on one page, how much a site links out), under Settings. A site with a number missing is marked Needs data instead of OK, and every reason has a code to filter on. (13:36 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: Replies read from your sequencer now reach Sales when a colleague answers from another address or the reply refers you to someone else, with the people to add suggested in the conversation, and the reply rule for referrals can add the person named to a sequencer campaign you choose. (15:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · SEO: Link orders: import an export of the sites that link to a competitor, with the competitor's domain beside the file. Each site is marked as found through a competitor and remembers which competitors it links to, and the catalog can list the sites that link to one of them. (15:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Analytics: Browsers that report the same campaign link click soon after it, such as a lander that opens its offer in a new tab, now count as one visitor. Once either is known as a person, funnels show the lander steps on their journey instead of skipping them. (15:29 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: You can change a partner's owner straight from the partners list: click the Owner cell to pick someone from the team, or pick Nobody to remove them. (15:39 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions can find YouTube videos that say your brand's or competitors' names out loud: connect your own Filmot API key, and with YouTube on each scan searches what videos say, with the channel, views and the second each name is said. Filmot alone is enough for YouTube. (16:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Social: Mentions: keep only the languages you pick (English only, for example). A podcast mention's page shows the show, the episode's notes, a player at the moment it was said and links to Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube. Competitor-scan YouTube videos show only while YouTube is on, so own channels are left out. (16:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Every dropdown now opens with a search field: type a few letters to find the option, filter, action or column you want instead of scrolling. (16:34 UTC)
- **Open source** · Inbox: The reply rule for referrals can now be limited to the campaigns you choose: with `from_sequence_ids`, only a referral from someone in one of those sequences adds the person named, and a referral from any other campaign is just suggested in the conversation. A rule that names a sequencer campaign for referrals no longer needs an Outflare sequence as well. (16:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Everything else: Dates are now written with the month as a word, 25/Nov/2026, across the app, and every date box takes them that way (25/Nov/2026, 25 nov 26 or 25/11/2026, or a pick from the calendar). People who chose month/day/year or year-month-day keep it. (17:14 UTC)
- **Open source** · Workspace and settings: The app now calls a workspace a company: settings, menus, members, brands and messages say "company". Nothing else changes, and the API still names it a workspace. (17:14 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales AI fields in My sequences: a company site typed on a form, or a second company address, is read; a site that can't be read uses the fallback and is tried again after a day; you can write your own when one fails. Models that think first have room: up to about 12 US cents a sentence and $12 per rep per day at a limit of 100 on Claude Opus 5.5 (calculated, not measured). (00:35 UTC)
- **Open source** · Paid ads: Conversions now go to your ad platforms within minutes even while a large import of past events is running. Past events sent late also no longer queue lifecycle and metric work they can't change, so imports finish faster. (10:13 UTC)
- **Open source** · Partners: The Owner column on the partners list shows the owner's name again. For a teammate who was in your staff list before they had a sign-in, it showed an id instead. (10:28 UTC)
- **Open source** · API, SDK, MCP and CLI: A POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE sent to an address outside the API (for example a base URL missing `/v1`) now fails with a 404 error that says API endpoints start with `/v1/`, instead of answering 200 with the web app's page as if the request had worked. (16:52 UTC)
- **Open source** · Agents and missions: When the workspace's AI provider refuses a call (a bad request, an account out of credit, an outage), the error now keeps the provider's own message, with the key removed, and carries the `ai_failed` code, so a failed task can say why instead of reporting an internal error. (17:14 UTC)
