# Outflare 2026.11.25

In progress: this day closes at midnight UTC. Last updated 2026-11-25 02:42 UTC.

On the web: https://outflare.ai/changelog/#2026-11-25

## New

- **Open source** · CRM: Bring a WhatsApp or Telegram conversation into a person's history: paste the messages, add the exported file or upload screenshots, check and correct every message, and save it. Exports are read for free; screenshots are read by your own AI, with the cost shown first. (01:41 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: Sales managers get a Team report: for any period, how many leads came in and from where, who saw, opened, was assigned, messaged or passed on what, and which leads nobody has touched, with the list behind every number. It replaces Team activity for managers and counts assignments for who received them. (02:12 UTC)

## Improved

- **Open source** · Outreach: Discovery shows two more channels a company can be reached on, QQ and WeChat, in a lane's companies and on the Channels page; a QQ number or a WeChat id with no link is shown as text. (00:20 UTC)
- **Open source** · CRM: What support hands to sales is now marked New until you have seen or opened it, beside New conversations in Sales and with a dot in Support and the Inbox. Each row says who on the team saw it and when they opened it. (02:42 UTC)

## Fixed

- **Open source** · CRM: Sales AI fields in My sequences: a company site typed on a form, or a second company address, is read; a site that can't be read uses the fallback and is tried again after a day; you can write your own when one fails. Models that think first have room: up to about 12 US cents a sentence and $12 per rep per day at a limit of 100 on Claude Opus 5.5 (calculated, not measured). (00:35 UTC)
